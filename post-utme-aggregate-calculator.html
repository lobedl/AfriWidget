Understanding Nigerian University Post-UTME Screening Formulas

Following the JAMB Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME), federal and state universities across Nigeria conduct mandatory screening exercises to derive a composite admission score out of 100%. Because individual universities exercise institutional autonomy over their screening modalities, multiple distinct mathematical models are used across the country.

1. The 50:30:20 Model (UNILAG, UI, OAU)

Adopted by the University of Lagos, University of Ibadan, and Obafemi Awolowo University.

  • **JAMB UTME (50%)** = JAMB Score ÷ 8
  • **Post-UTME CBT (30%)** = CBT Mark ÷ 100 × 30
  • **O'Level Points (20%)** = Sum of 5 core subjects:
  • A1 = 4.0 | B2 = 3.6 | B3 = 3.2 | C4 = 2.8 | C5 = 2.4 | C6 = 2.0
  • **Aggregate** = (JAMB ÷ 8) + (CBT ÷ 100 × 30) + O'Level Points

2. The 50:50 Model (UNIBEN, UNN, FUTO, DELSU)

Adopted by University of Benin, University of Nigeria Nsukka, and Federal University of Technology Owerri.

  • **JAMB UTME (50%)** = JAMB Score ÷ 8 (or JAMB ÷ 400 × 50)
  • **Post-UTME CBT (50%)** = Post-UTME Mark ÷ 2 (or Mark ÷ 100 × 50)
  • **Aggregate** = (JAMB Score ÷ 8) + (Post-UTME Score ÷ 2)

O'Level grades serve as a qualifying prerequisite (minimum 5 credits in 1 or 2 sittings) rather than scored points.

3. Point Grading Model (LASU, UNIOSUN)

Lagos State University and select state universities use point-based O'Level screening with no separate CBT examination.

  • **JAMB Part (50%)** = JAMB Score ÷ 8
  • **O'Level Part (50%)** = Sum of 5 subjects (A1=10, B2=9, B3=8, C4=7, C5=6, C6=5)
  • **Total Aggregate** = JAMB Points (50) + O'Level Points (50)

4. Standard 60:40 Screening Model

Used in various northern and eastern federal universities:

  • **JAMB Part (60%)** = JAMB Score ÷ 400 × 60
  • **Post-UTME Part (40%)** = Post-UTME Mark ÷ 100 × 40
  • **Total Aggregate** = JAMB (60%) + CBT (40%)

Complete Worked Screening Calculation Example

Candidate Profile: A student applying for Computer Engineering at UNILAG scores:

  • JAMB UTME Score: 280 / 400
  • Post-UTME CBT Exam Mark: 74 / 100
  • O'Level Grades (1 sitting): English (A1), Mathematics (A1), Physics (B2), Chemistry (B3), Further Maths (A1)

1. JAMB Points (50%) = 280 ÷ 8 = 35.00 points

2. Post-UTME Points (30%) = 74 ÷ 100 × 30 = 22.20 points

3. O'Level Points (20%) = 4.0 + 4.0 + 3.6 + 3.2 + 4.0 = 18.80 points

Total Composite Aggregate = 35.00 + 22.20 + 18.80 = 76.00% (High Merit Standing)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What happens if my aggregate is below the published Merit cutoff?

If your aggregate is slightly below the Merit cutoff, you may still qualify through your state's Catchment Area quota (which has slightly lower cutoff thresholds) or the Educationally Less Developed States (ELDS) quota. You can also apply for supplementary change of course to related programs with available capacity.

How can I check my admission status after Post-UTME screening?

Admission status is published directly on the JAMB Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) portal and your chosen university's undergraduate portal. Once offered admission, candidates must log in to JAMB CAPS to formally ACCEPT or REJECT the admission offer to receive their official JAMB Admission Letter.

Are WAEC and NECO results weighted equally in Post-UTME screening?

Yes. All Nigerian universities treat WAEC (WASSCE), NECO (SSCE), and NABTEB grades identically on the standard 9-point scale (A1=4.0/10, B2=3.6/9, B3=3.2/8, C4=2.8/7, C5=2.4/6, C6=2.0/5).

Can I participate in Post-UTME if I used Awaiting Result (AR)?

Yes, you can register for Post-UTME with Awaiting Result. However, you must upload your verified O'Level grades to the JAMB CAPS portal immediately upon release to avoid forfeiture of admission consideration during institutional screening.
