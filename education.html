About AfriWidget Education Tools

AfriWidget's education tools are designed to empower students, educators, and academic advisors with precise, browser-based calculations that support better decision-making. From cumulative GPA tracking to university admission aggregate planning, our tools eliminate uncertainty and help you understand exactly where you stand academically.

Every calculator is built using official grading scales and admission formulas published by national examination bodies including WAEC, JAMB, KNEC, KUCCPS, UNEB, ZIMSEC, DHET, and Cambridge International. We verify our mathematical models against published guidelines to ensure accuracy you can trust.

All tools run 100% client-side in your browser. No data is transmitted, stored, or shared. Your grades, admission scores, and academic plans remain private — because your educational journey is yours alone.
