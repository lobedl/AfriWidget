    About the Permanent Public Room

    The AfriWidget Public Room gives visitors a single always-open voice and text lounge where you can talk and message online without registering an account or downloading software. Unlike temporary group rooms that disappear when empty, the Public Lounge remains available 24 hours a day so anyone can drop in anytime.

    How to Join and Speak Without an Account

    Type any display name from 2 to 24 characters above and tap Join Room. Your browser immediately loads the live participant list and recent messages currently active in the lounge. Every newcomer enters in listen-only mode so you never start broadcasting audio unexpectedly.

    Last Updated: 26 November 2026

    Microphone Controls and Speaker Indicators

    • Listen first, speak when ready: Read live messages and hear speakers right away, or tap Enable Microphone whenever you want to join the voice conversation.
    • Instant mute toggle: Mute or unmute your microphone with a single tap, or disconnect your mic completely while staying in text chat.
    • Live speaker highlights: Active participant tiles highlight in yellow whenever someone speaks so group conversations are easy to follow on mobile and desktop.

    Privacy, Temporary Memory, and Lounge Rules

    Because the Public Room is open to everyone online, treat everything spoken or typed in this lounge as public. Avoid posting personal contact numbers, addresses, or account details. For private conversations with friends or teams, use Create & Join Rooms with Private access, or switch to Random Connect for 1-on-1 matches.

    Public Room FAQ

    Does the Public Room stay open even when nobody is inside?
    Yes. The Public Room is a permanent community lounge that remains available 24/7 even when the participant count is zero.
    Do I need an account or microphone to join the Public Room?
    No account or microphone is needed. Pick any display name from 2 to 24 characters to enter, read and send text messages, and listen to speakers. Turn your mic on only if you decide to speak.
    Can everyone see who is speaking in the Public Room?
    Yes. Live speaker highlights on participant tiles clearly indicate who currently has an active microphone and who is speaking.
    Are Public Room chat messages stored permanently?
    No. Recent room messages are kept only in temporary active memory while visitors are online and are never stored in a permanent database.
