How 1-on-1 Random Matching Works

Random Connect pairs two visitors who both want a live one-on-one conversation without filling out signup forms or creating profiles. Enter a temporary display name above and click Find Someone to join the matchmaking queue. An active geometric search indicator tracks your wait time while checking for another queued partner.

Visitors in the Public Room or inside private group rooms are never pulled into Random Connect matches. Only users explicitly waiting in the Random Connect queue are connected together.

Using the 1-on-1 Call Interface

  • Start Mic & Mute Controls: Calls start muted so you can hear clearly first. Tap Start Mic to speak, mute or unmute with one tap, or turn the mic back off anytime.
  • Private 1-on-1 Messages: Tap Messages on the call deck to open a live text panel visible only inside your call session, even if you prefer chatting without voice.
  • Next Match & End Call: Tap Next Match to leave the current call and immediately enter the queue for someone else, or tap End Call to return to the lobby.

Opt-In Queue Privacy and Automatic Session Removal

Each matched pair connects inside a temporary, unlisted 1-on-1 room that stays invisible to public room listings and carries search-engine non-indexing protections. As soon as both participants end the call, that session is erased from temporary memory automatically.

Safe 1-on-1 Voice and Text Conversation Tips

Always use a neutral display name rather than sharing private phone numbers, financial details, or personal social accounts. If a conversation becomes uncomfortable or off-topic, press End Call or Next Match immediately to disconnect cleanly.

Random Connect FAQ

Who gets matched in Random Connect?
Random Connect only pairs visitors who explicitly click Find Someone on this page. People browsing the Public Room or custom rooms are never placed into the random queue.
Are Random Connect calls and messages private?
Yes. Each match opens an isolated 1-on-1 voice and text session shared only between you and your partner. Sessions are excluded from public room directories and deleted when both participants leave.
Can I text chat during a Random Connect match without enabling my mic?
Yes. Every call opens with your microphone off by default. Tap Messages on the call screen to chat by text immediately, or press Start Mic when you want to speak.
Can I cancel waiting or skip to a different person?
Yes. Tap Cancel Search while waiting in the queue, or tap Next Match or End Call on the call interface at any moment.
