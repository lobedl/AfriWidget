Frequently Asked Before Contacting

  • → Is AfriWidget free? Yes, completely and permanently free for all users.
  • → Do you store my data? Most calculations happen in your browser. Finance and Education AI insights may send limited calculation context through the secure Groq proxy, but AfriWidget has no user accounts or calculator-submission database.
  • → Can I embed your calculators? Reach out via the partnership inquiry option above.
  • → Are the formulas accurate? We use WHO and peer-reviewed standards. See each calculator's explainer.

What makes a useful report?

For a bug report, include the name of the tool, the values you entered, the result you expected, and what appeared instead. If the problem is on a particular phone or browser, include that detail too. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, medical records, or other private information in a message. A short, reproducible example usually helps more than a long description without the input details.

For an accuracy concern, tell us which formula, threshold, institution, or source you believe should be checked. Admissions rules and health guidance can change, so include the country, programme, or date that gives your concern context. We review the report against the page’s stated method and, where appropriate, add a clearer limitation or source note.

If you are suggesting a new tool, describe the problem it would solve, who would use it, and what inputs or output would make it genuinely helpful. We prioritise requests that improve access, clarity, privacy, or usefulness on mobile devices.

Before you send a message

Most calculators are designed to work without an account and many calculations happen entirely in the browser. If a tool is not behaving as expected, first refresh the page, check that required fields are complete, and make sure numbers use the units shown beside each input. For a shared result, send the tool URL and a description of the state rather than copying sensitive values into email.

The contact form confirms a submission in this demo interface; it does not promise that a message has been transmitted to a mail server. For time-sensitive medical, financial, legal, or admissions decisions, contact the relevant qualified professional or official institution directly. AfriWidget feedback helps improve the site, but it is not an emergency or professional advice service.

You can also use the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, and AdSense Disclaimer links in the footer to understand how the site is intended to operate. Those pages explain the limits of browser calculations, optional analytics, and the difference between a planning estimate and professional advice.

How we handle feedback

We use feedback to improve clarity, accessibility, formulas, and navigation across the site. A report may lead to a corrected label, a new explanation, a refreshed source note, or a change to how a result is presented. We do not promise that every suggestion will become a feature, but each useful report helps us understand how people use AfriWidget in real situations.

If your question concerns a school requirement, medical decision, financial commitment, or legal obligation, include the official source you are comparing with where possible. AfriWidget can investigate the page’s calculation and wording, while the responsible institution or qualified professional remains the authority for the final decision.
