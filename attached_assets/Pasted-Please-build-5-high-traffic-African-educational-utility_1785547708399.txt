### 2. KCSE ELIGIBILITY TIER CHECKER (KENYA)
LOGIC:
- Input: User selects their KCSE Mean Grade (A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, D+, D, D-, E).
- Logic & Output:
  * Grade C+ to A: High Chance for Public/Private University Degree Placement (KUCCPS).
  * Grade C to C-: Qualified for Diploma Programs in TVET Colleges.
  * Grade D to D+: Qualified for Craft Certificate Programs.
  * Grade E to D-: Qualified for Artisan Certificate Programs.

### 3. SOUTH AFRICAN TVET NATED PASS CALCULATOR
LOGIC:
- Input: Internal ICASS Mark % (weighted at 40%) + Final Exam Mark % (weighted at 60%).
- Formula: Final Mark = (ICASS * 0.40) + (Exam * 0.60).
- Pass Criteria: Total Mark must be ≥ 40% AND Exam Mark must be ≥ 40% (sub-minimum requirement).

  * Output: Displays Placement Probability Card with suggested alternative backup school tiers.

### 5. IGCSE / A-LEVEL TO US GPA CONVERTER
LOGIC:
- Input: Dynamic row inputs for Subject Name, Level (A-Level, AS-Level, IGCSE), and Grade (A*, A, B, C, D, E).
- Conversion Scale: A* = 4.0, A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, E = 0.0 (A-Levels add +0.5 weight for AP equivalent).
- Formula: GPA = (Sum of Weighted Grade Points) / (Total Subjects Count).
