Please build 5 lightweight, 100% client-side health tools to expand our web utility platform:

---
### 1. PEDIATRIC PARACETAMOL & IBUPROFEN DOSAGE CALCULATOR
- INPUTS: Child weight (kg or lbs) + Medicine Type (Paracetamol 120mg/5mL, Paracetamol 250mg/5mL, or Ibuprofen 100mg/5mL).
- LOGIC: Paracetamol standard dosage = 15mg per kg per dose. Ibuprofen standard dosage = 10mg per kg per dose.
- OUTPUT: Exact single dose in mL, recommended frequency (every 4-6 hours), and max daily dose limit in mL.
- SAFETY DISCLAIMER: Prominent warning block advising parents to verify with a doctor or pharmacist.

---
### 2. OVULATION & SAFE PERIOD CYCLE CALCULATOR
- INPUTS: Start date of last period, average cycle length (21-35 days, default 28), period duration (days).
- LOGIC: Estimated Ovulation Day = (Last Period Date + Cycle Length - 14 days). Fertile Window = 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after.
- OUTPUT: Color-coded result cards highlighting Next Period Date, Fertile Window, Ovulation Peak, and Lower-Risk Days.

---
### 3. DAILY WATER INTAKE & HYDRATION CALCULATOR
- INPUTS: Body Weight (kg), Daily Exercise (minutes), Climate/Environment (Normal vs Hot/Humid).
- LOGIC: Baseline = Weight in kg × 0.033 L. Add +0.35 L per 30 mins exercise. Add +0.5 L if in hot climate.
- OUTPUT: Total Daily Water Goal in Liters, Milliliters, and number of 500ml Water Bottles.

---
### 4. BMR, TDEE & CALORIE DEFICIT CALCULATOR
- INPUTS: Gender, Age, Height (cm), Weight (kg), Activity Level (Sedentary, Lightly Active, Moderately Active, Very Active).
- FORMULA: Mifflin-St Jeor Equation for BMR. TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier.
- OUTPUT: BMR calories, TDEE calories, Weight Maintenance target, and Calorie Deficit target (-500 kcal/day for ~0.5kg/week loss).

---
### 5. BLOOD DONATION ELIGIBILITY & RE-DONATION CALCULATOR
- INPUTS: Gender, Age, Weight (kg), Date of Last Blood Donation.
- LOGIC: Age must be 18–65. Weight must be ≥ 50kg. Male donation gap = 90 days. Female donation gap = 120 days.
- OUTPUT: Instant Eligibility Status (Eligible vs Ineligible) + Earliest Date Allowed for Next Donation.
