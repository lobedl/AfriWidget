Complete Guide to Zambian University GPA Systems & Degree Honours

Compiled by the AfriWidget Academic Review Board in accordance with University of Zambia (UNZA) and Higher Education Authority (HEA) regulations.

1. Understanding the UNZA & CBU 5.0 Grading Scale

Unlike the ECZ secondary school system (where lower numbers like 1 are best), Zambian universities follow tertiary academic conventions where higher Grade Point Averages (GPA) indicate higher academic excellence.

At major public universities such as the University of Zambia (UNZA), Copperbelt University (CBU), and Mulungushi University, undergraduate degrees operate primarily on a 5.0 Point Quality Scale:

Official UNZA / CBU 5.0 Grading & Quality Point Matrix

A+ (Distinction)86% – 100% (5.0 Points)
A (Distinction)76% – 85% (4.0 Points)
B+ (Meritorious)68% – 75% (3.0 Points)
B (Very Satisfactory)61% – 67% (2.5 Points)
C+ (Definite Pass)56% – 60% (2.0 Points)
C (Bare Pass)50% – 55% (1.5 Points)
D+ (Bare Fail / Supp Eligible)45% – 49% (1.0 Point)
D (Clear Fail)0% – 44% (0.0 Points)

2. How Semester GPA and Cumulative CGPA Are Calculated

Your Grade Point Average is a weighted mathematical calculation that balances the letter grade you receive in each course against the credit unit weighting (contact hours or course units) of that course:

Step 1: Calculate Course Quality Points (QP)

For each course enrolled, multiply the course credit units by the grade point value:
Quality Points = Course Credit Units × Grade Point Value

Step 2: Calculate Semester GPA

Sum all quality points earned across all courses in the semester, and divide by the total number of credit units attempted:
Semester GPA = (Total Quality Points Earned) ÷ (Total Credit Units Attempted)

Step 3: Calculate Cumulative CGPA

Combine all historical quality points across all completed academic years and divide by the cumulative sum of all credit units attempted:
CGPA = (Sum of All Quality Points to Date) ÷ (Sum of All Credit Units to Date)

3. Zambian Degree Honours Classifications

Upon completion of your undergraduate degree (typically 4 years for BA/BSc/BEd, 5 years for Engineering/Law/Pharmacy, 6 years for MBChB Medicine), your graduating certificate is awarded with one of the following official honours standings:

Distinction (First Class Honours)

Awarded to top scholars demonstrating mastery across advanced coursework and final dissertation.

CGPA 4.50 – 5.00

Merit (Upper Second Class Honours)

Strong academic standing qualifying for direct postgraduate master's scholarships.

CGPA 3.75 – 4.49

Credit (Lower Second Class Honours)

Standard good standing across corporate recruitment and public service appointments.

CGPA 3.00 – 3.74

Pass

Satisfies degree graduation requirements without honours distinction.

CGPA 2.00 – 2.99

4. Worked Student Calculation Example

Example: Second Year UNZA Natural Sciences Student (Mutale)

Semester 1 Course Registrations & Grades:

MAT 2110 (Linear Algebra • 4 Credits):Grade A (4.0) = 16.0 QP
CSC 2010 (Data Structures • 4 Credits):Grade A+ (5.0) = 20.0 QP
PHY 2210 (Electronics • 4 Credits):Grade B+ (3.0) = 12.0 QP
STA 2050 (Applied Statistics • 4 Credits):Grade B (2.5) = 10.0 QP

Total Quality Points = 16.0 + 20.0 + 12.0 + 10.0 = 58.0 Quality Points

Total Credit Units = 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16 Credit Units

Semester GPA = 58.0 ÷ 16 = 3.625 GPA (Merit Standing)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if my GPA falls below 2.00 in a Zambian university? ▼

At UNZA, CBU, and Mulungushi, a semester GPA below 2.00 places a student on Academic Probation (Warning). If a student remains below 2.00 for two consecutive semesters, they face academic exclusion (suspension or deregistration). Maintaining a GPA of 2.00 or higher is required for good standing.

How do supplementary (supp) examinations affect my GPA? ▼

Students who score a D+ (45–49%) are typically permitted to write a supplementary examination. Upon passing a supplementary exam, university senate regulations usually cap the recorded grade at a C (1.5 Quality Points / Bare Pass), regardless of the numerical score achieved in the retake.

What is the difference between UNZA's 5.0 scale and a 4.0 scale? ▼

UNZA and CBU assign 5.0 quality points for an A+ (Distinction 86%+) and 4.0 for an A (76–85%). In American-style 4.0 scales (used by some private universities in Zambia like Cavendish and Eden), both A+ and A are capped at 4.0 points maximum.

Does my first-year GPA count toward my final degree classification? ▼

At UNZA and CBU, first-year courses in Natural Sciences and Humanities serve as general foundation prerequisites. In many schools, degree classification weighting is skewed towards 2nd, 3rd, and 4th/5th year advanced courses, though first-year grades remain on your permanent academic transcript.
