Solution via AfriWidget

Kwesi turned to AfriWidget’s KCSE KUCCPS Cluster Calculator and University Eligibility Checker. Within seconds of entering his subject grades, the tool calculated his exact weighted cluster points across engineering, information technology, and computer science faculties.

Measurable Outcome

  • Accurate Placement: Kwesi applied confidently to his top three university choices.
  • Result: Successfully admitted to his first-choice Electrical Engineering degree programme at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT).
  • Cost Saved: $0 spent on paid advisory agencies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are AfriWidget tools completely free with no sign-up required?

AfriWidget operates on a privacy-first, ad-supported model. By executing all calculation logic locally inside your browser, we minimize costly server infrastructure overhead and pass those savings directly to our users without charging subscription fees or requiring personal accounts.

Are my personal data or calculations stored on your servers?

No. All calculations, data inputs, financial figures, and personal information remain 100% private inside your web browser. Nothing is transmitted or saved to external databases.

Can I submit my own success story to be featured on AfriWidget?

Yes! We love hearing how our tools help students, business owners, and professionals across Africa. You can fill out the submission form on this page or contact us directly with your experience.

How accurate are the university cluster and APS calculators?

Our academic calculators are updated continuously based on official guidelines from examination bodies and tertiary placement boards (such as KUCCPS in Kenya, WASSCE/WAEC in West Africa, and USAf/APS in South Africa). However, final admission decisions rest solely with university admissions boards.
