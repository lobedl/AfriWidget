Complete Guide to Uganda University Admissions & PUJAB Weighting System

Gaining direct admission into public and private universities in Uganda requires navigating the official Public Universities Joint Admissions Board (PUJAB) weighting formula. Administered by the Academic Registrar committees of Makerere University (MAK), Kyambogo University (KYU), Makerere University Business School (MUBS), Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST), Gulu University, Busitema University, Muni University, and Kabale University, this formula converts Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) grades and Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) scores into a standardized weighted cut-off point.

1. How the PUJAB University Admission Weighting System Works

Every degree programme in Uganda has a defined curriculum structure with specific prerequisite subjects. When evaluating an applicant's UACE examination slip, the university weights subjects according to their relevance to the intended degree:

  • Essential Subjects (Weight = 3.0): The two core principal subjects mandatory for the discipline. Grade points (A=6, B=5, C=4, D=3, E=2, O=1, F=0) are multiplied by 3. Maximum contribution is 36.0 points (18.0 points per subject).
  • Relevant Subjects (Weight = 2.0): The third principal subject providing foundational knowledge for the programme. The grade point is multiplied by 2. Maximum contribution is 12.0 points.
  • Desirable Subjects (Weight = 1.0): Any other principal subject taken beyond the essential and relevant requirements. Multiplied by 1. Maximum contribution is 6.0 points.
  • Subsidiary Subjects (Weight = 1.0): General Paper (S101) and Subsidiary Mathematics or Subsidiary ICT. A subsidiary pass (Grades 1 to 6 on the subsidiary scale) contributes 1.0 point each (total 2.0 points).
  • UCE O-Level Distinctions & Credits: Performance in the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) adds crucial fractions to break ties. Distinctions (D1, D2) add 0.3 points each, Credits (C3, C4, C5, C6) add 0.2 points each, and Passes (P7, P8) add 0.1 points each across the candidate's best 8 or 10 subjects.
  • Gender & Affirmative Action Bonus (+1.5 Points): To promote female enrolment in tertiary institutions, the Ministry of Education and Sports awards an additional 1.5 points to all female applicants applying for public university admissions.

4. Admission Schemes in Ugandan Public Universities

Ugandan public universities admit students under four main sponsorship pathways:

  • 1. National Merit (Government Sponsorship): The highest-ranked applicants nationwide regardless of district origin. Tuition, functional fees, and living allowances are fully funded by the Government of Uganda.
  • 2. District Quota Scheme: Government sponsorship awarded to top-performing students from each recognized district in Uganda to ensure equitable regional access to higher education.
  • 3. Sports & Special Needs / Disability Quota: Dedicated government scholarships reserved for outstanding national athletes and candidates with recognized physical disabilities.
  • 4. Private Sponsorship Scheme (Direct Entry): Students who meet the NCHE 2 Principal Passes requirement and departmental cut-off points fund their tuition directly or through the Higher Education Students Financing Board (HESFB) student loan scheme.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if I get a Subsidiary pass (Grade O) in an Essential subject? ▼
A Grade O earns 1 raw point. Multiplied by the Essential weighting factor of 3.0, it gives you 3.0 weighted points. However, most highly competitive faculties (such as Medicine, Law, or Engineering) explicitly require a minimum of a Principal Pass (Grade E or better) in both Essential subjects to be eligible for consideration.
How many points does General Paper (GP) and Sub-Math add? ▼
General Paper (GP) and Subsidiary Mathematics or Subsidiary ICT carry a weight factor of 1.0. A passing grade (Grade 1 to 6 on the subsidiary slip) gives you 1.0 point each, providing a maximum of 2.0 points toward your total university admission score.
Can I combine UACE results from two different exam years? ▼
No. Under the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) and PUJAB regulations, all three Principal subjects, General Paper, and Subsidiary subject must be obtained at the same examination sitting. Combining subjects across multiple years is strictly prohibited for direct undergraduate entry.
Where can I apply if my score is below the degree cut-off points? ▼
If your weighted score falls below the degree cut-off, you can apply for:
  • Ordinary Diploma Programmes (OD): Requires 1 Principal Pass and 2 Subsidiary Passes. After 2 years of diploma study with a Second Class Upper or First Class, you qualify for direct admission into Year 2 of the bachelor's degree.
  • Private Evening or Weekend Programmes: Evening programmes typically have lower cut-off thresholds than day sessions.
  • Higher Education Certificate (HEC): A 1-year bridging course accredited by NCHE for students with 1 Principal Pass or Subsidiary passes to qualify for degree entry.
