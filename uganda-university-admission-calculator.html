1. How Uganda’s PUJAB admission weighting system works

Admission to public universities in Uganda—including Makerere University, Kyambogo University, Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST), Makerere University Business School (MUBS), Gulu University, and Busitema University—is not decided simply by adding raw UACE A-Level points out of 20. Instead, the Public Universities Joint Admissions Board (PUJAB) applies a programme-specific weighted system that rewards strong performance in the two subjects most critical to your chosen degree while still folding in your O-Level UNEB record.

2. Essential, Relevant, Desirable, and Subsidiary multipliers

Every undergraduate degree programme assigns each of your three Principal A-Level subjects into weighting categories using the UNEB numerical grade points (A=6, B=5, C=4, D=3, E=2, O=1, F=0):

  • Essential Subjects (×3): Usually the two primary subjects required for the degree (such as Biology and Chemistry for Medicine, or Mathematics and Physics for Civil Engineering). Maximum contribution: 36.0 points.
  • Relevant Subject (×2): Your third Principal subject supporting the degree (such as Physics for Medicine, or Economics/Chemistry for Engineering). Maximum contribution: 12.0 points.
  • Desirable / Subsidiary Subjects (×1): General Paper and Subsidiary Mathematics or Subsidiary ICT each contribute 1.0 point on passing (D1–P8), adding up to 2.0 points.

3. Converting UCE O-Level grades into PUJAB points

Your Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) O-Level sitting contributes up to 3.0 points across your best 10 subjects (or 8 best under legacy weighting, capped cleanly per subject): Distinction 1 or 2 earns 0.3 points each, Credit 3 through 6 earns 0.2 points each, and Pass 7 or 8 earns 0.1 points each. Female candidates automatically receive an extra 1.5 affirmative action bonus points at public universities.

5. The two Principal Pass rule and Diploma entry routes

Before PUJAB weights are even ranked, every applicant for a Bachelor’s degree in Uganda must meet the statutory National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) minimum: at least two Principal Passes (grade E or better, 2+ points) obtained at the same UACE sitting, plus at least five passes at UCE O-Level. Candidates with one Principal Pass qualify for accredited Diploma programmes at Kyambogo, MUBS, or technical colleges, which can later bridge into university degree entry.

6. Worked Makerere & Kyambogo examples

Example: BCM/ICT applicant with A in Chemistry, B in Biology, C in Mathematics

For Makerere MBChB, Chemistry (6 × 3 = 18.0) and Biology (5 × 3 = 15.0) are Essential, Mathematics (4 × 2 = 8.0) is Relevant, and passing General Paper and Sub-ICT adds 2.0 points—giving 43.0 from UACE. With 4 UCE Distinctions (1.2), 5 Credits (1.0), and 1 Pass (0.1), O-Level adds 2.3 points for a composite score of 45.3 (or 46.8 with the 1.5 female bonus), placing the candidate squarely in National Merit range.

Disclaimer: Cut-off points fluctuate every academic year based on national UACE grade distributions and available government/private slots, and programmes like Makerere Law require a separate pre-entry examination. Always verify official weights and cut-offs published by the Directorate of Academic Registrar at your chosen university.

Frequently asked questions

How does the PUJAB weighting system work for Ugandan universities?

PUJAB assigns UACE subjects as Essential (×3), Relevant (×2), and Desirable/Subsidiary (×1), then adds UCE O-Level points (Distinction 0.3, Credit 0.2, Pass 0.1) plus 1.5 bonus points for female applicants.

What is the maximum possible PUJAB score?

The academic maximum is 53.0 points (48 from three Principal As, 2 from Subsidiaries, and 3.0 from ten UCE Distinctions), or 54.5 points including the 1.5 female affirmative action bonus.

Who qualifies for the 1.5 female affirmative action bonus points?

All female Ugandan applicants applying to public universities receive 1.5 bonus points added to their composite academic weight.

What are the minimum UACE passes required for university entry in Uganda?

Applicants must hold at least two Principal Passes (grade E or higher) obtained in the same UACE sitting to qualify for direct Bachelor’s degree admission.

Does this calculator save my grades on a server?

No. All grade weighting happens privately inside your browser.
