What Is AfriWidget?

AfriWidget is a free, browser-based platform that puts practical calculations and intelligent explanations directly into your hands — with no app downloads, no sign-ups, and absolutely no hidden fees. We believe that access to high-quality information and useful tools should not be a privilege reserved for a few. Whether you are a student planning your university admission, a professional managing your finances, or a parent tracking your family's health metrics, AfriWidget provides the accurate, real-time insights you need to make confident decisions.

Our platform was born from a simple but profound observation: millions of people across Africa and the developing world lack access to the personalized financial advisors, nutrition coaches, and academic counselors that wealthier demographics take for granted. While a student in London or New York can easily access free academic planning tools and career guidance, a student in rural Kenya, Ghana, or South Africa often faces barriers — unreliable internet, expensive consultancy fees, or simply no access to accurate, up-to-date information.

AfriWidget closes that gap, one smart widget at a time. Every tool is carefully crafted to be simple, intuitive, and immediately useful — turning complex calculations into clear, actionable answers. We don't just give you numbers; we help you understand what those numbers mean for your life, your education, your health, and your financial future. Our optional realtime AI assistant provides context, explanations, and practical next steps based on your specific inputs and goals.

The name AfriWidget reflects our dual commitment: serving African users and the global community with the same quality of service, and delivering compact, powerful "widgets" that solve real problems. We are proudly headquartered in Accra, Ghana, and our team spans the continent — bringing together developers, educators, financial analysts, and health professionals who share a common vision of digital inclusion and knowledge democratisation.

AfriWidget is more than a collection of calculators. It is a movement toward a future where every person, regardless of their geographical location or economic status, can access the information and insights they need to build a better life. We are committed to staying 100% free, 100% private, and 100% focused on empowering our users. Because when knowledge is accessible, opportunity follows.

Why AfriWidget Exists — The Story Behind the Platform

The idea for AfriWidget came out of a very personal experience. Our founder, Obed Avorlenu, was helping a relative navigate university admissions in Ghana. The whole process felt stressful, confusing, and unnecessarily expensive — families and eager young students were paying hard-earned money to private "consultants" who were simply applying standard formulas that anyone could calculate if they had the right tool. Having studied business at Keta Business College and taught himself web development from scratch with a laptop and curiosity, Obed saw the problem clearly: these calculations didn't need gatekeepers or expensive consultation fees. They could be completely digitised, kept 100% free, and put directly into the hands of anyone with a smartphone.

That initial project — a WASSCE aggregate calculator — quickly grew into a comprehensive platform. Students asked for GPA calculators. Teachers requested BECE placement tools. Parents wanted health and fitness trackers. Entrepreneurs needed business calculators. Freelancers asked for invoicing and pricing tools. Each request revealed a gap in the market: there was no single, trusted, free platform where Africans could find all these tools in one place.

AfriWidget was built to fill that gap. Every tool is designed with the African user in mind — considering slower internet connections, older device compatibility, and the specific academic and financial contexts of the continent. We localise where it matters (WASSCE, KCSE, JAMB, APS, NSFAS) and globalise where it helps (UCAS, ATAR, GMAT, compound interest).

Today, AfriWidget serves users from over 50 countries across Africa, Europe, the Americas, and Asia. Our traffic is growing organically because users trust us — they know we don't sell their data, we don't charge for features, and we never compromise on accuracy. This trust is our most valuable asset, and we protect it fiercely.
