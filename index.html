What Is AfriWidget?

AfriWidget is a free, browser-based platform that puts practical calculations and intelligent explanations directly into your hands — with no app downloads, no sign-ups, and absolutely no hidden fees. We believe that access to high-quality information and useful tools should not be a privilege reserved for a few.

Whether you are planning a WASSCE or BECE aggregate, calculating your cumulative GPA, understanding your child's body mass index, or planning a savings strategy, AfriWidget provides the calculations, explanations, and insights you need to make confident, data-informed decisions.

Our platform was born from the recognition that millions of Africans — and people worldwide — lack access to the personalized financial advisors, nutrition coaches, and academic counselors that wealthier demographics take for granted. AfriWidget closes that gap, one smart widget at a time.

Our Mission & Privacy Promise

Privacy First

Most calculations stay in your browser. Finance and Education tools may send limited calculation context through our secure Groq proxy for an AI insight, but we have no user accounts or calculator-submission database and do not use calculator inputs for advertising targeting.

Accessible to All

AfriWidget is built for users across Africa and the developing world. Our tools work on low-bandwidth connections and older devices. No app store. No premium tier. No paywalls. Just knowledge, freely accessible.

AI-Powered Insights

Our tools go beyond simple math. Local logic and Groq-powered Finance and Education insights explain what your results mean, why they matter, and what steps you can take next — translating numbers into real-world guidance.
