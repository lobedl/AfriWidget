About AfriWidget

Practical tools. Real solutions.

AfriWidget is a free, browser-based platform that puts practical calculations and intelligent explanations directly into your hands — with no app downloads, no sign-ups, and absolutely no hidden fees. We believe access to high-quality tools should not be a privilege reserved for a few. Whether you are a student planning your university admission, a professional managing your finances, or a parent tracking your family's health metrics, AfriWidget provides the accurate, real-time insights you need to make confident decisions.

Our platform was born from the recognition that millions of people across Africa and the developing world lack access to the personalized financial advisors, nutrition coaches, and academic counselors that wealthier demographics take for granted. AfriWidget closes that gap, one smart widget at a time. Every tool is carefully crafted to be simple, intuitive, and immediately useful — turning complex calculations into clear, actionable answers.

Founded by Ghanaian web developer Obed Avorlenu, AfriWidget is built on the belief that geography and finances should never restrict access to essential educational, financial, and productivity calculations. 100% free, private, and calculated in-browser — because your data belongs to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

AfriWidget has no user accounts and does not use calculator inputs for advertising targeting. Most calculations run locally in your browser. Finance and Education tools may send the relevant calculation context through a secure server-side proxy to generate a Groq-powered AI insight; do not include names or other personal identifiers in calculator fields. We do not maintain a database of calculator submissions.
Our calculators use internationally recognised, clinically and academically validated formulas. Our BMI tool follows WHO guidelines, our BMR calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (considered the gold standard), and our GPA tool reflects standard 4.0-scale grading conventions. However, our tools are for informational purposes only and should not replace advice from qualified medical, financial, or academic professionals.
AfriWidget is designed for everyone, with a particular focus on users across Africa and other developing regions who may lack access to premium advisory services. Our tools work on older smartphones, slow internet connections, and a wide range of devices and browsers. We are committed to digital inclusion and believe that geography should never determine access to quality decision-making tools.
