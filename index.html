Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Everything on this site is 100% free. You don't have to create an account, you don't have to put in an email, and you never have to provide credit card details. There are no locked features, no trial periods, and no hidden subscriptions. You open the tool, use it as many times as you need, and close the tab when you're done.
No. AfriWidget does not run user accounts or maintain a database of calculator inputs. When you calculate your BMI, enter your exam grades, or test a loan payment, all that math happens locally on your own phone or laptop. None of those numbers get sent to a database or saved on a server. Once you refresh or leave the page, your inputs disappear immediately.
Every tool is built using standard, verified formulas from official sources. For health tools like BMI and BMR, standard World Health Organization thresholds and the Mifflin-St Jeor equation are applied. Academic tools for WASSCE, KCSE, and JAMB follow official grading guidelines from the respective examination boards. Financial calculators use standard compound interest and loan amortization math. These tools are built to give quick, accurate estimates; for major medical decisions or official loan contracts, always double-check with a licensed professional.
AfriWidget is built for everyday people: students checking admission scores, market traders figuring out profit markups, freelance workers needing an invoice PDF, and families working out a savings budget. Many calculator sites online assume a high-speed 5G connection and an expensive phone. These pages are built specifically so they load instantly on a basic smartphone using affordable prepaid data bundles anywhere in the world.
When you load a page on AfriWidget, your browser downloads a tiny piece of JavaScript that contains the math formulas. When you press calculate, your phone or computer does all the math right there on your device. Nothing gets sent across the internet to a remote machine. That's why the results pop up instantly, and that's why your numbers stay private on your own screen.
Yes, absolutely. In fact, that's how most people use it. Every button, input box, and table is designed to work comfortably on a phone screen without having to zoom in or pinch. The pages are lightweight so they don't eat your mobile data or freeze up on older devices. You don't need to install any app from the Play Store or App Store. Just bookmark the site in your mobile browser.
New tools are added regularly each month, with most additions coming directly from suggestions sent by users. If there is an exam calculator, currency conversion, or business utility you need that is not yet on the platform, send a suggestion through the contact page. Every request is reviewed and prioritized for development.
Standard Google AdSense advertisements run on the site. Those small ads generate enough income to cover web hosting, domain renewals, and ongoing development of new tools. You won't find loud pop-ups, video auto-plays, or deceptive download buttons here. Ads are kept quiet and non-intrusive so you can get your numbers and move on with your day.
