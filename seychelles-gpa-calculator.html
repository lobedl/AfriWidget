The Tertiary Grading & GPA Architecture in Seychelles

In the tertiary education landscape of Seychelles, including programs at the University of Seychelles (UniSey), The Guy Morel Institute (TGTC), the Seychelles Institute of Technology (SIT), and the National Institute of Health and Social Studies (NIHSS), academic achievement is measured using the Credit System and Grade Point Average (GPA). Standardized under the Seychelles National Qualifications Framework (SNQF) by the Seychelles Qualifications Authority (SQA), the GPA system weights each module's numerical grade against its allocated credit value, ensuring academic transparency and facilitating international degree equivalence.

UniSey & Seychelles Tertiary Grade Point Equivalence

Degree and advanced diploma courses in Seychelles assign letter grades, percentage bands, and quality point equivalents as follows:

Grade A+ (90% – 100%) 4.00 (or 4.30)

Outstanding mastery of course competencies. First Class benchmark.

Grade A (80% – 89%) 4.00 Quality Pts

Excellent performance with comprehensive theoretical comprehension.

Grade B+ (75% – 79%) 3.50 Quality Pts

Very good academic achievement. Upper Second Class standing.

Grade B (70% – 74%) 3.00 Quality Pts

Solid understanding of curriculum. Standard for postgraduate clearance.

Grade C+ (65% – 69%) 2.50 Quality Pts

Competent performance. Lower Second Class boundary.

Grade C (60% – 64%) 2.00 Quality Pts

Statutory pass mark. Minimum acceptable threshold for degree graduation.

Grade D (50% – 59%) 1.00 Quality Pt

Marginal / Compensatory pass. May require course repetition under faculty rules.

Grade F (< 50%) 0.00 Quality Pts

Fail. Zero credits earned; course must be re-registered.

Mathematical Formulation: SGPA and Cumulative CGPA

GPA calculation uses credit weighting rather than simple arithmetic averaging. Every course module carries a defined number of credit hours (typically 3, 4, or 6 credits), reflecting the weekly lecture and laboratory commitment:

1. Course Quality Points = Course Credit Hours × Grade Point Equivalent

2. Semester GPA (SGPA) = Total Semester Quality Points ÷ Total Semester Credit Hours

3. Cumulative GPA (CGPA) = (Prior Quality Points + Current Semester Quality Points) ÷ (Prior Total Credits + Current Semester Credits)

Degree Honors Classification at the University of Seychelles

Upon completion of an undergraduate degree (typically 360 UK credits or 120 UniSey credits over three years), degree classes are awarded according to the final Cumulative GPA:

  • First Class Honours (1st): CGPA of 3.60 to 4.00. Denotes superlative scholarship. Strongly prioritized for ANHRD postgraduate master's and doctoral scholarships abroad.
  • Upper Second Class Honours (2:1): CGPA of 3.00 to 3.59. High merit standing; standard qualification for professional career progression and direct entry to graduate research programs.
  • Lower Second Class Honours (2:2): CGPA of 2.50 to 2.99. Satisfactory academic standing; qualifies for undergraduate graduation and civil service roles.
  • Third Class Honours (3rd): CGPA of 2.00 to 2.49. Passing standard.
  • Academic Probation: A semester or cumulative GPA below 2.00 triggers an official academic warning. Sustained underperformance may lead to suspension of government bursaries.

Step-by-Step Realistic Worked Examples

Case 1: First-Year UniSey Business Administration Student (Semester 1) SGPA Calculation

Student: Marcus enrolled in 4 modules (12 credit hours total) during his first semester at the Anse Royale campus:

  • Management (3 credits): Grade A (4.00 points) → 3 × 4.00 = 12.0 Quality Points
  • Economics (3 credits): Grade B+ (3.50 points) → 3 × 3.50 = 10.5 Quality Points
  • Accounting (3 credits): Grade B (3.00 points) → 3 × 3.00 = 9.0 Quality Points
  • Statistics (3 credits): Grade C+ (2.50 points) → 3 × 2.50 = 7.5 Quality Points

Total Quality Points: 12.0 + 10.5 + 9.0 + 7.5 = 39.0 points.

Total Credit Hours: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12 credits.

Semester SGPA: 39.0 ÷ 12 = 3.25.

Verdict: Upper Second Class standing (3.25), comfortably exceeding the 2.00 threshold and in full compliance with ANHRD student allowance conditions.

Case 2: Second-Year Student Computing Cumulative CGPA CGPA Progression

Student Profile: Alisha completed Year 1 with a cumulative GPA of 3.40 across 30 credit hours. In her third semester, she completed 15 credits, earning 54.0 quality points (Semester GPA = 3.60).

Prior Quality Points: 30 credits × 3.40 = 102.0 quality points.

Current Semester Points: 54.0 quality points (15 credits).

Combined Quality Points: 102.0 + 54.0 = 156.0 quality points.

Combined Credit Hours: 30 + 15 = 45 credit hours.

New Cumulative CGPA: 156.0 ÷ 45 = 3.47.

Verdict: CGPA increased from 3.40 to 3.47, solidifying her position in the Upper Second Class bracket and approaching First Class honors contention.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA in Seychelles? ▼

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) evaluates your academic performance exclusively across the modules registered in a single term. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) aggregates all grades and credit points earned from your first semester up to the current date.

Can a failing grade (Grade F) be replaced if I resit the module? ▼

Under UniSey and SQA regulations, when a student retakes a failed course, the higher grade earned is incorporated into the cumulative CGPA calculation, although the original registration may remain recorded on the official academic transcript.

What minimum GPA is required to maintain an ANHRD student allowance? ▼

Students on government sponsorship and living allowances administered by ANHRD must maintain good academic standing, defined as a minimum CGPA of 2.00 (Grade C pass average) with no unexcused module failures. Falling into academic probation can result in suspension of monthly allowances.

How do credits from SIT and TGTC transfer into a UniSey degree? ▼

Advanced Diplomas from SIT or The Guy Morel Institute (TGTC) accredited under the Seychelles National Qualifications Framework (SNQF Level 6) can be articulated into UniSey undergraduate degrees with advanced standing, typically granting direct entry into the second year of a related bachelor's program.
