Complete Guide to the Tanzania University 5.0 GPA & Degree Classification System

In the United Republic of Tanzania, the Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) regulates academic degree standards across public and private universities. The standard undergraduate grading structure is built on a 5-point Grade Point Average (GPA) scale. Whether you are enrolled at the University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM), Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA), the University of Dodoma (UDOM), Mzumbe University, the Institute of Finance Management (IFM), or the Open University of Tanzania (OUT), your academic standing, scholarship qualification, and degree classification are governed by this 5.0 scale.

1. Official Tanzania University Grading Scale (TCU Standard)

Examinations and continuous assessments (CA) in Tanzanian universities are graded on percentage bands converted into letter grades and quality points:

Grade A (70% – 100%) Performance: Excellent
5.0 Grade Points
Grade B+ (60% – 69%) Performance: Very Good
4.0 Grade Points
Grade B (50% – 59%) Performance: Good
3.0 Grade Points
Grade C (40% – 49%) Performance: Marginal Pass
2.0 Grade Points
Grade D (35% – 39%) Performance: Marginal Fail (Eligible for Supplementary Examination)
1.0 Grade Point
Grade E (0% – 34%) Performance: Absolute Fail (Repeat Course or Discontinuation)
0.0 Grade Points

2. Degree Classifications & Honours Thresholds

Upon completion of all required core and elective modular credits for your bachelor degree program, your final Cumulative GPA (CGPA) determines your statutory degree classification:

1. First Class Honours (GPA: 4.40 – 5.00)

The highest academic distinction awarded to students who maintain exceptional mastery across all core modules. Highly competitive for automatic academic staff recruitment (Tutorial Assistant positions) and international postgraduate scholarships.

2. Upper Second Class Honours / Class II Division I (GPA: 3.50 – 4.39)

Signifies strong, consistent academic excellence. In Tanzania, a minimum GPA of 3.50 or Upper Second is the statutory prerequisite set by TCU and universities for direct admission into Master's degree and postgraduate programs.

3. Lower Second Class Honours / Class II Division II (GPA: 2.70 – 3.49)

A respectable and widely awarded bachelor degree classification accepted across the corporate, financial, civil service, and private employment sectors.

4. Pass Degree (GPA: 2.00 – 2.69)

The minimum threshold required for undergraduate graduation. Students whose overall CGPA is below 2.00 fail to satisfy degree requirements and are subject to academic discontinuation.

3. How GPA & CGPA Quality Points are Calculated

In the Tanzanian modular system, each course module carries a defined number of credits (e.g. 7.5, 10, or 12 credits, where 1 credit represents approximately 10 notional learning hours):

Quality Points (QP) = Course Module Credits × Letter Grade Point
Semester GPA = Sum of all Quality Points / Sum of all Semester Credits
Cumulative CGPA = Sum of all Lifetime Quality Points / Sum of all Completed Credits

4. Step-by-Step Worked Student Examples

Example A: Ashura – Year 1 Semester 1 B.Sc Computer Science at UDSM

- IS 161 (Basic Computer Programming): 12 Credits, Grade A (5.0 GP) → QP = 12 × 5.0 = 60.0
- MT 100 (Foundations of Analysis): 12 Credits, Grade B+ (4.0 GP) → QP = 12 × 4.0 = 48.0
- CS 174 (Computer Architecture): 10 Credits, Grade B (3.0 GP) → QP = 10 × 3.0 = 30.0
- DS 101 (Development Perspectives): 8 Credits, Grade A (5.0 GP) → QP = 8 × 5.0 = 40.0
- Total Semester Credits: 12 + 12 + 10 + 8 = 42 Credits
- Total Quality Points: 60.0 + 48.0 + 30.0 + 40.0 = 178.0 Quality Points
- Semester GPA Calculation: 178.0 ÷ 42 = 4.24 GPA
- Result: Upper Second Class standing (Class II Division I).

Example B: Baraka – Final Year Cumulative CGPA Computation at UDOM

- Previous Record (Years 1 to 2): 240 Completed Credits, Cumulative Quality Points = 888.0 (Prior CGPA = 3.70)
- Year 3 (Final Year) Record: 120 Completed Credits, Final Year Quality Points = 504.0 (Final Year GPA = 4.20)
- Combined Total Credits: 240 + 120 = 360 Credits
- Combined Total Quality Points: 888.0 + 504.0 = 1392.0 Quality Points
- Final Cumulative CGPA: 1392.0 ÷ 360 = 3.87 CGPA
- Graduation Degree Verdict: Bachelor of Education with Upper Second Class Honours.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if I score a Grade D in a core module? ▼

In most Tanzanian universities, a Grade D (35%–39%) is a marginal fail that allows the student to sit for a Supplementary Examination before the start of the subsequent academic year. When a supplementary exam is passed, the maximum grade awarded for that course is capped at Grade C (2.0 GP).

What is the minimum GPA required to avoid discontinuation? ▼

Under TCU guidelines and standard university prospectuses (such as UDSM, UDOM, and SUA), a student must maintain a minimum semester and cumulative GPA of 2.00 and pass at least half of the registered core credits to remain in good academic standing. Falling below 2.00 may result in academic probation or discontinuation.

What GPA do I need to pursue a Master's degree in Tanzania? ▼

The Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) specifies that candidates seeking direct admission into Master's degree programs must possess a bachelor degree with a minimum classification of Upper Second Class (minimum CGPA of 3.50 on the 5.0 scale) or a Lower Second (2.70–3.49) accompanied by relevant postgraduate diplomas or verified professional experience.

How are Continuous Assessment (CA) and Final University Exams weighted? ▼

Most Tanzanian university courses allocate 40% of the course grade to Continuous Assessment (tests, quizzes, laboratory practicals, and group presentations) and 60% to the University Examination (UE) written at the end of the semester. Students must typically score a minimum of 16 out of 40 in CA to be eligible to sit for the UE.
