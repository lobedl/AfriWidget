How to Plan Your TVET Final Examination Target

Knowing your required examination score transforms revision into a strategic process. In NATED Report 191 courses (N1–N6), the final examination carries a 60% weight while ICASS carries 40%. In NC(V) vocational subjects, the national exam carries 50%, ISAT practical carries 25%, and ICASS carries 25%.

The DHET Sub-Minimum Rule

The Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET) enforces a mandatory 40% sub-minimum on external examination papers. Even if your ICASS score is high enough to mathematically bring your overall total above 40%, you will fail if your examination mark is below 40.0%.

Distinction (Cum Laude) Requirements

To receive a subject distinction in TVET NATED or NC(V) courses, a final combined percentage of 75% or higher must be achieved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pass if my ICASS mark is below 40%?

At most TVET colleges, an ICASS mark of at least 40% is mandatory just to qualify for examination admission (exam entrance). If your college has granted exam admission with a borderline ICASS (e.g., 38%), you will need a higher exam mark to reach an overall 40% pass.

Does a 75% final mark give me an official distinction?

Yes. Under DHET and TVET grading regulations, a final combined percentage of 75% or higher earns a Subject Distinction (Pass with Distinction / Cum Laude).
