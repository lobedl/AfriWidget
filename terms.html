AfriWidget provides financial calculators — such as loan repayment, mortgage, compound interest, savings projections, profit margins, and currency conversion. These tools are for educational and illustrative purposes only. They are not financial advice. They are not investment recommendations. They are not tax advice. They are not a substitute for a licensed financial advisor, accountant, or tax professional.

What Our Financial Tools Can Do

  • Show you how a loan's monthly payment would change if the interest rate changed.
  • Project how much savings might grow with compound interest over a certain number of years.
  • Compare profit margins between different pricing scenarios.
  • Convert currencies using current or reference exchange rates.

What Our Financial Tools Cannot Do

  • They cannot tell you whether a specific loan, investment, or business decision is right for you.
  • They cannot account for your full financial picture — debts, obligations, goals, taxes, and future needs.
  • They cannot predict market movements, interest rate changes, inflation, or economic conditions.
  • They cannot replace the professional judgement of a certified financial advisor.

The Assumptions Problem

Every financial projection is based on assumptions. When you enter "5% annual return" into a compound interest calculator, you are assuming that markets will provide exactly 5% every single year. Real markets do not work that way. Some years are up. Some years are down. Some years are flat. Our calculator cannot model the messy reality of real investing. It can only model the clean, idealised math of the formula.

Use our financial tools to understand the mechanics of financial math. Use a professional advisor to make decisions about your actual money.

Bottom line: AfriWidget is a calculator, not a financial advisor. Always consult a licensed professional before making investment, borrowing, or business decisions.

10. AI-Powered Insights Disclaimer

Some AfriWidget tools offer optional AI-powered insights. These are short, plain-language explanations of your calculation result, generated by a large language model running on external servers. The AI insights are provided as a convenience and are not guaranteed to be accurate.

How AI Insights Work

When you click an AI insight button, the tool sends a short, anonymous summary of your calculation through a secure proxy to the AI provider. The AI generates a response, and the response is sent back to your browser. The summary and response are not stored anywhere.

Why You Should Be Careful

AI models are not always correct. They can misunderstand context. They can produce confident-sounding answers that are wrong. They can occasionally invent facts. While we have tuned the prompts to encourage accurate and helpful responses, we cannot guarantee that every AI insight is correct.

Treat AI insights as a starting point for your own thinking, not as a final answer. Verify important information with a professional source. Do not act on AI-generated advice about your health, finances, or legal situation without first confirming it with a qualified expert.

A note on privacy: The AI feature does not send your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier. It sends only the anonymous numerical context of your calculation. If you do not want the AI feature to be used, simply do not click the AI button — the standard calculator result appears automatically without any data leaving your browser.

11. Accuracy of Results and Estimates

We work hard to make AfriWidget accurate. Every formula we use has been researched, tested, and cross-checked against official sources. Where possible, we cite the source of our formulas so that you can verify them yourself. That said, we cannot guarantee perfection.

Sources of Potential Error

  • Rounding differences: Different systems sometimes round numbers differently, leading to slight variations in results.
  • Formula variants: Some fields have multiple accepted formulas (for example, there are several different equations for ideal body weight). We choose the most widely used one, but another valid formula may give a slightly different answer.
  • Rule changes: Exam boards, tax authorities, and health organisations occasionally change their official rules. We update our tools when we learn about changes, but there may be a delay.
  • Regional variations: A rule that applies in one country may not apply in another. Our tools try to reflect regional differences, but we may not always succeed.
  • Input errors: The most common source of wrong results is a mistyped number. Always double-check your inputs.

For any decision with serious consequences, verify the result independently. Our tool is a helpful assistant, not an infallible authority.

21. Plain-Language Glossary

If any term in this agreement was unfamiliar, here is a short glossary in everyday language.

Terms of Service

A document that explains the rules for using a website or service. Sometimes called "Terms of Use" or "Terms and Conditions."

Intellectual Property

Ideas, designs, code, and content that belong to a person or organisation. This includes things like logos, writing, artwork, and software code.

Disclaimer

A statement that limits what a service promises. For example, our medical disclaimer clarifies that AfriWidget is not a doctor and cannot provide medical advice.

Liability

Legal responsibility for something. A "limitation of liability" clause means that in certain situations, the service is not legally responsible for the outcome.

Indemnification

An agreement to cover someone else's costs if they are sued because of your actions. In this document, it means that if you break the rules and someone sues us, you agree to cover our costs.

Jurisdiction

The legal territory where a dispute would be heard. Our jurisdiction is Ghana, where AfriWidget is operated from.

As-Is

A phrase meaning that a service is provided without guarantees. The service is offered "as it currently exists," and the provider does not promise anything beyond that.

As-Available

A phrase meaning that a service is provided when it is online. The provider does not promise continuous availability.

Mediation

A process in which two parties try to resolve a dispute with the help of a neutral third person, before going to court.

Attribution

Giving credit to the original source. If you use content from AfriWidget, you should clearly state that you got it from AfriWidget.
