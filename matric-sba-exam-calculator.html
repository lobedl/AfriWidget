How the NSC Matric SBA & Exam Pass Works

The National Senior Certificate (NSC) uses a dual-assessment model. School-Based Assessment (SBA) compiles all term tests, projects, and trial examinations throughout Grade 12 to count for 25% of your final mark. The external national examination counts for 75%. Life Orientation is assessed 100% internally through Common Assessment Tasks (CAT).

Umalusi National Pass Requirements

  • Bachelor's Degree Pass: Pass Home Language at 40%+, achieve at least 50% (Level 4) in four high-credit subjects, and at least 30% in two other subjects.
  • Diploma Pass: Pass Home Language at 40%+, achieve at least 40% (Level 3) in four subjects, and at least 30% in two other subjects.
  • Higher Certificate Pass: Pass Home Language at 40%+, achieve at least 40% in two subjects, and at least 30% in three other subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a strong SBA mark save a poor exam mark?

Yes, up to a mathematical limit. Because the SBA counts for 25% of your total grade, an SBA score of 80% contributes 20 guaranteed points towards your final 100-point total. However, the final examination accounts for 75%, meaning exam preparation remains the dominant factor.

How is Life Orientation calculated in Matric?

Unlike other subjects, Life Orientation is assessed 100% through School-Based Assessment (SBA), physical education participation, and the national Common Assessment Task (CAT) written in September.
