AdSense & Advertising DisclaimerHow Ads Keep AfriWidget Free

This page explains, in plain words, why AfriWidget shows ads, what Google AdSense does, what information is involved, and what stays entirely private no matter what you do on the site.

Effective Date: 1 January 2026 • Last Updated: 31 March 2026

The Short Version

AfriWidget is free because of advertising. We use Google AdSense to display those ads. Google picks which ads you see, and Google may use its own cookies to do that. We do not share your calculator inputs with anyone, ever. If you would rather not have personalised ads, you can turn them off, and every tool on the site will keep working exactly the same.

  • Ads pay for the servers, the domain, and the time it takes to build tools.
  • Google AdSense chooses the ads. We do not hand-pick them.
  • Calculator inputs never go to Google or to any advertiser.
  • You can decline personalised ads in one click.
  • Declining ads changes nothing about how the tools work.

1. Why There Are Ads At All

Running a website costs money. Servers, a domain name, security certificates, and the many hours spent building and updating tools all add up. AfriWidget has no subscription, no premium plan, and no paywall. It never will. So we need another way to cover those costs, and advertising is the one we chose.

We chose it deliberately over the alternatives. A subscription would exclude the students, parents, and small business owners who need these tools most. Selling user data would betray the trust people put in a site that promises in-browser calculation. A donation model would work only if enough people felt moved to give, and it would be unreliable from month to month. Advertising, done carefully, lets the site stay free for everyone, forever, without any of those compromises.

That said, we know ads can be annoying. We have tried to keep them out of the way. They sit in fixed positions on the page, never between you and your result, never popping up as you type, and never disguised as part of a tool. If you ever see an ad that feels like it is doing any of those things, please tell us and we will look into it.

2. What Google AdSense Actually Does

Google AdSense is the advertising programme we use. It is run by Google and used by hundreds of thousands of websites around the world. Here is how it works in plain terms.

When a page loads on AfriWidget, we tell Google that a slot is available. Google looks at the page — its topic, its language, the general category — and picks an ad from its pool of advertisers. Google then places that ad into the slot. We do not see the ad before you do, and we do not choose the advertiser.

Google may also use signals beyond the page itself, if you have advertising cookies enabled. Those signals can include your general location (country level, based on IP), the type of device you are using, and information Google already has about your browsing across other sites that use Google services. All of that is Google's system. We do not see any of it and we do not control any of it.

Because Google controls the ad selection, if you see an ad that feels out of place, the fastest fix is often to report it directly to Google using the small icon on the ad itself. We also welcome a note through our contact page, because it helps us fine-tune the categories we exclude.

3. What We Share, and What We Never Share

We want to be very clear about this section, so we have split it into two lists.

What we share with Google AdSense

We share only the standard, anonymous information that any website running AdSense shares:

  • The fact that a page has loaded and an ad slot is available.
  • The general topic of the page (for example, "health calculator" or "finance calculator").
  • The country you are connecting from, at a broad level, as determined by your IP address.
  • The type of device and browser you are using (mobile, desktop, Chrome, Safari, and so on).

None of this identifies you as an individual. Google cannot tell that "person X in Accra opened the BMI calculator at 3pm." It sees patterns and categories, not people.

What we never share with Google AdSense or any advertiser

  • Your calculator inputs. Not your weight, not your loan amount, not your exam grades, not anything you type into any tool.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier. We do not collect these on the calculator pages.
  • Your full IP address. We have IP anonymisation switched on for Analytics, and we do not forward IP-level data to advertisers ourselves.
  • Any information from a private message you send us through the contact page.

This is not a marketing promise. It is a description of how the site is actually built. The calculators run in your browser, so there is no technical mechanism by which your inputs could leave your device, even if we wanted them to. You can verify this yourself by watching your browser's network activity while using a tool.

4. Personalised Ads and How to Turn Them Off

Some ads are contextual, meaning they match the topic of the page. Others are personalised, meaning they reflect interests Google has inferred from your browsing across other websites. You have full control over whether you see personalised ads on AfriWidget and elsewhere.

Here are the ways to turn personalised advertising off:

  • Through our cookie banner. When you first visit, click Decline instead of Accept. This prevents the advertising cookies from loading at all.
  • Through Google Ads Settings. Visit adssettings.google.com and turn off personalised advertising for your Google account. This affects every site that uses Google Ads, not just ours.
  • Through your browser. Set your browser to block third-party cookies. This prevents most advertising cookies from being set on any website.
  • Through industry opt-out tools. Visit aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org to opt out of interest-based advertising from many companies at once.
  • Through browser extensions. Tools such as uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, and Ghostery can block advertising scripts across the web. AfriWidget works perfectly with all of them.

If you turn personalised ads off, you will still see ads on AfriWidget, but they will be generic rather than targeted. Every tool works exactly the same either way.

5. Ads and Your Calculator Inputs

This is the question we get most often, so we want to answer it directly.

No advertiser has access to anything you type into an AfriWidget calculator. Not the loan amount, not the body weight, not the exam grades, not the salary figures. None of it.

The reason is architectural, not just policy. Every calculator on the site runs inside your own browser. When you type a number and press Calculate, the maths happens on your device. There is no round trip to a server, no API call, no message sent anywhere. The result appears on your screen and disappears when you close the tab.

Advertisers see the page you are on — for example, "BMI Calculator" — because that information is part of the page itself. They do not see the number you entered. If you calculated a BMI of 23.4, Google has no idea. It only knows that someone in your country visited a BMI calculator page.

You can test this yourself. Open your browser's developer tools (usually by pressing F12), switch to the Network tab, and use any calculator. You will see no message sent to any server containing the numbers you typed. That is not a policy promise — it is a fact about how the site is built.

6. What We Do Not Allow

Google AdSense lets publishers set category filters, and we have used them. We do not allow advertisements for the following kinds of products or services on AfriWidget:

  • Predatory loans, payday lenders, or any financial product designed to trap people in debt.
  • Unverified health products making medical claims that no regulator has approved.
  • Gambling and betting services, including online casinos and sports betting apps.
  • Adult content or anything inappropriate for a general audience, including minors.
  • Get-rich-quick schemes, investment opportunities with guaranteed returns, or anything that sounds too good to be true.
  • Cryptocurrency speculation products that treat investing like a lottery.
  • Political advertising of any kind.

Google's filtering is not perfect. If an ad slips through that falls into one of these categories, please tell us. We can add the advertiser to our block list and, in most cases, the problem is fixed within a day.

7. Report a Bad Ad

If you see an advertisement on AfriWidget that you believe is misleading, offensive, or falls into one of the categories above, there are two ways to report it.

First, you can click the small information icon (an "i" or a small "x") that appears in the corner of most Google ads. That opens a menu that lets you report the ad directly to Google and even block it from appearing on your device again.

Second, you can send us a note through our contact page. Please include a short description of the ad, which page you saw it on, and, if possible, a screenshot. We cannot block a specific ad we cannot identify, so the screenshot really helps.

We read every report and treat them seriously. A well-functioning ad setup is in everyone's interest — it keeps the site free and it keeps the experience respectful.

8. Advertising on AfriWidget Directly

At the moment, all ads on AfriWidget are served through Google AdSense. We do not currently accept direct advertising from third parties. That may change in the future, and if it does, we will update this page to say so clearly.

If you are a business interested in reaching AfriWidget's audience and want to discuss a possible arrangement, you are welcome to send a message through the contact page using the "Advertising" subject option. We will read it and reply honestly about whether a fit exists.

One thing we will never do, whatever arrangement is proposed: we will not sell user data, we will not embed tracking scripts that observe calculator inputs, and we will not accept advertising that conflicts with the categories listed above. Those are not negotiable.

9. Changes to This Page

If we add a new advertising partner, change how ads are delivered, or change the way advertising cookies are handled, we will update this page and change the "Last Updated" date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also mention it on the homepage so regular visitors do not miss it.

Small wording tweaks do not trigger a homepage notice, but every change appears here first.

Common questions about advertising

Do I have to see ads to use AfriWidget?

Yes, but only if you want the site to stay free. Ads pay for the servers, the domain, and the time that goes into building and maintaining the tools. What you can control is whether those ads are personalised. Click Decline on the cookie banner and you will still see ads, but they will be generic rather than targeted at your browsing history.

Will my calculator results ever influence which ads I see?

No. The numbers you enter are processed inside your browser and never transmitted anywhere. Google AdSense sees the page you are on, not what you typed into it. If you calculated a loan repayment, Google knows someone visited a loan calculator page. It does not know the amount, the interest rate, or the result.

Why do some ads look unrelated to the page I am on?

Because Google AdSense decides which ads to show, based on a mix of signals that includes the page topic, the country you are visiting from, and, if you have personalised ads enabled, your browsing history elsewhere. Sometimes Google decides a general ad will perform better than a topic-matched one. If an ad feels genuinely out of place, please report it, and we will look at our category filters.

Can I turn ads off completely?

Not from within the site, but you can block advertising scripts in your browser using an extension such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger. AfriWidget works perfectly with all of them. The tools do not depend on ads in any way. If you choose to block ads, we lose the small revenue they would have generated, but the site keeps working for you exactly the same.

Do you sell my data to advertisers?

Never. We do not have a database of user information to sell, and we have no commercial relationship with advertisers beyond the standard AdSense revenue share. What little data exists (anonymous analytics) stays with us and is used only to understand which tools are worth improving.

Are the ads on AfriWidget safe to click?

We do not vet each ad individually, because Google controls the delivery. In practice, ads served through AdSense come from Google's advertiser network, which has its own review process, and misleading or malicious ads are removed quickly when reported. Still, please apply the same caution you would anywhere else on the internet: if an ad promises something that sounds too good to be true, it probably is. If you see one that feels wrong, report it to Google and let us know.

Why not use a subscription instead of ads?

Because a subscription would lock out the people who need these tools most. A student preparing for WASSCE or a parent checking their child's BMI should not have to pay to access a simple calculation. Advertising lets the site stay free and open to everyone, which is the whole point of the project.

What happens if I report a bad ad?

If you report it directly to Google, Google blocks that specific ad from your device and considers removing it from the network. If you report it to us, we review the category filter settings and can add specific advertisers to a block list so their ads stop appearing on AfriWidget for everyone. Between the two, most problematic ads are gone within a day.

See a problem with an ad?

Tell us which page it was on and what the ad looked like. We will investigate and, if needed, block the advertiser.
