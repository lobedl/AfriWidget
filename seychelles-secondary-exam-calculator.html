Understanding the Seychelles Secondary 5 (S5) & Cambridge IGCSE Examination System

In the Republic of Seychelles, secondary education spans five years from Secondary One (S1) through Secondary Five (S5). At the conclusion of S5, students complete their compulsory schooling cycle by sitting both the Cambridge International General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE) examinations and national secondary examinations administered in partnership with the Ministry of Education. Performance in S5 examinations is the central gatekeeper determining entry into Seychelles' specialized Post-Secondary Institutions (PSIs), most notably the School of Advanced Level Studies (SALS) for GCE Advanced Level study, as well as vocational and professional academies across Mahé and Praslin.

The Cambridge IGCSE 8-Point Numerical & Credit Conversion Scale

For post-secondary selection in Seychelles, Cambridge IGCSE grades are converted into numerical point equivalents and assessed against national credit standards. A grade of C or higher constitutes a certified "Credit Pass," which is mandatory for admission to advanced diplomas and A-Level streams:

Grade A* (or 9 / 8) 8 Points (Credit)

Exceptional performance. Automatic qualification for advanced subject streams at SALS.

Grade A (or 7) 7 Points (Credit)

Excellent mastery of syllabus. Strongly favored for competitive science and math courses.

Grade B (or 6) 6 Points (Credit)

Merit grade. Standard requirement for taking that specific subject at Advanced Level (A-Level).

Grade C (or 5 / 4) 5 Points (Credit)

Universal minimum Credit Pass. Mandatory in English and Mathematics across all PSI diploma pathways.

Grade D (or 3) 4 Points (Pass)

Ordinary pass. Does not count as an academic credit; eligible for certificate-level vocational programs.

Grade E (or 2) 3 Points (Pass)

Basic secondary pass. Satisfies foundational criteria for apprenticeship or entry certificate modules.

Grades F, G, and U 2, 1, or 0 Points

Grade F (2 pts), Grade G (1 pt), and Grade U / Ungraded (0 pts). Below standard certificate thresholds.

Post-Secondary Institution (PSI) Admission Rules in Seychelles

Seychellois students submit their Post-Secondary Institution (PSI) preference forms during the final term of S5. Admission is competitive and strictly based on credit counts and prerequisite grades:

1. School of Advanced Level Studies (SALS)

To qualify for the two-year GCE A-Level program at SALS (located at Anse Royale), candidates must obtain a minimum of 5 IGCSE Credit Passes (Grade C or above), including Credits in English Language and Mathematics. Furthermore, candidates must have achieved at least Grade B in the specific subjects they intend to study at Advanced Level (e.g. Grade B in IGCSE Physics and Chemistry for A-Level Physics and Chemistry).

2. Seychelles Institute of Technology (SIT)

Offers advanced diplomas and certificates in Mechanical Engineering, Telecommunications, Construction, and Automotive Technology. Advanced Diploma entry requires 4 credits including Mathematics, English, and a physical science subject. Certificate and apprenticeship courses require 2 to 3 passes.

3. National Institute of Health and Social Studies (NIHSS)

Prepares students for healthcare careers including Nursing, Environmental Health, and Social Work. The Diploma in Nursing requires at least 4 to 5 credits with mandatory credit passes in English, Mathematics, and Biology / Co-ordinated Science.

4. Seychelles Tourism Academy (STA)

Trains students for Seychelles' premier tourism and hospitality sector. The Advanced Diploma in Hospitality Management (run in partnership with Shannon College, Ireland) requires 4 credits including English Language. Certificate programs in culinary arts, tour guiding, and restaurant operations require 2 to 3 passes.

5. The Guy Morel Institute (TGTC) & UniSey Foundation

The Guy Morel Institute offers ACCA, business administration, and public sector qualifications requiring 4 credits including English and Mathematics. The University of Seychelles offers pre-degree access foundation pathways for students holding 4 credits who wish to bridge into undergraduate programs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the minimum grade required for a subject to count as a "Credit" in Seychelles? ▼

In Seychelles and under Cambridge IGCSE regulations, a grade of C or higher (or numerical grade 4 or higher on the 9-1 scale) is officially recognized as a Credit Pass. Grades D, E, F, and G are considered passes but do not qualify as credit passes for diploma or A-Level prerequisites.

Can I enter SALS if I have 5 credits but failed IGCSE Mathematics? ▼

No. The School of Advanced Level Studies (SALS) strictly requires credits in both English Language and Mathematics alongside three other academic subjects. If you have 5 credits but lack a Grade C in Mathematics or English, you may be redirected to an alternative PSI or required to sit a resit examination.

What is the difference between SALS entry and SIT / NIHSS entry? ▼

SALS provides purely academic preparation for Cambridge International A-Levels, requiring at least 5 IGCSE credits and Grade B in subject majors. SIT and NIHSS provide applied professional and technical diplomas, generally requiring 4 credits tailored to technical or healthcare competencies.

Can students from Praslin or La Digue apply for accommodation at SALS? ▼

Yes. Students from Praslin, La Digue, and other inner islands who gain admission to SALS or specialized PSIs in Mahé are eligible for government-supported student hostel accommodation or a living allowance provided through the Ministry of Education.

What happens if I obtain fewer than 4 credits at the end of S5? ▼

Students with 1 to 3 passes are eligible for certificate-level apprenticeship programs at SIT, culinary and beverage service certificates at STA, or direct vocational training programs designed to transition school leavers into employment.
