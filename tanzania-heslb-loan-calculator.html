Frequently asked questions

What is HESLB and who qualifies for its loans in Tanzania?
What cost items does the HESLB loan cover?

An annual HESLB package is built from distinct statutory items: Meals and Accommodation, Tuition Fees, Books and Stationery, Field Practical Training, Special Faculty Requirements where the programme demands them, and a one-time final year Research allowance. Meals and Accommodation is paid directly to the student, Tuition is paid directly to the university, and the remaining allowances are paid to the student's account in semester or quarterly instalments. The Tuition component is capped at a national ceiling per academic year, with the exact percentage awarded depending on the applicant's Means-Testing score.

What is the 6% Value Retention Fee and how does it differ from bank interest?

The Value Retention Fee is a 6% annual charge applied to the outstanding loan balance at the end of each financial year. It is not interest in the commercial sense; it exists to protect the revolving fund against inflation and currency depreciation so that future cohorts can be funded on the same terms. Because the fee compounds annually on the unpaid balance, the repayment schedule must be modelled as a reducing balance with monthly accrual, which is exactly what this calculator does.

How much is deducted from a graduate's salary each month?

Under Section 19 of the HESLB Act, employers are required to deduct 15 per cent of the graduate's monthly basic salary and remit it directly to HESLB until the loan is fully repaid. Self-employed graduates and those in the informal sector are required to make direct monthly payments of at least TZS 100,000, or 10 per cent of declared taxable monthly income, whichever is higher. The deduction continues until the principal, the one per cent administration fee, and the accrued Value Retention Fee are all cleared.

Is there a grace period before repayment begins?

Yes. Beneficiaries have a 24-month moratorium after graduation before active recovery of the loan begins. However, the 6 per cent Value Retention Fee continues to accrue on the outstanding balance during the grace period. Graduates who can start paying sooner reduce the total amount repaid, because the fee compounds on whatever balance remains.

What is the 1% Loan Administration Fee?

The Loan Administration Fee is a one-off charge of 1 per cent of the total cumulative principal disbursed across the study period. It covers the administrative cost of processing and maintaining the loan ledger. The fee is added to the principal at graduation, and the total is then subject to the 6 per cent Value Retention Fee in the same way as the base principal.

Can the calculator model a partial tuition allocation?

Yes. The Means-Testing tier field lets you model tuition allocations of 100 per cent, 80, 60, 40, and 20 per cent, or zero if you are self-paying tuition and only drawing Meals and Accommodation. The remaining statutory items (books, meals, field training) are added on top of the tuition share, exactly as HESLB packages them in an award letter.

Does this calculator store my personal details?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser tab and nothing is stored on any server. If you press the AI insight button, only an anonymous summary of the study duration, the annual allocation, the cumulative principal, the monthly deduction, the VRF accrued, and the estimated payoff timeline is sent to a small serverless function that runs once and then stops. Your name, admission number, university, and salary are never collected because they are never asked for.
