Liberia University Admission & Entrance Examination Guide: UL, Cuttington, Tubman & Tertiary Cut-Offs

Navigating university admission in the Republic of Liberia requires understanding two complementary evaluation tiers: national secondary certificate achievement via the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) and competitive institutional entrance examinations administered by individual universities. Overseen by the National Commission on Higher Education (NCHE), Liberian higher education comprises public flagship universities such as the University of Liberia (UL) in Monrovia and William V.S. Tubman University (TU) in Harper, alongside venerable faith-based and private institutions including Cuttington University (CU) in Suakoko, Stella Maris Polytechnic (SMP), African Methodist Episcopal University (AMEU), and the United Methodist University (UMU).

Securing a coveted placement in high-demand faculties—such as the A.M. Dogliotti College of Medicine, the College of Science & Technology, the College of Engineering, or the College of Business & Public Administration—demands strategic preparation across both high school credentials and aptitude testing.

The Dual Architecture of University Admission in Liberia

Unlike tertiary systems that rely entirely on a single centralized computerized placement portal, Liberian universities utilize a dual qualification framework:

1. The Statutory WASSCE Matriculation Baseline

The National Commission on Higher Education mandates that applicants for undergraduate bachelor's degree programmes present:

  • Minimum Five (5) Credit Passes (Grades A1 to C6) obtained in the WASSCE or WAEC examination.
  • Compulsory English Language Credit (Grade C6 or better): Required across all universities and faculties without exception.
  • Compulsory General Mathematics Credit (Grade C6 or better): Required for all STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics), Health Sciences, Agriculture, Business, and Economics programmes.
  • Relevant Track Credits: Three elective credits in subjects directly aligned with the intended collegiate major (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, and Physics for medical streams; Financial Accounting, Economics, and Commerce for business streams).
  • Applicants holding passes below credit level (Grades D7 or E8) may only be admitted into pre-university bridging programmes, associate degree pathways, or vocational diploma streams.

2. Institutional Entrance Examinations & Aptitude Tests

Because the volume of qualified high school graduates significantly exceeds available physical classroom seats and laboratory facilities, major institutions administer rigorous competitive entrance examinations:

  • University of Liberia (UL) Entrance Exam: Renowned across West Africa for its competitive rigor, the UL Entrance Examination tests candidates in two distinct sections: English Language & Verbal Aptitude (comprising reading comprehension, grammatical structure, vocabulary, and verbal reasoning) and Mathematics & Quantitative Aptitude (covering algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics, and logical deduction).
  • The UL Passing Threshold: To be granted direct undergraduate admission, candidates must achieve a passing score (traditionally 50% or higher) in both English and Mathematics sections independently. Candidates who pass only one section (e.g., pass English but score 45% in Math) may be placed in the UL Remedial Academic Programme (Freshman Bridging), while those failing both sections must re-sit the examination.
  • Cuttington University Placement & Aptitude Assessment: Evaluates verbal proficiency, quantitative analysis, and general analytical aptitude, combined with high school grade point average and WASSCE credit profiles.
  • Tubman University & Stella Maris Testing: Features customized STEM and technical aptitude assessments for students applying to civil, electrical, and mechanical engineering technologies.

How the Admission Composite Index Works

To rank candidates objectively for quota-restricted faculties, universities combine entrance examination scores and secondary school achievement into a Composite Admission Index (CAI) on a 100-point scale:

Mathematical Formula:

Composite Admission Index (CAI) = (Entrance Examination Score × 0.60) + (WASSCE Credit Score × 0.40)

Where:

  • Entrance Examination Score (0% to 100%): Represents the combined weighted average of the institution's English and Mathematics entrance test papers:

    • Entrance Average = (English Score + Mathematics Score) ÷ 2

  • WASSCE Credit Score (0 to 100): Quantifies secondary academic achievement based on the number and quality of WASSCE credit passes:
  • 9 Credits (All A1–C6): 100 Points
  • 8 Credits: 90 Points
  • 7 Credits: 80 Points
  • 6 Credits: 70 Points
  • 5 Credits (Statutory Baseline): 60 Points
  • 4 Credits: 45 Points (Conditional threshold)
  • Fewer than 4 Credits: 25 Points (Deficient)
  • Quality Bonus: Candidates with Grade A1 or B2 in compulsory English Language or Mathematics receive a 5-point merit bonus added to their WASSCE score.

Faculty Admission Tiers in Liberia

  • Tier 1: High Demand / Quota-Restricted (Composite Index: 75% to 100%):
  • Medical Sciences (A.M. Dogliotti College of Medicine, UL / Cuttington School of Health Sciences).
  • Engineering & Technology (UL College of Science & Tech, Tubman University, Stella Maris Mother Patern College).
  • Pharmacy & Nursing.
  • Typical requirement: Both entrance sections 60%+, 7+ WASSCE credits with distinctions/credits in Sciences and Mathematics.
  • Tier 2: Standard Professional Degree (Composite Index: 60% to 74%):
  • Business & Public Administration, Accounting, Economics, Management.
  • Computer Science, Information Technology, Environmental Science.
  • Typical requirement: Both entrance sections 50%+, 5+ WASSCE credits including English and Mathematics.
  • Tier 3: General Arts, Education & Agriculture (Composite Index: 50% to 59%):
  • Liberia College (Social Sciences, History, Sociology, Political Science).
  • William R. Tolbert, Jr. College of Agriculture & Forestry.
  • Typical requirement: Passing entrance exam score (50%+) and 5 WASSCE credits.
  • Tier 4: Remedial / Bridging Stream (Composite Index: 40% to 49%):
  • Candidates who passed one section of the entrance exam (45% to 49%) or have 4 credits. Placed in foundational non-degree coursework before regular matriculation.

Step-by-Step Worked Examples

Example 1: Applicant for Pre-Medicine at University of Liberia (UL)

Applicant Musu from Paynesville applies to the UL College of Health Sciences with strong secondary credentials:

  • Applicant Profile:
  • Target Institution: University of Liberia (UL)
  • Target Major: Pre-Medicine / Biology (College of Health Sciences)
  • WASSCE Credits: 8 Credits (including English A1, Mathematics B2, Biology A1, Chemistry B2, Physics B3)
  • English Language WASSCE Grade: A1 (Merit bonus applicable)
  • Mathematics WASSCE Grade: B2
  • UL Entrance Exam — English Section: 72%
  • UL Entrance Exam — Mathematics Section: 68%
  • Calculation Audit:
  • Entrance Average: (72 + 68) ÷ 2 = 70.0%.
  • WASSCE Credit Base: 8 Credits = 90 Points + 5 Quality Bonus (English A1) = 95 Points.
  • Composite Admission Index: (70.0 × 0.60) + (95 × 0.40) = 42.0 + 38.0 = 80.0%.
  • Prerequisite Audit: English Credit verified (A1), Math Credit verified (B2), 5+ Credits verified (8), Entrance English passed (72% >= 50%), Entrance Math passed (68% >= 50%).
  • Official Admission Verdict:
  • Status: High Chance / Direct Admission (Tier 1 Qualified).
  • Exceeds both the entrance exam 50% pass hurdle and the competitive 75% composite threshold for the College of Health Sciences. Highly recommended for scholarship consideration.

Example 2: Applicant for Business Administration at Cuttington University

Applicant Emmanuel from Kakata, Margibi County, applies to the Cuttington University College of Business and Public Administration:

  • Applicant Profile:
  • Target Institution: Cuttington University (CU)
  • Target Major: Accounting & Finance
  • WASSCE Credits: 6 Credits
  • English Language WASSCE Grade: C4
  • Mathematics WASSCE Grade: C5
  • Entrance Exam — Verbal / English: 58%
  • Entrance Exam — Quantitative / Math: 54%
  • Calculation Audit:
  • Entrance Average: (58 + 54) ÷ 2 = 56.0%.
  • WASSCE Credit Base: 6 Credits = 70 Points.
  • Composite Admission Index: (56.0 × 0.60) + (70 × 0.40) = 33.6 + 28.0 = 61.6%.
  • Prerequisite Audit: English Credit passed (C4), Math Credit passed (C5), 5+ Credits achieved (6), Both entrance exam sections above 50%.
  • Official Admission Verdict:
  • Status: Competitive / Regular Direct Admission (Tier 2 Qualified).
  • Satisfies all prerequisites for admission to Cuttington's undergraduate accounting curriculum.

Example 3: Applicant with Entrance Exam Section Deficiency

Applicant Gbessay from Buchanan, Grand Bassa County, applies to William V.S. Tubman University for Civil Engineering:

  • Applicant Profile:
  • Target Institution: William V.S. Tubman University
  • Target Major: Civil Engineering Technology
  • WASSCE Credits: 5 Credits
  • English Language WASSCE Grade: C5
  • Mathematics WASSCE Grade: C6
  • Entrance Exam — English Section: 62%
  • Entrance Exam — Mathematics Section: 42% (Deficient)
  • Calculation Audit:
  • Entrance Average: (62 + 42) ÷ 2 = 52.0%.
  • WASSCE Credit Base: 5 Credits = 60 Points.
  • Composite Admission Index: (52.0 × 0.60) + (60 × 0.40) = 31.2 + 24.0 = 55.2%.
  • Prerequisite Audit: High school credits meet the 5-credit statutory baseline, but Entrance Mathematics (42%) is below the mandatory 50% cutoff for engineering disciplines.
  • Official Admission Verdict:
  • Status: Conditional / Remedial Mathematics Placement.
  • The applicant cannot matriculate directly into 100-level calculus and engineering physics. Must enroll in Tubman University's pre-engineering mathematics bridging course for one semester or re-sit the quantitative entrance paper.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the passing mark for the University of Liberia (UL) entrance exam?

The University of Liberia traditionally establishes a 50% passing threshold for each individual section of its entrance examination: 50% in English Language & Verbal Aptitude and 50% in Mathematics & Quantitative Aptitude. Scoring 50% or above in both sections qualifies a candidate for direct freshman admission, provided they also present an official high school diploma and WASSCE credit credentials.

Can I be admitted to a Liberian university with four WASSCE credits?

Under National Commission on Higher Education guidelines, direct entry into a four-year bachelor's degree requires five credit passes (A1 to C6). Candidates with four credits may be offered conditional admission into an associate degree programme or pre-university preparatory classes, with the requirement to re-sit and clear their deficient fifth credit through the private WASSCE examination series before graduation.

What is the difference between regular admission and remedial admission at UL?

Regular admission grants immediate registration in credit-bearing 100-level collegiate courses. Remedial admission (often termed the Freshman Transition or Access Programme) is granted to candidates who achieved passing marks in one entrance subject but marginally missed the threshold in another. Remedial students take non-credit preparatory courses in English or Mathematics and must achieve a grade of 'C' or better to transition into regular freshman standing.

Do private universities in Liberia require entrance examinations?

Yes. Although the University of Liberia entrance examination is the largest single testing exercise in the country, private and faith-based institutions such as Cuttington University, Stella Maris Polytechnic, AMEU, and United Methodist University also administer institutional placement assessments, oral interviews, or diagnostic aptitude tests to determine curriculum placement.

How are WASSCE science subjects weighted for nursing and pharmacy in Liberia?

For healthcare disciplines, admission committees scrutinize grades in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and General Mathematics. To be competitive for the A.M. Dogliotti College of Medicine, Cuttington School of Health Sciences, or Mother Patern College of Health Sciences, candidates should present grades of B3 or higher in Biology and Chemistry, in addition to strong performance in the natural science section of the entrance test.

How many times can a student take the UL entrance examination?

There is no statutory limit on the number of times a candidate may sit the University of Liberia entrance examination. If you do not achieve passing scores on your initial attempt, you are permitted to register and sit for subsequent testing cycles announced by the UL Testing and Evaluation Center.

What is the difference between regular admission and remedial admission at UL?

Regular admission grants immediate registration in credit-bearing 100-level collegiate courses.
