What Every AfriWidget Tool Has in Common

100% Private

Calculations run in your browser. Finance and Education AI insights can use a secure Groq proxy on the deployed site; no calculator submission database or advertising profile is created.

Instant Results

Local results are instant. Where enabled, Finance and Education insight cards make a secure AI request through the deployed Groq proxy and fall back safely when it is unavailable.

Mobile-Friendly

Every tool is fully responsive and tested on low-bandwidth connections and older Android devices. No app download needed.

Always Free

Every tool on AfriWidget is free — no paywalls, no premium tiers, no registration. Supported by non-intrusive advertising so the tools stay free forever.

About Each Tool

BMI, BMR & TDEE Calculator

Enter your age, weight, height, and activity level to instantly receive your Body Mass Index, Basal Metabolic Rate, and Total Daily Energy Expenditure. After the result appears, the realtime AI assistant can explain what the numbers mean for your long-term health and what steps to take next. Results are grounded in the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and WHO classification standards.

Compound Interest Calculator

Model your investment or savings growth over any time horizon. Enter your principal, interest rate, compounding frequency, and optional monthly contributions to see a year-by-year breakdown of your growing balance. The realtime AI assistant can explain your real rate of return, inflation-adjusted purchasing power, and practical strategies to accelerate your wealth-building journey.

Cumulative GPA Calculator

Add as many courses as you need — with course name, letter grade, and credit hours — and calculate your weighted cumulative GPA on the standard 4.0 scale in seconds. The realtime academic AI assistant can explain where your GPA stands relative to Dean's List thresholds, scholarship minimums, and graduate programme requirements, and recommend targeted strategies for improvement based on your specific standing.

Mortgage Payment Calculator

Free mortgage payment calculator. Compute your monthly repayment, total interest, and amortisation schedule with AI-powered affordability insights that explain what your numbers mean in the context of your income and local property market conditions.

Rent Affordability Calculator

Free rent affordability estimator. Find out how much rent you can afford using the 30% rule, 50/30/20 budgeting, and debt-to-income analysis with AI insights. Understand the true ceiling for rent given your income, existing obligations, and savings goals — so you can make housing decisions with confidence.

Payment Processor Fee Calculator (PayPal, Stripe, Square)

Enter any sale amount and choose your platform — PayPal Goods & Services, Stripe Standard, Stripe International, Square Online, or Square In-Person — to instantly see the exact fee deducted and the net amount you receive. A reverse-fee mode lets you determine what to charge in order to land a specific target payout, making it effortless to price products and invoices so that processor fees never eat into your intended margin.

Profit Margin Calculator

Enter your product cost and selling price to instantly compute gross profit, gross margin percentage, and markup percentage — and understand the crucial difference between the two. The pricing scenarios table shows how adjusting your price by ±10–20% affects all three metrics, helping you stress-test your pricing strategy. The realtime business AI assistant can benchmark your margin against retail, software, food service, manufacturing, services, and freelance norms.

Hourly Rate to Annual Salary Converter

Enter your hourly rate, hours per day, days per week, weeks per year, and billable utilisation percentage to see your daily, weekly, monthly, and annual equivalent income — at the utilisation level you actually work at, not an unrealistic 100%. Enter a salary offer for a side-by-side comparison that tells you which arrangement truly pays more, factoring in the hidden cost of unpaid admin time and the value of employment benefits.

JSON Formatter, Validator & Minifier

Paste JSON to instantly format with syntax highlighting or validate and get precise error messages. No sign-up, no data upload to any server — all processing happens locally in your browser. The minifier strips whitespace for production use, while the formatter produces beautifully indented, human-readable output for debugging.

Secure Password Generator

Free secure password generator using the browser's Web Crypto API. Choose length, character sets, and generate up to 10 cryptographically random passwords instantly. Because generation uses the native Crypto API, passwords are not predictable from a seed and never leave your device — not even to AfriWidget's servers.

Word & Character Counter

Free online word and character counter. Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time. Keyword density analysis included. Ideal for writers, students, SEO professionals, and social media managers who need to stay within character limits or analyse text structure. No sign-up required.

Why AfriWidget Builds Tools This Way

AfriWidget was created with a single conviction: the gap between people who have access to personalised, expert guidance — nutritionists, financial advisors, academic counsellors — and those who do not, is one of the most consequential inequalities in the world today. That gap is not primarily about income. It is about access to reliable, context-aware information at the moment a person needs it.

A student in Lagos trying to understand whether their 2.8 GPA qualifies them for a postgraduate scholarship does not need a generic article about GPA ranges. They need an interactive tool that takes their specific courses, grades, and credit hours, computes their exact standing, and tells them — in plain language — what it means and what to do next. That is precisely what AfriWidget's GPA Calculator delivers.

Similarly, a young professional in Nairobi saving KSh 5,000 per month does not need a generic blog post about compound interest. They need a calculator that models their exact scenario — principal, rate, contribution, time horizon — and generates a year-by-year table alongside an insight card explaining their real inflation-adjusted return. Our Compound Interest Calculator does exactly that, down to the last detail.

The Realtime AI Assistant Difference

What separates AfriWidget from a basic online calculator is the optional realtime AI assistant that becomes available after a result is calculated. It requests a concise, contextual explanation through the secure AI proxy using the displayed result and relevant inputs, so a BMI of 27.4 can receive different guidance from a BMI of 32.1. The assistant explains what the number may mean, why it matters, and what practical next step or caution is appropriate.

Calculations still run in the browser using vanilla JavaScript. When realtime AI guidance is requested, the relevant calculation context is sent through the secure server-side proxy; the Groq key is never exposed in the browser. The calculator formulas remain calibrated against internationally recognised standards from organisations like the World Health Organisation (WHO), the American Council on Exercise, and standard academic grading conventions used across African and international universities.

Built for Africa, Useful Everywhere

AfriWidget is deliberately designed to work well on the devices and connections most common across Sub-Saharan Africa. The site is pure static HTML — no JavaScript framework to download, no React bundle to parse, no backend roundtrip before the page is usable. Every page is fully rendered before a single byte of JavaScript runs, which means even on a 2G connection, the content is immediately visible and readable.

This also serves the site's AdSense and SEO goals: because all content is present in the raw HTML source, search engine crawlers index every word immediately. There are no lazy-loaded components, no client-side routing frameworks that confuse bots, and no empty div containers waiting for JavaScript to populate them. What you see in the browser is what Google sees — making AfriWidget's tools discoverable by the people who need them most.

Coming Tools

Future additions planned for the Health category include a calorie deficit tracker, a hydration calculator, and a pregnancy due-date estimator. Every tool will be built to the same standards: privacy-first, no registration, mobile-optimised, AI-enhanced, and completely free. If you have a tool you would like to see added, use our Contact page to tell us — user requests directly shape AfriWidget's roadmap.
