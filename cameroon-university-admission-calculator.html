Cameroon University Admission Calculator (UB, UBa, Concours & Cut-offs)

Pursuing higher education in the Republic of Cameroon requires navigating a dual admission landscape: direct degree admissions into general university faculties and highly competitive entrance examinations (commonly known as Concours) for professional institutes and Grandes Écoles. Supervised by the Ministry of Higher Education (MINESUP), Cameroon's state university network spans iconic Anglophone institutions such as the University of Buea (UB - "A Place to Be") and the University of Bamenda (UBa - Bambili), alongside prestigious bilingual universities including the University of Yaoundé I, University of Yaoundé II (Soa), University of Douala, and University of Dschang.

This comprehensive calculator helps prospective students, parents, and secondary school leavers evaluate their admission chances. By analyzing Cameroon GCE Advanced Level points (or Baccalauréat equivalent), Ordinary Level subject credits (including English Language and Mathematics), faculty-specific prerequisite combinations, and optional competitive Concours exam scores, this tool generates instant eligibility assessments and strategic academic recommendations.

How to Use the Cameroon University Admission Calculator

The calculator provides two intuitive evaluation pathways designed to match how Cameroonian higher institutions evaluate candidate files:

Pathway 1: Direct Degree Admission (General Faculties)

  1. Select Target University: Choose from the University of Buea (UB), University of Bamenda (UBa), University of Yaoundé I, University of Yaoundé II, University of Douala, or University of Dschang.
  2. Select Target Faculty / Department:
  • Faculty of Science: Biochemistry, Microbiology, Computer Science, Chemistry, Physics, Geology.
  • Faculty of Arts & Humanities: English Literature, History, Linguistics, French, Philosophy.
  • Faculty of Laws & Political Science (FSJP): English Common Law, Political Science, International Relations.
  • Faculty of Economics & Management (FSEG): Economics, Accounting, Finance, Management.
  • Faculty of Education: Educational Psychology, Curriculum Studies, Educational Administration.
  1. Input Your Academic Qualifications:
  • Total GCE A-Level Points (ranging from 2 to 25 points).
  • Best 3 Subjects Points (ranging from 0 to 15 points).
  • Number of GCE O-Level Passes (minimum 4 or 5 required).
  • Confirm Grade C or better in O-Level English Language.
  • Confirm Grade C or better in O-Level Mathematics (essential for Sciences, Economics, and Tech).
  1. Specify Relevant Prerequisite A-Level Passes: Check off whether you passed the mandatory subject requirements for your desired course (e.g. Biology and Chemistry for Life Sciences, Pure Maths for Computer Science).

  3. Click "Calculate University Admission Chances": The engine assesses your overall composite score against historical institutional cut-offs and outputs your admission probability rating.

How the Cameroon University Admission Calculation Works

Higher education admissions in Cameroon are governed by statutory standards enacted by MINESUP and faculty academic boards:

1. General Degree Matriculation Thresholds (MINESUP Directive)

To gain direct admission into an undergraduate Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.), Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), or Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) program:

  • Minimum A-Level Requirement: At least two (2) principal passes (Grades A to E) obtained in one examination sitting.
  • Minimum O-Level Requirement: At least four or five (4–5) GCE Ordinary Level subject passes obtained at grades A, B, or C.
  • Language Prerequisite: At least Grade C in GCE O-Level English Language for Anglophone degree programs (or Grade C in French for Francophone programs).
  • Quantitative Prerequisite: At least Grade C in GCE O-Level Mathematics for all degree programs in the Faculty of Science, Faculty of Economics and Management, and College of Technology.

2. Faculty Cut-off Points & Competitiveness Tiers (Out of 15 Points)

General faculties evaluate applicants on the basis of their Best 3 relevant A-Level subjects:

  • Ultra-Competitive Tier (12 to 15 Points):
  • Computer Science / Software Engineering: Requires 12+ points with A or B in Pure Mathematics or Further Mathematics.
  • Law (LL.B. Common Law, University of Buea & Bamenda): Highly sought-after; typically requires 10 to 14 points with literature and history prerequisites.
  • Economics & Finance: Requires 9 to 13 points with high passes in Economics and Mathematics.
  • Competitive Tier (8 to 11 Points):
  • Biochemistry, Medical Laboratory Science (direct stream), Microbiology, Environmental Science: Requires 8 to 11 points with Biology and Chemistry passes.
  • Political Science & Public Administration: Requires 8 to 10 points.
  • Open Matriculation Tier (4 to 7 Points):
  • General Arts, History, Literature, Linguistics, Philosophy, Geography, Sociology, Educational Foundations: Candidates meeting the statutory minimum of two passes (4–6 points) in relevant subjects generally gain direct entry.

3. Professional Concours Composite Formula

For competitive professional schools (FHS, FET, NAHPI, ENS), final admission is determined by a composite score combining the candidate's academic background and their performance in the national competitive written examination:

  • Study Dossier Mark (Academic Background): Evaluates GCE O-Level and A-Level result slips. Standard weighting is 20% to 30% of the composite admission score.

    • Formula: Dossier Score = (A-Level Best 3 Points ÷ 15) × Dossier Percentage.

  • Written Concours Mark: Evaluates physical written examination papers covering specialty subjects (e.g. Biology and Chemistry for Medicine; Mathematics and Physics for Engineering; General Knowledge and Specialty for ENS). Standard weighting is 70% to 80%.

    • Formula: Written Exam Score = (Raw Exam Score out of 100) × Written Percentage.

  • Composite Score: Composite Score = Dossier Score + Written Exam Score.

    • A composite score exceeding 65% to 75% typically places an applicant within the quota of admitted candidates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between direct faculty admission and a Concours in Cameroon?

Direct faculty admission applies to general degree programs (Faculty of Science, Faculty of Arts, Faculty of Laws, Faculty of Economics) where candidates are selected purely based on their academic dossier (GCE A-Level points and O-Level grades). A Concours is a national competitive entrance examination required for specialized professional schools (Medical Schools like FHS/FMSB, Engineering like FET/NAHPI/ENSPY, Teacher Training like ENS/HTTTC, and Journalism like ASMAC/ESSTIC), where candidates must write an on-site competitive examination.

Can I get into the University of Buea (UB) with only two A-Level passes?

Yes. The statutory minimum requirement for matriculation into state universities in Cameroon is two (2) GCE Advanced Level principal passes (Grades A through E) in one sitting, alongside at least four or five GCE Ordinary Level passes including English Language. Candidates with two passes can enter programs in Arts, Humanities, Social Sciences, and selected sciences, although high-demand departments (such as Computer Science, Nursing, and Law) demand higher point tallies.

What A-Level subjects are required for Engineering at NAHPI or FET?

To qualify for the competitive entrance into the National Higher Polytechnic Institute (NAHPI) at the University of Bamenda or the Faculty of Engineering and Technology (FET) at the University of Buea, candidates must possess A-Level passes in Pure Mathematics with Mechanics (or Further Mathematics) and Physics. Chemistry and Computer Science are highly beneficial third subjects.

What happens if I pass A-Levels but do not have O-Level Mathematics?

If you lack a passing grade in GCE O-Level Mathematics, you cannot be admitted into programs within the Faculty of Science, Faculty of Economics and Management, College of Technology, or polytechnic engineering institutes. You are, however, still eligible for degree programs within the Faculty of Arts, Letters and Social Sciences, and the Faculty of Laws and Political Science, provided you possess O-Level English Language.

When do Cameroon university admission applications open?

Admissions into Cameroon state universities for the academic year starting in October typically open between June and August, immediately following the official release of Cameroon GCE results. Concours launch dates are decreed annually by ministerial orders published by the Ministry of Higher Education (MINESUP).

How is the academic dossier weighted in competitive Concours?

Under standard MINESUP circulars, candidate files in professional entrance concourses are assessed through a weighted model: the candidate's academic background (GCE A-Level points, O-Level marks, and age) carries a 20% to 30% weight, while the written examination papers carry the remaining 70% to 80% weight.
