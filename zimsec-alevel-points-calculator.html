Understanding the ZIMSEC A-Level 15-Point Grading System

In Zimbabwe, Form 6 students sit for the Zimbabwe School Examinations Council (ZIMSEC) General Certificate of Education (GCE) Advanced Level. A-Level results are the universal benchmark for undergraduate admission across Zimbabwean universities, including the University of Zimbabwe (UZ), National University of Science and Technology (NUST), Midlands State University (MSU), Chinhoyi University of Technology (CUT), Great Zimbabwe University (GZU), and Africa University (AU).

Most candidates sit for three principal subjects along with the subsidiary paper Communication Skills. The maximum score achievable for three principal subjects is 15 points (AAA), with each Grade A carrying 5 points.

This calculator helps you evaluate your exact total points, verifies your number of Principal Passes (Grades A to E), and matches your profile against competitive university cut-off tiers.

University Admissions Tiers in Zimbabwe

Cut-off points vary each academic year based on applicant volume and quota, but generally adhere to the following benchmarks:

Tier 1: Ultra-Competitive Programs (14 – 15 Points)

Straight A / A-A-B

Faculty of Medicine, Health Sciences, and Law at University of Zimbabwe (UZ) and NUST.

  • Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery (MBChB): 15 points (Chemistry, Biology, Math/Physics).
  • Bachelor of Laws (LLB): 14–15 points (Arts or Commercials combination).
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (Honours): 14–15 points (Chemistry and Biology mandatory).
  • BSc Aeronautical & Electrical Engineering: 13–15 points (Pure Mathematics and Physics mandatory).

Tier 2: Competitive STEM & Commercials (10 – 13 Points)

B-B-C / A-B-C / B-B-B

Engineering, Computer Science, Actuarial Science, Accounting, Economics, and Data Science.

  • Computer Science & Software Engineering: 11–14 points (Mathematics mandatory).
  • Civil, Mechanical, Mining, Chemical Engineering: 11–13 points (Mathematics and Physics).
  • Bachelor of Accountancy / Finance / Economics: 10–13 points (Mathematics / Accounting / Economics).
  • Actuarial Science & Financial Mathematics: 12–14 points (Mathematics at Grade A/B).

Tier 3: Standard Degree Entry (6 – 9 Points)

C-C-C / C-D-D / B-D-E

Humanities, Social Work, Media Studies, Agriculture, Natural Resource Management, Education.

  • BSc Agriculture & Agribusiness: 6–9 points (Biology/Chemistry/Agriculture).
  • BA Media, Communication & Journalism: 7–9 points (Literature/History/Divinity/Languages).
  • BSc Sociology, Psychology, Development Studies: 6–8 points.
  • Bachelor of Education (BEd Secondary): 5–8 points in two teaching subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if I write 4 A-Level subjects instead of 3? ▼

While standard Zimbabwean high school programs focus on 3 principal subjects (15 points max), some ambitious students write 4 subjects (20 points max). Most universities (such as UZ and NUST) calculate your admission score using your Best 3 relevant subjects for the specific degree, though having a 4th A-grade provides a strong competitive advantage in tie-breaking quotas.

Is Communication Skills compulsory for university admission in Zimbabwe? ▼

Communication Skills is written as a subsidiary subject alongside your A-Level principal subjects. While it does not carry points on the 15-point principal matrix, universities require candidates to have attempted or passed Communication Skills (or possess an O-Level English Language pass at Grade C or better) before conferring degree certificates.

Can I enter university with two Principal Passes (e.g., C and D = 5 points)? ▼

Yes! By statutory definition across Zimbabwe, two Principal Passes (Grade E or better) satisfies the minimum legal entry requirement for undergraduate study. You will be eligible for general humanities, education, agriculture, parallel/block-release degree programs, or Higher National Diplomas (HND) at polytechnics.
