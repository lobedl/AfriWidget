## Comprehensive Guide to Kenyan University GPA, CGPA & Degree Honours Classifications

In Kenya’s higher education system, academic performance at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels is regulated by the **Commission for University Education (CUE)** under the **Universities Act No. 42 of 2012**. Whether enrolled at public chartered universities—such as the **University of Nairobi (UoN)**, **Kenyatta University (KU)**, **Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT)**, **Moi University**, and **Egerton University**—or private chartered institutions like **Strathmore University**, **United States International University-Africa (USIU-Africa)**, **Daystar University**, and **Mount Kenya University (MKU)**, understanding how your semester marks translate into Grade Point Averages (GPA), Cumulative Grade Point Averages (CGPA), or Cumulative Weighted Averages (CWA) is vital for tracking academic standing and graduation honours.

## Kenyan University Grading Systems & Scale Standards

Kenyan universities employ two primary grading frameworks based on their institutional statutes and academic charters:

### 1. Standard Public University Percentage Scale (UoN, KU, JKUAT, Moi, Egerton, Maseno)

Most Kenyan public universities use a 5-tier percentage-to-letter grading system where semester exams (typically 70%) combine with Continuous Assessment Tests / CATs (typically 30%):

- **Grade A (70% to 100%)**: Excellent / Distinction (4.00 Grade Points). Qualifies towards First Class Honours standing.
- **Grade B (60% to 69%)**: Good / Credit (3.00 Grade Points). Qualifies towards Second Class Honours (Upper Division).
- **Grade C (50% to 59%)**: Satisfactory / Average (2.00 Grade Points). Qualifies towards Second Class Honours (Lower Division).
- **Grade D (40% to 49%)**: Pass (1.00 Grade Point). The minimum statutory pass mark for degree units in Kenyan public universities.
- **Grade E or F (0% to 39%)**: Fail (0.00 Grade Points). Requires a supplementary examination or retaking the course unit.

### 2. 12-Tier Letter Grade & 4.0 GPA Scale (Strathmore, USIU-Africa, Daystar, CUEA, MKU)

Private universities and international-model institutions in Kenya utilize a granular 4.0 grade point scale:

- **Grade A (90% to 100%)**: 4.00 Grade Points (Superior)
- **Grade A- (85% to 89%)**: 3.70 Grade Points (Excellent)
- **Grade B+ (80% to 84%)**: 3.30 Grade Points (Very Good)
- **Grade B (75% to 79%)**: 3.00 Grade Points (Good)
- **Grade B- (70% to 74%)**: 2.70 Grade Points (Above Average)
- **Grade C+ (65% to 69%)**: 2.30 Grade Points (Competent)
- **Grade C (60% to 64%)**: 2.00 Grade Points (Average)
- **Grade C- (55% to 59%)**: 1.70 Grade Points (Below Average)
- **Grade D+ (50% to 54%)**: 1.30 Grade Points (Marginal Pass)
- **Grade D (45% to 49%)**: 1.00 Grade Point (Minimal Pass)
- **Grade F (0% to 44%)**: 0.00 Grade Points (Fail)

## The Mathematical Formulas for University GPA and Cumulative CGPA

Academic progression in Kenyan universities is determined by credit-weighted calculations:

### 1. Semester Quality Points:
- **Quality Points (QP)** for a Course = Course Grade Points × Course Credit Hours (or Units)
  - Example: A student earning an **A (4.0 pts)** in a **3-credit unit** earns: 4.0 × 3 = **12.0 Quality Points**.
  - A student earning a **B (3.0 pts)** in a **4-credit unit** earns: 3.0 × 4 = **12.0 Quality Points**.

### 2. Semester Grade Point Average (GPA):
- **Semester GPA** = Total Semester Quality Points ÷ Total Registered Semester Credit Units

### 3. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):
- **Cumulative CGPA** = (Prior Cumulative Quality Points + Current Semester Quality Points) ÷ (Prior Total Credit Units + Current Semester Credit Units)

### 4. Weighted Average Mark (WAM) in Percentage Systems:
- **WAM (%)** = Sum of (Course Numeric Mark × Course Credit Units) ÷ Total Registered Credit Units

## Official Kenyan Degree Classification & Honours Thresholds

Upon completion of all prescribed undergraduate credit requirements (typically 120 to 160 credit units over 4 to 6 academic years), Kenyan universities award Bachelor's degrees according to standardized classifications:

### For 4.0 GPA-Based Degree Systems:
- **First Class Honours**: Cumulative CGPA of **3.70 to 4.00** (or Cumulative Average of 70.0% and above). Represents outstanding academic scholarship across all undergraduate years.
- **Second Class Honours (Upper Division)**: Cumulative CGPA of **3.00 to 3.69** (or Cumulative Average of 60.0% to 69.9%). The standard competitive qualification for corporate graduate trainee programmes and direct Master's degree entry.
- **Second Class Honours (Lower Division)**: Cumulative CGPA of **2.00 to 2.99** (or Cumulative Average of 50.0% to 59.9%). Qualifies for Bachelor's degree graduation and general employment.
- **Pass Degree**: Cumulative CGPA of **1.00 to 1.99** (or Cumulative Average of 40.0% to 49.9%). Satisfies degree completion requirements without honours designation.
- **Fail / Incomplete**: Cumulative CGPA below **1.00** (or average below 40.0%). Requires repeating failed units or academic bridging.

## Worked Kenyan University GPA Calculation Examples

### Example 1: Bachelor of Science (Computer Science) Semester at University of Nairobi (UoN)

A second-year student completes 6 units with the following marks:

- **CSC 201: Data Structures & Algorithms** (3 Credits): Mark 74% &rarr; Grade A (4.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 4.0 × 3 = **12.0 QPs**
- **CSC 203: Computer Networks** (3 Credits): Mark 68% &rarr; Grade B (3.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 3.0 × 3 = **9.0 QPs**
- **CSC 205: Database Systems** (3 Credits): Mark 71% &rarr; Grade A (4.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 4.0 × 3 = **12.0 QPs**
- **MAT 211: Discrete Mathematics** (3 Credits): Mark 62% &rarr; Grade B (3.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 3.0 × 3 = **9.0 QPs**
- **CSC 207: Operating Systems** (4 Credits): Mark 78% &rarr; Grade A (4.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 4.0 × 4 = **16.0 QPs**
- **CCS 001: Communication Skills** (2 Credits): Mark 82% &rarr; Grade A (4.0 pts) &rarr; Quality Points = 4.0 × 2 = **8.0 QPs**

**Semester Computation**:
- **Total Credit Units** = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 4 + 2 = **18 Credits**
- **Total Quality Points** = 12.0 + 9.0 + 12.0 + 9.0 + 16.0 + 8.0 = **66.0 Quality Points**
- **Semester GPA** = 66.0 ÷ 18 = **3.67 / 4.00**
- **Weighted Average Mark (WAM)** = [(74×3) + (68×3) + (71×3) + (62×3) + (78×4) + (82×2)] ÷ 18 = (222 + 204 + 213 + 186 + 312 + 164) ÷ 18 = 1301 ÷ 18 = **72.28%**
- **Classification Standing**: **First Class Honours** (70%+ WAM or High Second Class Upper).

### Example 2: Multi-Year Cumulative CGPA Projection (Strathmore University B.Com Student)

A student enters their final year with the following academic record:
- **Prior Cumulative Record**: Completed 90 Credit Units with a Cumulative GPA of **3.45 / 4.00** (Total Prior Quality Points = 3.45 × 90 = **310.5 QPs**).
- **Current Final Year Semester 1**: Completed 15 Credit Units with a Semester GPA of **3.80 / 4.00** (Current Semester Quality Points = 3.80 × 15 = **57.0 QPs**).

**Cumulative CGPA Calculation**:
- **Combined Total Credits** = 90 + 15 = **105 Credit Units**
- **Combined Total Quality Points** = 310.5 + 57.0 = **367.5 Quality Points**
- **Updated Cumulative CGPA** = 367.5 ÷ 105 = **3.50 / 4.00**
- **Degree Classification Status**: **Second Class Honours (Upper Division)**. (Needs a 3.70 CGPA across remaining 15 credits to reach First Class Honours).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the minimum pass mark in Kenyan public universities?
In Kenyan public universities (such as UoN, KU, JKUAT, and Moi), the statutory pass mark for undergraduate degree courses is **40% (Grade D)**. Any mark below 40% (0% to 39%) is classified as Grade E or F (Fail). In postgraduate programmes (Master's and PhD), the statutory pass mark is typically **50% (Grade C)**.

### How do supplementary examinations affect your GPA and transcript?
If a student scores between 30% and 39% in a unit, university regulations allow them to sit a **Supplementary Examination**. When a supplementary exam is passed, university statutes cap the recorded grade at **Grade D (40% / 1.00 Grade Point)** on the official transcript, regardless of how high the student scored in the retake.

### What is the difference between GPA and CWA in Kenyan tertiary institutions?
**GPA (Grade Point Average)** converts letter grades into point values (0.0 to 4.0) before weighting them by credit units. **CWA (Cumulative Weighted Average)** calculates the exact percentage score (0% to 100%) for each unit, multiplies by credit hours, and divides by total credit hours without intermediate point rounding.

### What is academic probation in Kenyan universities?
Under Commission for University Education (CUE) and university Senate guidelines, a student whose semester GPA or cumulative CGPA falls below **2.00 (or below 40% average)** is placed on **Academic Probation** for one academic semester. If performance does not improve after probation, the student may be required to repeat the academic year or face academic discontinuation.

### Do all four undergraduate years count equally toward final degree classification?
Yes, in most Kenyan universities, all approved credit units taken from Year 1 to Year 4 contribute to the final Cumulative CGPA and degree honours calculation. However, some university faculties apply progressive weighting where Year 3 and Year 4 carry higher percentage weights (e.g., 20% Year 1 & 2, 80% Year 3 & 4).
