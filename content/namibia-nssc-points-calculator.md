# Namibia NSSC Points Calculator (DNEA NSSCO & NSSCAS Admission Points Guide)

Calculate your official Namibian secondary school qualification points for the Namibia Senior Secondary Certificate Ordinary (NSSCO) and Namibia Senior Secondary Certificate Advanced Subsidiary (NSSCAS) levels according to the statutory standards established by the Directorate of National Examinations and Assessment (DNEA) and the Ministry of Education, Arts and Culture (MoEAC). Determine your Best 5 and Best 6 subject aggregates, verify your government funding eligibility under the Namibia Students Financial Assistance Fund (NSFAF), and evaluate your admission readiness for undergraduate programs at the University of Namibia (UNAM) and the Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST).

---

## How to Use the Namibia NSSC Points Calculator

Navigating the transition from secondary education to higher learning in Namibia requires an accurate calculation of admission points across different curriculum levels. Following the national curriculum transformation, candidates may present results from Grade 11 (NSSCO Ordinary Level) or Grade 12 (NSSCAS Advanced Subsidiary Level), or a combined transcript of both qualifications.

### Step 1: Enter Your Subjects and Select Qualification Levels
- For each subject on your official DNEA statement of results, select the qualification level from the dropdown menu:
  - **NSSCO (Ordinary Level)**: Standard Grade 11 secondary curriculum assessed on an 8-point alphanumeric scale from A* down to G.
  - **NSSCAS (Advanced Subsidiary Level)**: Rigorous Grade 12 pre-university curriculum assessed on a 10-point lowercase scale from a down to e.
- Enter your subject titles, ensuring your core language requirement (**English First Language**, **English Second Language**, or **English as an Additional Language**) is included in Subject Row 1.

### Step 2: Input Your Official Letter Grades
- Select your certified letter grade for each course:
  - For **NSSCAS** subjects, choose from grades **a**, **b**, **c**, **d**, or **e**. Any grade below e is classified as Ungraded (U) and carries 0 points.
  - For **NSSCO** subjects, choose from grades **A\***, **A**, **B**, **C**, **D**, **E**, **F**, or **G**. An Ungraded (U) result carries 0 points.
- If you took more than the standard 5 or 6 subjects, click the "Add Subject" button to evaluate all completed courses. The calculator automatically identifies and aggregates your highest-scoring subjects while enforcing compulsory language requirements.

### Step 3: Click Calculate to Audit Your Results
- Click the **"Calculate NSSC Points"** button to execute the calculation.
- Review your detailed performance audit:
  - **Best 5 Subjects Points Total**: The primary benchmark used for general university degree and diploma entry.
  - **Best 6 Subjects Points Total**: Used for specialized multi-disciplinary programs and national merit ranking.
  - **NSFAF Financial Assistance Status**: Verifies whether you have satisfied the statutory requirement of 25+ points in Best 5 with a minimum Grade C in English for government loan funding.
  - **Institutional Eligibility Overview**: Compares your aggregate against baseline admission thresholds at UNAM, NUST, and IUM.

## How the Namibian NSSC Points Calculation Works

The Ministry of Education, Arts and Culture (MoEAC), in conjunction with the National Qualifications Authority (NQA) and the Directorate of National Examinations and Assessment (DNEA), governs the statutory point allocation scales for Namibian secondary exit examinations.

### Official DNEA Point Conversion Scale

The point value assigned to each subject depends directly on whether the subject was taken at Ordinary Level (NSSCO) or Advanced Subsidiary Level (NSSCAS):

- **NSSCAS Advanced Subsidiary Level (Grade 12)**:
  - **Grade a**: **10 Points** (80% – 100% / Outstanding Mastery)
  - **Grade b**: **9 Points** (70% – 79% / Excellent Achievement)
  - **Grade c**: **8 Points** (60% – 69% / Good Competence)
  - **Grade d**: **7 Points** (50% – 59% / Satisfactory Competence)
  - **Grade e**: **6 Points** (40% – 49% / Minimum Passing Level)
  - **Grade U**: **0 Points** (Below 40% / Ungraded)

- **NSSCO Ordinary Level (Grade 11)**:
  - **Grade A\***: **8 Points** (90% – 100% / Exceptional Distinction)
  - **Grade A**: **7 Points** (80% – 89% / Distinction)
  - **Grade B**: **6 Points** (70% – 79% / Very Good Achievement)
  - **Grade C**: **5 Points** (60% – 69% / Solid Pass — Tertiary Standard Baseline)
  - **Grade D**: **4 Points** (50% – 59% / Moderate Achievement)
  - **Grade E**: **3 Points** (40% – 49% / Basic Passing Grade)
  - **Grade F**: **2 Points** (30% – 39% / Elementary Achievement)
  - **Grade G**: **1 Point** (20% – 29% / Minimum Recorded Achievement)
  - **Grade U**: **0 Points** (Below 20% / Ungraded)

### The Best 5 and Best 6 Aggregation Formula

Universities and funding bodies in Namibia rank applicants using strict scoring rules:

1. **Mandatory English Requirement**: Under statutory admission criteria across all public universities in Namibia, **English** (First or Second Language) must be included in your calculated aggregate. If your English grade is among your lowest marks, it is still retained within the calculation, while the remaining 4 (or 5) subjects are selected from your highest-performing courses.
2. **Mathematical Point Summation**:
   - **Best 5 Total** = English Point Value + Sum of Top 4 Non-English Subject Point Values
   - **Best 6 Total** = English Point Value + Sum of Top 5 Non-English Subject Point Values
3. **Maximum Attainable Scores**:
   - On a pure **NSSCAS** transcript (5 subjects at Grade a), the maximum Best 5 score is **50 Points**.
   - On a pure **NSSCO** transcript (5 subjects at Grade A*), the maximum Best 5 score is **40 Points**.
   - Mixed transcripts blend the scales based on the exact combination of subjects sat by the learner.

### NSFAF Government Financial Sponsorship Thresholds

The Namibia Students Financial Assistance Fund (NSFAF) provides student loans and bursaries to eligible Namibian citizens entering accredited public and private tertiary institutions.

- **Undergraduate Degree Sponsorship**:
  - Minimum of **25 Points** in the Best 5 subjects.
  - Minimum of **Grade C (NSSCO)** or **Grade d (NSSCAS)** in English.
  - Full admission letter to an NQA-accredited qualification at NQF Level 7 or above.
- **Undergraduate Diploma Sponsorship**:
  - Minimum of **22 Points** in the Best 5 subjects with at least Grade D in English.
- **TVET and Certificate Sponsorship**:
  - Minimum of **18 to 20 Points** with demonstrable technical subject aptitude.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I combine Grade 11 NSSCO and Grade 12 NSSCAS subjects in the same points calculation?
Yes. Namibian tertiary institutions (UNAM, NUST, IUM) and NSFAF accept a blended score combining your best results from both your Grade 11 NSSCO and Grade 12 NSSCAS examination sittings. The calculator applies the 10-point scale to your NSSCAS subjects and the 8-point scale to your NSSCO subjects to produce your unified Best 5 or Best 6 aggregate.

### Why is English compulsory in the Best 5 subjects calculation?
English is the sole official medium of instruction across all higher education institutions in Namibia. By statutory mandate from the Ministry of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, candidates must demonstrate English communicative proficiency. Consequently, English is automatically included in your Best 5 score, even if a non-English elective earned a higher individual point value.

### What is the difference between NSSCO and NSSCAS in points weighting?
NSSCAS (Advanced Subsidiary Level) covers an intensive pre-university curriculum equivalent to Cambridge International AS-Level and carries higher point values: Grade a is worth 10 points, down to Grade e worth 6 points. NSSCO (Ordinary Level) is equivalent to Cambridge IGCSE, where Grade A* is worth 8 points and Grade G is worth 1 point. A Grade e in NSSCAS (6 points) is equivalent in point weighting to a Grade B in NSSCO.

### What minimum score do I need for NSFAF government financial assistance?
For undergraduate bachelor's degree programs, the Namibia Students Financial Assistance Fund (NSFAF) requires a minimum of 25 points in your Best 5 subjects, along with a minimum Grade C in English at NSSCO level (or Grade d at NSSCAS level). For vocational and diploma programs, the threshold is typically 20 to 22 points.

### What happens if I receive an Ungraded (U) mark in one of my subjects?
An Ungraded (U) mark indicates that the candidate did not achieve the minimum learning objectives required for a recorded grade (below 40% on NSSCAS or below 20% on NSSCO). A grade of U carries 0 points. While it cannot be used toward your Best 5 aggregate, you can still qualify for admission if your other 5 passed subjects meet the institutional point requirements.

### Can I gain admission to university with only Grade 11 NSSCO results?
Yes. Students who achieve 25 or more points across their Best 5 NSSCO subjects (with at least Grade C in English) can qualify for direct admission into selected undergraduate degree programs at UNAM, NUST, and IUM, or enter specialized diploma and bridging pathways such as the InSTEM foundation program.
