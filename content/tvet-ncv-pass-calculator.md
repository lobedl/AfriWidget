## About the TVET NC(V) Pass & Aggregate Calculator

The National Certificate (Vocational), or NC(V), is a premier vocational qualification offered across South Africa's 50 public Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) colleges and accredited private colleges. Structured across NQF Levels 2, 3, and 4, the NC(V) curriculum provides students with direct practical workplace skills alongside foundational academic theory.

Unlike traditional schooling, obtaining an NC(V) certificate requires satisfying strict multi-tier assessment criteria. Each subject comprises an Internal Continuous Assessment (ICASS) component, an Integrated Summative Assessment Task (ISAT) for practical subjects, and a national external examination conducted by the DHET and certified by Umalusi.

This calculator computes your subject results and overall certificate pass status instantly in your browser, verifying all fundamental and vocational sub-minimums without sending data to any external server.

## Official DHET & Umalusi NC(V) Pass Criteria

To pass an NC(V) Level (Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4) and qualify for the national certificate, a candidate must register for and pass seven subjects (three fundamental subjects and four vocational subjects):

### 1. Fundamental Subjects (3 Subjects)
- **Official Language (e.g., English First Additional Language)**: Minimum **40%** final combined mark.
- **Life Orientation**: Minimum **40%** final combined mark.
- **Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy**: Minimum **30%** final combined mark.

*Weighting for Fundamental Subjects*: ICASS (Internal mark) counts **50%**, and the National Final Examination counts **50%**.

### 2. Vocational Subjects (4 Subjects)
- **All 4 Vocational Subjects (Core & Elective)**: Minimum **50%** final combined mark in EACH subject.

*Weighting for Vocational Subjects*:
- **ICASS (Internal Continuous Assessment)**: **25%**
- **ISAT (Integrated Summative Assessment Task / Practical)**: **25%**
- **National External Examination**: **50%**

### 3. Sub-Minimum Examination Rules
- Students must obtain at least the prescribed sub-minimum examination mark in the national external exam to be credited.
- A failure in any single vocational subject means the full qualification certificate is withheld until the subject is passed in a supplementary or subsequent examination cycle.

## Mathematical Calculation Formulas

All calculations follow the deterministic official formulas specified by the DHET:

### Fundamental Subject Final Mark:
- **Final Fundamental Mark** = (ICASS Mark × 0.50) + (National Exam Mark × 0.50)

### Vocational Subject Final Mark:
- **Final Vocational Mark** = (ICASS Mark × 0.25) + (ISAT Practical Mark × 0.25) + (National Exam Mark × 0.50)

### Overall Vocational Average:
- **Vocational Aggregate** = (Vocational 1 + Vocational 2 + Vocational 3 + Vocational 4) ÷ 4

## Step-by-Step Worked Technical Examples

### Example 1: Successful NC(V) Level 4 Pass
Consider a student enrolled in NC(V) Level 4 Civil Engineering & Building Construction:

1. **English FAL**: ICASS = 56%, Exam = 48% -> Final = (56 × 0.50) + (48 × 0.50) = 28.0 + 24.0 = **52.0%** (PASS >= 40%)
2. **Life Orientation**: ICASS = 64%, Exam = 58% -> Final = (64 × 0.50) + (58 × 0.50) = 32.0 + 29.0 = **61.0%** (PASS >= 40%)
3. **Mathematics**: ICASS = 42%, Exam = 36% -> Final = (42 × 0.50) + (36 × 0.50) = 21.0 + 18.0 = **39.0%** (PASS >= 30%)
4. **Vocational 1 (Construction Planning)**: ICASS = 60%, ISAT = 70%, Exam = 52% -> Final = (60 × 0.25) + (70 × 0.25) + (52 × 0.50) = 15.0 + 17.5 + 26.0 = **58.5%** (PASS >= 50%)
5. **Vocational 2 (Construction Supervision)**: ICASS = 55%, ISAT = 65%, Exam = 50% -> Final = (55 × 0.25) + (65 × 0.25) + (50 × 0.50) = 13.75 + 16.25 + 25.0 = **55.0%** (PASS >= 50%)
6. **Vocational 3 (Materials)**: ICASS = 62%, ISAT = 68%, Exam = 54% -> Final = (62 × 0.25) + (68 × 0.25) + (54 × 0.50) = 15.5 + 17.0 + 27.0 = **59.5%** (PASS >= 50%)
7. **Vocational 4 (Carpentry & Roof Work)**: ICASS = 58%, ISAT = 74%, Exam = 48% -> Final = (58 × 0.25) + (74 × 0.25) + (48 × 0.50) = 14.5 + 18.5 + 24.0 = **57.0%** (PASS >= 50%)

**Outcome**: Full NC(V) Level 4 Certificate Awarded.

### Example 2: Subject Retention & Supplementary Need
A student in NC(V) Tourism scores 46% in Vocational Subject 3 (below 50%). Although the student passed all 3 fundamental subjects and the other 3 vocational subjects, the full NC(V) certificate cannot be issued until Vocational Subject 3 is re-written and passed at or above 50%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between NC(V) and NATED (Report 191)?
NC(V) is a 3-year vocational programme (Levels 2–4) combining general academic education with practical workplace skills, ideal for students who completed Grade 9 or higher. NATED (Report 191 N1–N6) consists of theoretical trimester/semester courses requiring Matric (Grade 12) or equivalent for N4–N6.

### Why do vocational subjects require 50% while maths requires only 30%?
The DHET and Umalusi framework mandates higher competence thresholds (50%) in core vocational specialisations to ensure industry readiness and technical safety, while general foundational numeracy retains standard national school benchmarks (30%).

### What happens if I fail one vocational subject in NC(V)?
Under Umalusi guidelines, subject credits are retained permanently. You do not need to repeat the entire year; you only register to rewrite the failed vocational subject in the mid-year supplementary or end-of-year examination session.

### Can I study at a university with an NC(V) Level 4 qualification?
Yes. An NC(V) Level 4 certificate with appropriate fundamental passes (minimum 50% in English, Mathematics, and 60%+ in vocational electives) qualifies for Higher Certificate, Diploma, or Bachelor degree admission in related fields at South African universities and Universities of Technology (UoTs).
