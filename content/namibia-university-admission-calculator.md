## How to Use the Namibia University Admission Calculator

Admission into tertiary programmes in Namibia involves a dual-layered evaluation: meeting the institution's overall points threshold (calculated on the DNEA NSSCO/NSSCAS scales) and fulfilling non-negotiable faculty-specific subject requirements (such as minimum letter grades in English, Mathematics, and Physical Sciences).

### Step 1: Select Your Target University and Field of Study
- Choose your preferred Namibian university:
  - **University of Namibia (UNAM)**: The national comprehensive university renowned for Health Sciences (School of Medicine), Law (Hage Geingob Campus / Main Campus), Agriculture, Education, and Natural Sciences.
  - **Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST)**: The premier STEM and technological university specializing in Engineering, Computing & Informatics, Architecture, Spatial Sciences, and Applied Sciences.
  - **International University of Management (IUM)**: The accredited private university known for Business Administration, Information Technology, Tourism, and Education qualifications.
- Select your target faculty or field (e.g., Medicine & Surgery, Engineering, Computer Science, Law, Business Administration, General Sciences).

### Step 2: Input Your Admission Points and Core Subject Grades
- **Best 5 Admission Points**: Enter your calculated Best 5 points total (from 20 to 50 points) derived from your DNEA NSSCO/NSSCAS statement of results.
- **English Grade**: Select your official letter grade in English (First or Second Language) and specify whether the grade was achieved at NSSCO (A* to G) or NSSCAS (a to e) level.
- **Mathematics Grade**: Input your grade in Mathematics (or Technical Mathematics).
- **Physical Science / Chemistry / Biology Grade**: Input your science marks if applying to STEM, Medical, Agricultural, or Engineering programmes.

### Step 3: Run the Evaluation and Review Your Admission Audit
- Click the **"Check Admission Eligibility"** button.
- Review your comprehensive institutional report:
  - **Overall Eligibility Classification**: Directly indicates whether you qualify for *Direct Full Admission*, *Competitive Review / Merit Pool*, *Foundation / InSTEM Bridging Pathway*, or *Alternative Diploma / TVET Progression*.
  - **Faculty Prerequisite Audit**: Verifies line-by-line whether your individual subject grades satisfy statutory departmental cut-offs.
  - **Campus-Specific & Alternative Options**: Highlights bridging entry requirements (e.g., NUST InSTEM for aspiring engineers scoring 22–24 points).

## How Namibian University Admission Evaluation Works

Undergraduate selection across Namibian universities is governed by academic regulations established under the Higher Education Act, individual university senates, and professional statutory councils (such as the Medical and Dental Council of Namibia and the Engineering Council of Namibia).

### 1. General Institutional Admission Thresholds

- **University of Namibia (UNAM)**:
  - **Standard Degree Benchmark**: A minimum of **25 Points** in the Best 5 subjects on the UNAM evaluation scale, with at least a **Grade C** in English at NSSCO level (or Grade d at NSSCAS level).
  - **Diploma Programmes**: A minimum of **22 Points** in the Best 5 subjects with at least a Grade D in English.
  - **Certificate Programmes**: A minimum of **18 to 20 Points** with demonstrable foundational proficiency.
- **Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST)**:
  - **Standard Degree Benchmark**: A minimum of **25 Points** in the Best 5 subjects, with a minimum **Grade C** in English (NSSCO) or **Grade d** (NSSCAS).
  - **STEM Specific Rule**: Most bachelor's degrees in Computing, Applied Sciences, and Engineering mandate at least a **Grade C in Mathematics** and **Grade C in Physical Science** (or NSSCAS d).
- **International University of Management (IUM)**:
  - **Standard Degree Benchmark**: A minimum of **25 Points** in the Best 5 subjects with Grade C in English, or progression from an accredited NQA Level 5 Certificate or Level 6 Diploma.

### 2. High-Demand Professional Faculty Cut-Offs & Prerequisites

Competitive professional degrees enforce strict subject prerequisites that supersede general point minimums:

- **Medicine & Surgery (UNAM School of Medicine - MBChB)**:
  - Minimum Points: **35+ Points** in Best 5 subjects (often requiring 40+ points in practice due to competitive quotas).
  - Prerequisites: Minimum **Grade a or b in Biology (NSSCAS)**, **Grade b in Chemistry (NSSCAS)**, **Grade b in Mathematics (NSSCAS)**, and **Grade B in English (NSSCO)** or Grade c in NSSCAS English.
  - Selection: Shortlisted candidates undergo a formal admissions interview and psychometric aptitude test.
- **Bachelor of Engineering (BEng Honours - NUST & UNAM)**:
  - Minimum Points: **30 to 35+ Points** in Best 5 subjects.
  - Prerequisites: Minimum **Grade c in Mathematics (NSSCAS)** and **Grade c in Physical Science / Physics (NSSCAS)**, or Grade A in NSSCO Mathematics and Physical Science.
- **Bachelor of Laws (LLB - UNAM Faculty of Law)**:
  - Minimum Points: **30 Points** in Best 5 subjects.
  - Prerequisites: Minimum **Grade B in English (NSSCO)** or **Grade c in English (NSSCAS)**.
- **Bachelor of Computer Science / Software Engineering (NUST & UNAM)**:
  - Minimum Points: **25 to 28 Points**.
  - Prerequisites: Minimum **Grade C in NSSCO Mathematics** or Grade d in NSSCAS Mathematics.

### 3. Alternative Foundation & Bridging Pathways (InSTEM)

Students who do not immediately qualify for direct degree admission can access recognized bridging programmes:

- **NUST InSTEM (Introduction to Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics)**:
  - Target: Aspiring engineering, computing, and applied science students who obtained between **22 and 24 points** with at least a Grade D in Mathematics and Physical Science.
  - Structure: A one-year intensive foundation curriculum covering pre-calculus, foundational physics, chemistry, and academic English. Successful completion with an average of 60%+ grants direct articulation into year 1 of NUST engineering and science bachelor's degrees.
- **UNAM Foundation & Mature Age Entry**:
  - Mature Age Entry Scheme: Open to candidates aged 25 or older who do not meet secondary points criteria, requiring passing written examinations in English, General Knowledge, and Faculty Numerical Aptitude with 60%+.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the minimum points requirement for general admission to UNAM and NUST?
Both the University of Namibia (UNAM) and the Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST) enforce a baseline requirement of **25 Points** in your Best 5 subjects, along with a minimum **Grade C** in English (at NSSCO level) or **Grade d** (at NSSCAS level). Certain diplomas allow entry with 22 points and Grade D in English.

### Can I be admitted to UNAM Law (LLB) if I have a Grade C in English?
Generally, no. The UNAM Faculty of Law requires a minimum of **30 Points** and a minimum of **Grade B** in English at NSSCO level (or Grade c at NSSCAS level). Candidates with a Grade C in English may apply for a Diploma in Law or complete a general Bachelor of Arts degree before applying for post-graduate LLB articulation.

### How does the NUST InSTEM foundation programme work?
The Introduction to Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (InSTEM) is a two-semester bridging curriculum designed for students who score 22 to 24 points in their Best 5 subjects with Grade D in Mathematics and Physical Science. It equips students with advanced foundational mathematics, physics, and computing concepts, enabling direct transition into full NUST degree programmes upon successful completion.

### What is the Mature Age Entry Scheme in Namibia?
Under the Mature Age Entry Scheme, Namibian citizens who are at least 25 years of age and do not possess standard secondary exit points can gain university admission. Applicants must write and pass standardized university examinations assessing English language proficiency, numerical skills, and specialized faculty aptitude with a composite score of 60% or higher.

### Are admission point requirements different for regional UNAM campuses?
The statutory admission point thresholds set by the UNAM Senate (e.g., 25 points for degrees, 22 points for diplomas) apply uniformly across all campuses, including Main Campus (Windhoek), Hifikepunye Pohamba Campus (Ongwediva), Rundu Campus, Katima Mulilo Campus, and Southern Campus (Keetmanshoop). However, competition for specific programmes may vary based on applicant volume and quota allocations.

### Can I apply for university admission with only Grade 11 NSSCO results?
Yes. Candidates with strong Grade 11 NSSCO results (25+ points with Grade C in English and relevant subject prerequisites) can be admitted directly into select degree and diploma programmes. However, highly competitive fields such as Medicine (MBChB) and certain Engineering streams specifically require Grade 12 NSSCAS advanced qualifications.
