Why Health Literacy Matters

Health literacy is the foundation of personal wellbeing. Across Africa and the developing world, millions of people face decisions about diet, exercise, and chronic disease management without the benefit of a regular physician or nutritionist. AfriWidget's Health & Wellness toolkit was built specifically to close this knowledge gap — providing clinically grounded calculations that were previously available only to those who could afford professional consultations. When you understand your Body Mass Index, you gain a medically recognised reference point for evaluating whether your weight is within a range associated with lower risks of chronic disease. When you know your Basal Metabolic Rate, you understand exactly how many calories your body burns at complete rest — a baseline figure that unlocks smart, science-backed approaches to weight management.

Body Mass Index (BMI) — A Vital Health Screening Tool

BMI is the most widely used screening tool for categorising body weight relative to height. Developed in the early 19th century and adopted by the World Health Organisation as a global standard, BMI divides your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in metres. The resulting number places you into one of four categories: underweight (below 18.5), normal weight (18.5–24.9), overweight (25–29.9), or obese (30 and above). While BMI is not a diagnostic tool — it does not directly measure body fat percentage or distinguish between muscle and adipose tissue — it remains an invaluable population-level indicator associated with risks of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, certain cancers, and other lifestyle-related conditions. Our AfriWidget BMI calculator provides your result in real time and can contextualise your score, explain what it means for your long-term health, and suggest evidence-based next steps.

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

Your Basal Metabolic Rate is the number of calories your body burns every day just to maintain basic physiological functions — breathing, circulation, cell production, and temperature regulation — while at complete rest. It accounts for the majority of your total energy expenditure, typically 60–75% for most adults. AfriWidget calculates your BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which has been validated in numerous independent clinical studies as the most accurate formula for estimating resting energy expenditure in diverse adult populations. TDEE builds on your BMR by factoring in your actual daily physical activity level using scientifically validated activity multipliers. Whether you are sedentary, lightly active, moderately active, very active, or extra active, our calculator applies the appropriate activity factor to produce your personalised daily caloric requirement.

Macronutrient Planning and Sports Nutrition

Beyond calorie counting, understanding the composition of your diet — the balance of protein, carbohydrates, and fats — is essential for athletic performance, muscle recovery, and metabolic health. Our Macro Calculator uses evidence-based guidelines to determine your optimal daily intake of each macronutrient based on your TDEE, activity level, and specific fitness goals. Whether you are an endurance athlete, a strength athlete, or someone seeking general wellness, personalised macronutrient ratios help you fuel your body effectively and achieve measurable results.

Hydration and Reproductive Health Tools

Water is fundamental to every biological process in the human body. Yet, many people do not consume enough fluids to maintain optimal physiological function. Our Water Intake Calculator builds a personalised hydration goal based on your weight, daily physical activity, and climate. The Ovulation and Cycle Calculator offers individuals a private, browser-based tool for tracking menstrual cycles, estimating fertile windows, and planning reproductive health decisions.
