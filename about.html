About AfriWidget

Built in Ghana. Made for the World. A Platform That Puts People First

The story, mission, and reason behind AfriWidget — a free, private, browser-based toolkit designed to help students, creators, and everyday people get things done without barriers.

What Is AfriWidget?

AfriWidget is a free collection of online tools that work directly inside your web browser. There is no app to download, no account to create, and no personal information required to use it. You open the website, choose a tool, and get your answer. That is the entire experience.

We built AfriWidget because we believe that useful, accurate, everyday tools should be available to everyone — not just to those who can afford paid software, professional consultants, or expensive data plans. Whether you are a student calculating your exam score, a freelancer preparing an invoice, a parent checking a body mass index, or a small business owner planning a loan, the tools on AfriWidget are here for you, free of charge.

Today, AfriWidget offers more than 175 tools spread across five main areas: education, finance, health, productivity, and business. Each tool is designed to be quick, private, and easy to understand. Nothing is hidden behind a paywall. Nothing is sold to advertisers. Nothing asks you to log in first.

In one sentence: AfriWidget exists to remove the small obstacles that stop people from getting useful things done. No accounts. No cost. No compromise on privacy.

The Story Behind AfriWidget

Every platform has a beginning. Ours started with a personal problem.

Obed Avorlenu, the founder of AfriWidget, grew up in Ghana. Like many young people across the continent, he faced challenges that could easily have stopped him from pursuing his goals. Money was often tight. Educational opportunities were sometimes out of reach. He watched friends and family members struggle with decisions that could have been made easier with the right tool — but the right tool was either unavailable, too expensive, or buried behind a complicated sign-up process.

He studied business at Keta Business College. While there, he noticed how often everyday tasks depended on simple calculations that people did not know how to do. A student wanted to understand what final exam score would raise their average. A small shop owner wanted to know if a loan was worth taking. A parent wanted to check their child's body weight. A job seeker wanted to know if their grades met the requirements of a university program. In each case, the answer was a calculation away — but there was no free, simple, private tool to do it.

Obed decided to build those tools himself.

Learning to Build

He did not have a large team. He did not have outside funding. He did not have a computer science degree. What he had was a laptop, a willingness to learn, and an internet connection whenever it was available. He taught himself how modern web development works — how to write HTML, how to style pages with CSS, how to make things work with JavaScript, and how to publish a website that anyone could access from anywhere.

The learning process was not quick or smooth. There were many nights spent debugging simple things. There were many moments of confusion. There were tools that broke. There were calculations that were wrong at first. Every problem became a chance to learn something new.

Slowly, the idea turned into a real website. The first tools were simple: a GPA calculator, a compound interest tool, a body mass index check. But as people started using them and asking for more, the platform grew. Every request led to another tool. Every new tool brought new users. And every new user reminded Obed why the project was worth continuing.

Why Privacy Comes First

From the very beginning, AfriWidget was designed to protect the people who use it. That decision was not a marketing strategy. It was a principle. When someone enters their exam grades, their salary, their weight, or their loan amount into a calculator, that information is deeply personal. It should never leave their device. It should never be sold. It should never be stored on a server somewhere for someone else to use.

So every AfriWidget tool runs entirely inside your browser. The math happens on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere. When you close the tab, the calculation disappears. There is no history, no tracking, no record. This is not a feature we added later. It is the foundation on which the platform was built.

“When you help someone solve a real problem, they remember it. That is the only marketing that matters.”
— Obed Avorlenu, Founder of AfriWidget

Why We Focus on Africa and the World

AfriWidget has a special focus on Africa. This is not because we think the tools only matter here. It is because Africa is where the gap between "I need a tool" and "I can find one" is widest.

Across the continent, hundreds of millions of people use mobile phones as their main way of getting online. Data costs are high. Devices are often older. Internet connections are sometimes slow. Many tools that are easy to find in richer countries do not work well in these conditions. Pages are heavy. Ads cover the screen. Sign-ups block progress. Languages do not match. Payment methods are not available.

AfriWidget is built to work in this environment. Pages load fast. Sizes stay small. There is no video, no animation that slows things down, no pop-ups that block the screen. Everything is designed for mobile first, because that is how most people will use it.

At the same time, the tools are useful everywhere. A student in the United Kingdom can use our UCAS points calculator. Someone in Canada can use the loan repayment tool. A freelancer in the United States can use the invoice generator. A parent anywhere in the world can use the BMI check. The platform is built for Africa, but it works for everyone.

What Makes AfriWidget Different

There are many websites with calculators. Some are good. Many are cluttered with ads and unclear. A few are accurate but slow. Very few are truly free. Here is what makes AfriWidget stand apart.

1. No Accounts, Ever

You never have to create an account. You never have to give us an email address. You never have to prove you are a real person. Open the tool, use it, close the tab. That is the whole flow.

2. Everything Runs in Your Browser

Your numbers stay on your device. We do not collect them. We do not store them. We do not use them to show you ads. Even our developers cannot see what you calculated. That is how it should be.

3. Built for Slow Connections

AfriWidget pages are lightweight. They load quickly on 2G, 3G, and 4G. They work on older phones and cheap tablets. They do not require the latest browser version or the newest device. Anyone with internet access can use them.

4. Accurate Formulas, Clearly Explained

Every calculation on AfriWidget follows a real formula. Where possible, we follow official standards from recognised bodies — the World Health Organization for BMI, official exam boards for grading, standard financial math for loans. We show our work. We explain the math. We link to official sources so you can verify for yourself.

5. No Hidden Costs

AfriWidget is free. It will stay free. There is no premium tier, no "pro" version, no monthly subscription, no unlock code. The tools you see are the tools you get.

The Tools We Build

AfriWidget is organized into five main categories. Each one serves a different part of everyday life.

Education & Academic Tools

This is where AfriWidget started. Students across Africa often need to calculate their exam aggregates, understand their GPA, or figure out their university admission score. Official systems vary from country to country. A Ghanaian student uses WASSCE aggregate points. A Kenyan student uses KCSE and KUCCPS cluster points. A Nigerian student uses JAMB UTME. A South African student uses APS and Matric results. A Zimbabwean student uses ZIMSEC points. Each system has its own rules, and each one can be confusing.

Our education tools translate these systems into simple forms. You enter your grades. The tool does the math. You get a clear answer that matches what the official body would say. We also explain what the result means — whether it qualifies you for a specific course, a specific university, or a specific scholarship.

Finance & Money Tools

Money decisions are some of the most important decisions anyone makes. Should I take this loan? How much will I actually pay over time? How much will my savings grow? What is my monthly payment going to be? These questions matter, and the answers are usually a calculation away — but only if you know how to do it.

Our finance tools cover compound interest, loan payments, mortgage math, savings projections, student loan repayment, and more. Each one is based on standard financial formulas. Each one shows you not just the answer but also the reasoning.

Health & Wellness Tools

Health tools on AfriWidget are focused on general awareness. They are not a replacement for a doctor. They are not a diagnosis. They are simple checks that help you understand basic information about your body — weight range, body mass index, daily calorie needs, hydration targets, and cycle tracking.

Every health tool comes with a clear reminder: this is not medical advice. Please see a qualified professional for anything related to your health.

Productivity & Everyday Tools

This category covers all the small tasks that come up in daily work. Need to clean up text? Need to make a QR code? Need to convert a unit of measurement? Need to generate a strong password? Need to prepare a simple invoice? These tools handle those tasks without requiring you to open five different apps.

Business & Freelance Tools

Small business owners and freelancers need to stay organised. Our business tools help with pricing, invoicing, profit margins, and basic planning. They are designed for people who do not have an accountant on call and do not want to learn a complicated software package just to send a bill.

How We Build and Maintain Our Tools

Every tool on AfriWidget follows the same process.

Step 1: Identify a Real Need

We do not build tools just because they look interesting. We build them because people ask for them. Requests come in through email, through messages on social media, and through the contact form on the site. When we see the same request repeated, we know it is worth building.

Step 2: Research the Official Rules

Before writing code, we study the official rules. For an education tool, that means reading the exam board's published grading system. For a finance tool, that means checking standard financial formulas. For a health tool, that means following guidelines from recognised bodies like the World Health Organization. Where the rules are unclear, we say so — we do not guess.

Step 3: Build in the Simplest Possible Way

Each tool is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We do not use heavy frameworks. We do not require users to load large libraries. This keeps pages fast and reduces the chance of something breaking.

Step 4: Test with Real Cases

Every tool is tested against real examples. If a student uses the WASSCE aggregate calculator and gets a result, that result should match what the official WAEC body would give. If not, the tool is not ready.

Step 5: Publish, Monitor, Improve

Once a tool is live, we watch for feedback. If users find bugs, we fix them. If rules change — for example, if an exam board updates its grading system — we update the tool. The work does not end when a tool is published. It only begins.

How We Protect Your Privacy

Privacy is not an afterthought. It is the reason AfriWidget runs the way it does.

When you use an AfriWidget tool, here is exactly what happens:

  • Your browser loads the tool's code.
  • You enter your numbers.
  • Your browser performs the calculation locally.
  • The result appears on your screen.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. Nothing is stored. Nothing is logged.

There is no database of user calculations. There is no analytics on what numbers people enter. There is no way for us to see your results. Even if we wanted to, we could not — because the code does not send anything back.

Some of our pages use Google Analytics for basic traffic counting. This tells us how many people visited a page and where they came from, but it does not know your name, your email, your numbers, or anything you typed. Some pages show ads through Google AdSense. These ads are used to cover the cost of running the platform. They do not have access to your calculations.

Bottom line: When you use AfriWidget, your data stays with you. Always.

Who Uses AfriWidget?

AfriWidget is for anyone who needs a quick, accurate, free tool. In practice, our users fall into a few groups:

Students and Applicants

Secondary school students preparing for national exams. University applicants trying to figure out if their grades meet the requirements of a course. Parents helping their children plan. Teachers who want to explain a calculation clearly. Students applying to universities abroad and needing to convert their grades.

Freelancers and Small Business Owners

People who run their own business and need to price a service, prepare an invoice, calculate a profit margin, or plan a marketing budget. People who do not have an accountant on call and prefer simple tools over complicated software.

Everyday Users

Anyone who wants to check their body mass index, plan a savings goal, convert a currency, or work out a percentage. Not everyone needs an advanced tool. Most people just need a quick answer.

Professionals and Researchers

People who need to convert units, format data, generate QR codes, or work with text. These users appreciate tools that are fast, private, and do exactly one thing well.

What AfriWidget Is Not

We want to be honest about what AfriWidget does not do.

We are not a replacement for professional advice. If you have a serious medical question, see a doctor. If you have a serious financial question, see a financial advisor. If you have a serious legal question, see a lawyer. AfriWidget tools are for general awareness and quick calculations, not for making life-changing decisions on their own.

We are not a data company. We do not collect user data. We do not sell user data. We do not want user data. Our only goal is to run useful tools and keep them free.

We are not trying to be the biggest platform. There are larger companies with more resources. That is fine. Our goal is to serve the people we do serve, well. Quality matters more than size.

What Comes Next for AfriWidget

AfriWidget is still growing. The platform started with a handful of tools. Today it has over 175. In the coming months and years, the plan is to:

  • Expand the education tools to cover more countries and more exam systems.
  • Add deeper explanations and guides alongside each tool, so users can learn as they calculate.
  • Improve support for older devices and slower connections, so that no one is left out.
  • Keep every tool free and private, no matter how much the platform grows.

The long-term vision is simple. AfriWidget should become the first place anyone turns when they need a quick, trustworthy, private calculation. Not because we have the most tools or the flashiest design, but because we are the most reliable, the most honest, and the most respectful of the people who use us.
