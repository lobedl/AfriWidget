The Story Behind AfriWidget

Building fast, free, and completely private browser-based utilities to help students, creators, and professionals across Africa solve everyday problems in seconds.

OA
Founder & Lead Developer

Obed Avorlenu

Self-taught Ghanaian Web Developer & Creator of AfriWidget • Accra, Ghana

Some people are born into opportunity. Others have to build it themselves.

Obed Avorlenu belongs to the second group.

At just 19 years old, Obed's journey has been defined by determination, self-learning, and an unwavering belief that technology can change lives. Coming from Ghana, he faced challenges that could have easily discouraged him, including financial obstacles that affected his educational path. Instead of allowing those difficulties to define his future, he chose to define it himself.

Without expensive resources, a large team, or outside funding, Obed taught himself web development and began turning ideas into reality. He spent countless hours learning, building, failing, improving, and trying again. Every challenge became a lesson. Every setback became motivation.

That persistence eventually led to the creation of AfriWidget — a platform built with a simple but powerful vision: to give people access to useful digital tools that can make life easier, work faster, and opportunities more accessible.

What makes AfriWidget special is not just the technology behind it. It is the purpose behind it.

Obed understands what it feels like to search for opportunities, to look for solutions, and to face limitations that many people encounter every day. That understanding shaped AfriWidget into more than just a website. It became a platform designed to help students plan their futures, freelancers grow their careers, professionals increase productivity, and everyday users solve problems within seconds.

Today, AfriWidget offers more than 100 tools across multiple categories, helping users save time, make smarter decisions, and access resources that would otherwise be difficult or expensive to obtain.

For Obed, success has never been only about building software. It is about creating impact.

His story is proof that where you start does not determine where you finish. A young person with a laptop, a vision, and the willingness to learn can create something meaningful that serves thousands of people.

As the founder of AfriWidget, Obed continues to build with a mission greater than himself: empowering people through technology, creating opportunities through innovation, and showing others that even the biggest dreams can begin with a single decision to start.

“Your circumstances may shape your journey, but they do not have to limit your destination.”
— Obed Avorlenu, Founder of AfriWidget

Our Mission & Purpose

AfriWidget was created to solve an urgent structural problem: across Africa and the developing world, access to essential calculations in academics, business, health, and personal finance is frequently gated behind complex software, paywalls, high-bandwidth web pages, or expensive private consultants.

Whether it is a Ghanaian student trying to calculate their exact WASSCE university aggregate, a Kenyan applicant computing KUCCPS degree cluster points, a South African matriculant checking APS eligibility for NSFAS funding, or a freelance creator invoicing an international client — AfriWidget provides immediate, 100% free, deterministic mathematical tools that run instantly in the user's browser with zero sign-up friction.

What We Build: Four Core Pillars

Education & Academic Calculators

National examination grading tools rigorously calibrated against official standards from WAEC (West Africa), JAMB (Nigeria), KUCCPS & KNEC (Kenya), ZIMSEC (Zimbabwe), UNEB (Uganda), and the South African Department of Higher Education & Training (DHET).

Finance & Business Math

Standardized loan amortization, compound interest modeling, freelance pricing calculators, VAT/GST estimates, and startup burn rate projections to empower entrepreneurs and savers with transparent, math-backed projections.

Health & Wellness Utilities

Clinically grounded BMI, BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor), TDEE, water intake, and calorie deficit planners engineered strictly for general wellness awareness with clear clinical disclaimers.

Productivity & Web Utilities

Clean in-browser tools including PDF Invoice/Receipt generators, QR Code builders, JSON formatters, text case modifiers, and reading-time estimators that require zero file uploads or account creations.

Our Engineering & Privacy Standards

✓

100% Client-Side In-Memory Execution

All computations are executed directly inside your web browser via vanilla JavaScript. When you calculate your GPA, matric points, or mortgage payments, your private numbers are never transmitted to or stored in a backend server database.

✓

Low-Bandwidth & Mobile-First Performance

In many African regions, mobile data costs are high and network coverage is variable. AfriWidget pages are optimized for near-instant rendering with static pre-rendered HTML, compressed CSS, and zero bloated JavaScript frameworks.

✓

Algorithmic Transparency & Official Citations

Every educational and financial formula is fully documented with step-by-step mathematical explanations, worked examples, and direct links to official governing bodies (such as WAEC, JAMB, KUCCPS, ZIMSEC, and the South African Government Gazette).
