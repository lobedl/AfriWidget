How CGPA is Calculated in Nigerian Universities & Polytechnics

Under the guidelines established by the National Universities Commission (NUC) through the Core Curriculum and Minimum Academic Standards (CCMAS), tertiary institutions in Nigeria operate on a standard 5-point Grade Point Average system (with selected polytechnics and colleges operating on the 4-point NBTE system).

Academic standing is determined by two core parameters: the Credit Unit (CU) weighting assigned to each course and the Grade Point (GP) earned according to your percentage score in continuous assessments (CAs) and semester examinations.

Mathematical Formulas

1. Quality Point (QP) Per Course:

**QP** = Course Credit Units (CU) × Grade Point Earned (GP)

Example: A 3-unit course passed with an 'A' (5.0 points) yields 3 × 5.0 = 15.0 Quality Points.

2. Semester Grade Point Average (GPA):

**Semester GPA** = Total Semester Quality Points (TQP) ÷ Total Semester Credit Units Registered (TCU)

3. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):

**CGPA** = Cumulative Total Quality Points (All Semesters) ÷ Cumulative Total Credit Units Registered (All Semesters)

Realistic 100-Level First Semester Example

Suppose a 100-level student registered 5 courses totaling 14 credit units:

  • GST 111 (2 Units): Grade A (5.0) = 2 × 5.0 = 10.0 QP
  • MTH 101 (3 Units): Grade A (5.0) = 3 × 5.0 = 15.0 QP
  • PHY 101 (3 Units): Grade B (4.0) = 3 × 4.0 = 12.0 QP
  • CHM 101 (3 Units): Grade C (3.0) = 3 × 3.0 = 9.0 QP
  • BIO 101 (3 Units): Grade A (5.0) = 3 × 5.0 = 15.0 QP

Total Quality Points = 10 + 15 + 12 + 9 + 15 = 61.0

Total Credit Units Registered = 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 14

Semester GPA = 61.0 ÷ 14 = 4.36 (Second Class Upper Division)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum CGPA required to graduate from a Nigerian university?

Under current NUC guidelines, the minimum CGPA required to graduate with a Pass degree is 1.00. However, many universities have abolished the Pass degree, setting 1.50 (Third Class Honours) as the minimum benchmark for graduation. Students with a CGPA below 1.00 or 1.50 after the maximum allowable duration (usually 1.5 times the standard program length) face academic probation or withdrawal.

How does an 'F' grade (Carryover) affect my CGPA?

An 'F' grade carries 0.0 Quality Points, but its credit units are added to your registered total. This causes a steep drop in your CGPA denominator. When you retake and pass the course, the new grade points and credit units are added to cumulative totals. Under standard NUC guidelines, both attempts remain on your official academic transcript and cumulative calculation.

Can I move from Second Class Lower (2:2) to First Class in 300 or 400 Level?

While possible, raising a CGPA significantly in later years becomes mathematically harder because the accumulated credit unit denominator is already large. Use our Target Honours Forecaster tab above to calculate the exact semester GPA required in your remaining courses.

Are continuous assessment tests (CA) included in the final grade point?

Yes. In Nigerian universities, course grades typically combine Continuous Assessment (30% to 40%) — including tests, mid-semester exams, quizzes, and laboratory practicals — with the Final Semester Examination (60% to 70%) to form your total 100% mark.
