Complete Guide to UK Student Finance (Tuition, Maintenance & Repayments)

Navigating higher education funding in the United Kingdom involves two key components: **Tuition Fee Loans** paid directly to your chosen university, and **Maintenance Loans** paid into your personal bank account to cover rent, food, books, and living expenses.

1. How Student Finance Maintenance Loans Work

Student Finance England (SFE) calculates maintenance loans using a two-tier formula:

  • Non-Means-Tested Base: Every eligible full-time student receives a baseline sum regardless of parental or household earnings (e.g., £4,767 outside London or £6,647 inside London).
  • Means-Tested Top-Up: Students whose residual household income is £25,000 or lower receive the maximum top-up. For incomes between £25,000 and ~£62,311, the top-up decreases gradually by roughly £1 for every £7.50 to £8.00 of additional income.

2. Key Differences: Plan 5 vs. Plan 2 Loans

In August 2023, the UK Government introduced **Plan 5** for all new undergraduate students starting in England:

Plan 5 (2023+ Starters)

  • • Repayment Threshold: £25,000 / year
  • • Repayment Rate: 9% above threshold
  • • Interest Rate: RPI only (no real interest penalty)
  • • Write-Off Period: 40 years post-graduation

Plan 2 (2012–2023 Starters)

  • • Repayment Threshold: £27,295 / year
  • • Repayment Rate: 9% above threshold
  • • Interest Rate: RPI up to RPI + 3%
  • • Write-Off Period: 30 years post-graduation

3. Mathematical Repayment Formula

Student loan repayments in the UK operate like a graduate contribution tax deducted automatically via PAYE:

Monthly Repayment = ((Gross Annual Salary - Repayment Threshold) × 0.09) ÷ 12

If your gross earnings fall below the threshold in any pay period (e.g. during career breaks or unpaid leave), deductions automatically drop to zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I start repaying my UK student loan? ▼

You only become liable to make repayments from the April after you leave or graduate from your course, and only if your annual salary exceeds the relevant plan threshold (e.g. £25,000 for Plan 5).

Should I overpay my student loan to clear it early? Should I overpay my student loan to clear it early? ▼

For the majority of UK graduates, voluntary overpayments are financially inadvisable. Because student loans are wiped after 30 or 40 years, overpaying often means repaying money you would never have otherwise had to pay. Only very high earners who are mathematically certain to clear the full debt plus interest within the term benefit from early clearance.

Can I get a Maintenance Loan if I study part-time? ▼

Yes. Part-time students with a course intensity of at least 25% are eligible for part-time tuition loans and a pro-rata maintenance loan calculated based on course intensity.
