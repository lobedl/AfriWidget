Privacy Policy Full Transparency About Your Data

This document explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your information when you use AfriWidget. No hidden clauses. No confusing legal jargon. Just clear answers.

Effective Date: 1 January 2026 • Last Updated: 5 October 2026

Frequently Asked Questions About Privacy

Does AfriWidget store or transmit the calculation numbers I enter?

No. Every calculator and academic tool runs 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your numbers, formulas, and results are never sent to, processed by, or saved on AfriWidget servers.

What personal information does AfriWidget collect from visitors?

AfriWidget requires no user accounts, logins, emails, or passwords. We only collect standard anonymized technical server logs (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent strings) and aggregated traffic metrics through Google Analytics.

How do advertising cookies from Google AdSense work on AfriWidget?

Google AdSense uses cookies to serve relevant advertisements that fund our free calculators. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner, or adjust ad preferences directly in Google's Ads Settings.

Is AfriWidget compliant with African and international privacy regulations?

Yes. Because our tools operate with client-side computation and zero database storage of calculation data, AfriWidget adheres to data minimization requirements under the Ghana Data Protection Act (Act 843), Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), South Africa POPIA, and EU GDPR.

What data is sent if I use an optional AI insight feature?

When you click an AI insight button, only the anonymous calculation inputs and immediate results are sent to our secure backend proxy to generate the explanation. No personal names, IP addresses, or tracking identifiers are passed to the AI service.

How can I contact AfriWidget regarding privacy questions?

You can reach out through our Contact page or email our team directly. We are committed to transparency and happy to explain our local-execution architecture in detail.
