Privacy Policy

AfriWidget is engineered with a strict zero-server data retention architecture. Your calculations stay completely inside your browser's private memory.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed & Updated: March 31, 2026

Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

At AfriWidget, we believe privacy is not an afterthought, marketing slogan, or complex settings toggle — it is the foundational architecture of everything we build. Founded by Ghanaian web developer Obed Avorlenu, AfriWidget executes 100% of mathematical, academic, financial, health, and productivity calculations directly inside your device's web browser via client-side JavaScript. We do not operate user account databases, we do not require signups, and we do not store, harvest, or monetize your personal inputs.

1. Who We Are & Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the website https://afriwidget.com and all related subpages, calculators, generators, and digital tools provided by AfriWidget (“we,” “our,” or “us”). AfriWidget was founded and is maintained by independent developer Obed Avorlenu, based in Accra, Ghana.

This document explains with complete technical clarity what happens when you visit our website, how client-side computing protects your confidentiality, what limited network metadata is generated during web visits, and how advertising partners like Google AdSense process non-identifying contextual cookies.

2. The Client-Side Zero-Storage Architecture

Traditional web applications transmit your form submissions across the internet to backend databases. AfriWidget is engineered deliberately differently:

01. In-Browser RAM

When you enter numbers, grades, or text into any AfriWidget tool, JavaScript code executes locally inside your browser's memory without sending payloads to our server.

02. No User Accounts

We require no email registration, usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or credit cards. There is no account profile tracking your tool usage history.

03. Instant Volatility

Closing your browser tab or refreshing the page immediately purges all input state from your device's memory. We maintain zero database records of your computations.

3. Domain-Specific Privacy Breakdown

To demonstrate our commitment to transparency, here is how each specific category of tools on AfriWidget handles your inputs:

🎓 Education & Academic Calculators (WAEC, JAMB, KUCCPS, ZIMSEC, APS, GPA)

Your candidate grades, subject selections, target university courses, JAMB UTME scores, credit unit weights, and semester GPA inputs are calculated entirely in-memory. We never collect student names, index numbers, transcripts, or institutional IDs.

💰 Financial & Business Math (Loans, Mortgages, Compound Interest, VAT, Freelance)

All principal loan amounts, annual percentage rates, mortgage payment schedules, freelance hourly rates, and profit margin formulas are evaluated using deterministic client-side mathematics. No banking details, income records, or credit scores are ever requested or stored.

🏥 Health & Wellness Tools (BMI, BMR, TDEE, Calorie Deficit, Water Intake, Ovulation)

Body metrics including weight, height, age, biological sex, activity factor, and menstrual cycle dates remain strictly on your local device. We are not a medical provider and we never construct biometric profiles or sell health telemetry to third-party brokers.

⚡ Productivity & Utilities (PDF Invoices, QR Codes, JSON Formatter, Text Tools)

Invoice generation and QR Code encoding use in-browser Canvas and client-side vector libraries. When you generate an invoice PDF or QR image, the file is rendered directly on your device and saved to your local downloads folder without uploading files to our servers.

4. AI Features & Stateless Ephemeral Proxy

Certain educational and financial tools offer optional AI-powered guidance (e.g. course admission advice, budgeting recommendations) powered by Groq / LLaMA acceleration.

When you explicitly click an AI action button:

  • Only the anonymous calculated numerical parameters (e.g. “WASSCE Aggregate: 14, Target: Computer Science”) are transmitted via an encrypted HTTPS connection to our stateless Netlify function proxy.
  • No Personal Identifiers (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or IP addresses are passed to the AI inference engine.
  • The request is processed ephemerally in real-time, the response text is returned to your browser screen, and the payload is immediately discarded. No prompt history or user log is stored.

6. Web Analytics & Infrastructure Security Logs

To understand aggregate platform traffic patterns, diagnose technical faults, and protect against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, limited non-identifying telemetry is collected:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Collects anonymized, aggregated metrics (e.g. browser type, operating system, page views, session duration) with IP masking active. This data cannot be reverse-engineered to identify individual human users.
  • Hosting & CDN Server Logs (Netlify / Cloudflare): Standard web server access logs recording timestamp, HTTP request method, user-agent header, and generic origin routing. These logs are maintained temporarily solely for cybersecurity forensics and traffic routing.
  • Contact Form Communications: If you voluntarily reach out via our contact page, your name, email address, and message are used solely to reply to your inquiry. We never sell, rent, or distribute your email address to marketing lists.

7. International Privacy Rights & Regulatory Compliance

AfriWidget adheres strictly to international privacy and data protection frameworks across the jurisdictions we serve:

Guaranteed rights to information, transparent communication, right to erasure, and zero profiling without explicit affirmative consent.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

AfriWidget provides educational grading calculators widely utilized by secondary and high school students across Africa (such as BECE, WASSCE, KCSE, and O-Level candidates).

Because our calculators do not require registration, login credentials, names, birthdays, or locations, we do not collect or store personal identifiable information from children under 13 or minors of any age. If a parent or guardian believes a child has transmitted personal data through a contact inquiry, please reach out to us for immediate erasure.
