If you only remember one thing from this page, let it be this: AfriWidget does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. Every calculator, converter, and tool on this website runs entirely inside your own web browser. The numbers you type never leave your device.

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

AfriWidget is a free, browser-based toolkit built and maintained by founder Obed Avorlenu in Accra, Ghana. The platform offers more than 175 calculators, converters, generators, and utilities across five main categories: education, finance, health, productivity, and business. Every tool is free to use, requires no account, and runs entirely inside your web browser.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit afriwidget.com. It applies to every page on this website — including the homepage, the category pages (education, finance, health, productivity), each individual tool page, and every supporting page such as this one, the Contact page, and the Cookie Policy.

By using AfriWidget, you agree to the practices described in this document. If any part of this policy does not match your expectations, you should stop using the website. We designed this policy to be read and understood, not skimmed and ignored. Take your time with it. If anything is unclear, contact us using the details at the end.

2. What We Do Not Collect

Before we explain what we do collect (which is very little), let us be completely clear about what we do not collect. This section lists information that we do not ask for, do not receive, and do not store in any form.

Personal Identifiers

We do not collect your full name, first name, last name, email address, phone number, physical address, postal code, date of birth, gender, national identification number, passport number, driver's license number, or any other personal identifier. AfriWidget does not have an account system, so there is no profile for us to attach any of these details to.

Financial Information

We do not collect credit card numbers, bank account details, mobile money numbers, PayPal account information, or any other financial credentials. When you use a finance calculator — such as the loan calculator, mortgage calculator, or compound interest calculator — the amounts you type are calculated in your browser. Those numbers are never sent to us.

Academic Records

We do not collect student names, exam index numbers, school names, transcripts, admission letters, or grades from any official academic database. When you use an education tool — such as the WASSCE aggregate calculator, GPA calculator, or KCSE grade calculator — you type your grades directly into the tool. Those numbers stay on your device.

Health Information

We do not collect body weight, height, age, biological sex, medical history, menstrual cycle dates, or any other health-related information. When you use a health tool — such as the BMI calculator, water intake calculator, or ovulation tracker — the numbers you enter are processed entirely within your browser. They never reach our servers.

Location Data

We do not collect GPS coordinates, IP-based location data, city, region, or country-level location history. We do not use location-tracking features. The only location-related information that may be captured is the general country/region from our analytics provider (Google Analytics), and that data is anonymized and aggregated — meaning we can see that, for example, 500 people from Ghana visited a page, but we cannot see who they were or exactly where they came from.

Device and Browser Fingerprints

We do not attempt to build a unique fingerprint of your device or browser. We do not track you across other websites. We do not use hidden pixels, canvas fingerprinting, or any technique designed to identify you as a specific individual.

3. How Our Client-Side Calculators Work

The words "client-side" appear frequently on this page. They are important, so let us define them clearly.

When a website runs a calculator, that calculator has to perform a mathematical operation. There are two ways to do this:

Server-Side Calculation (What We Do Not Do)

In server-side calculation, the numbers you type are sent across the internet to a computer controlled by the website owner. That computer performs the math and sends the result back to your screen. The problem with this approach is that your numbers leave your device. They travel over the internet, they arrive at a server, and they can be logged, stored, or shared. Many websites operate this way without telling users.

Client-Side Calculation (What AfriWidget Does)

In client-side calculation, the numbers you type never leave your device. The website sends your browser a small piece of code (usually written in JavaScript). Your browser runs that code locally, using your own device's processor. The result appears on your screen without any data being transmitted to a server. This is how every AfriWidget tool works.

Practical example: When you open the WASSCE aggregate calculator and enter your grades, the calculation happens on your own phone, tablet, or computer. The result appears on your screen. If you close the tab, everything disappears. There is no server on our end receiving those grades, and there is no database to store them.

This architecture gives you three important guarantees:

  • Your inputs stay with you. Nothing you type is transmitted to AfriWidget or to anyone else.
  • Your results are private. Nobody at AfriWidget can see what you calculated, and there is no log of your session.
  • Your data cannot be breached. If a hacker were to break into a hypothetical AfriWidget database, they would find nothing — because there is no such database. There is literally nothing to steal.

4. What Information We Do Collect

We said earlier that we do not collect personal information. That is true. But there are three categories of information that are collected at a technical level when you visit any modern website, and AfriWidget is no exception. We want to be honest about these three categories so that you are not surprised later.

Category 1: Aggregated Anonymous Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how many people visit AfriWidget, which pages they read, and how long they stay. This information is aggregated — meaning it is presented to us as numbers, not as individual visitors. We cannot see that "John from Accra visited the GPA page at 3:15 pm." We only see that "5,000 people visited the GPA page this week."

The information GA4 collects includes: the pages you visited, how long you stayed on each page, the type of device you used (mobile, tablet, desktop), the browser you used (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.), the country you connected from (not the exact city), and how you arrived at AfriWidget (for example, from a Google search or a social media link). This data is anonymized, meaning your IP address is masked and we cannot identify you.

Category 2: Cookies from Google AdSense

AfriWidget is free to use, and we plan to keep it that way forever. To cover the cost of running the website — servers, domain names, and the time spent building and maintaining tools — we display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google uses its own cookies to decide which ads to show you. These cookies are set by Google, not by AfriWidget, and they do not have access to anything you type into our calculators.

You can read more about Google's advertising cookies on their official pages, and you can opt out of personalized ads at any time (see section 12 of this policy).

Category 3: Server Access Logs

Like every website on the internet, AfriWidget's hosting provider records basic information about every page request. These logs typically include: the date and time of the request, the browser type you used, and a truncated version of your IP address. These logs exist for cybersecurity purposes only — to detect and block attacks, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems. They are not used for marketing, they are not sold to anyone, and they are automatically deleted after a short period (usually 30 days).

That is the complete list. Those three categories — aggregated analytics, advertising cookies, and server logs — are the only types of information generated when you visit AfriWidget. None of them can be used to identify you personally.

5. Cookies and How They Work on AfriWidget

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. Cookies have many legitimate uses — they can remember that you are logged in, they can remember your language preference, and they can help websites measure traffic. Unfortunately, they can also be used to track you across the internet, which is why so many people are understandably cautious about them.

Here is the honest breakdown of every type of cookie you may encounter on AfriWidget.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly. On AfriWidget, the only strictly necessary cookie we use is one that remembers your cookie consent choice — so that you are not asked the same question every time you visit a new page. This cookie is set by AfriWidget itself and does not track you across other websites.

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish between different visitors so that it can produce accurate traffic statistics. For example, without analytics cookies, Google would not be able to tell the difference between one person visiting the website 100 times and 100 different people visiting once. These cookies do not contain personal information about you, and they are not shared with AfriWidget — we only see the aggregated statistics.

Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

Google AdSense uses cookies to show you ads that are more likely to be relevant to you. These cookies remember the pages you have visited on AfriWidget and other websites, and they use that information to select which ads to display. These cookies are set and controlled by Google, not by AfriWidget. If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you can disable them — see section 12 for instructions.

Cookies We Do Not Use

For complete clarity: AfriWidget does not use cookies for user accounts (we have no accounts), cookies for tracking your calculator inputs, cookies for email marketing, or cookies for cross-site profile building of our own design. We do not run any third-party scripts on calculator pages that could observe your inputs.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Every major browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Brave — allows you to view, block, and delete cookies. Instructions are available in each browser's help documentation.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

AfriWidget is free to use. There is no premium tier, no subscription option, and no plan to introduce one. To cover the costs of running the website, we display advertisements supplied by Google AdSense. This is a common and transparent arrangement: users get free tools, advertisers pay to reach an audience, and the website owner earns a small amount from each ad view.

What Google AdSense Does

Google AdSense is a program operated by Google that places advertisements on websites. When you visit a page on AfriWidget, Google may display an ad. The ad is selected by Google's automated systems based on a combination of factors — including the content of the page, the country you are connecting from, and (if advertising cookies are enabled) your browsing history on other websites.

What Google AdSense Does Not Do

Google AdSense does not have access to anything you type into AfriWidget's calculators. When you calculate your loan payment or check your body mass index, those numbers are handled exclusively by your browser. Google's advertising systems have no visibility into those inputs. They see the page (for example, "BMI Calculator"), not your personal result.

Your Control Over Advertising Cookies

You have full control over advertising cookies. If you do not want to receive personalized ads, you can:

  • Visit Google Ads Settings and disable personalized advertising for your Google account.
  • Visit aboutads.info to opt out of interest-based advertising from many companies at once.
  • Use your browser's cookie settings to block third-party cookies entirely.
  • Install a browser extension that blocks advertising and tracking scripts (such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger).

If you disable personalized advertising, you will still see ads on AfriWidget, but they will be generic instead of targeted. The tools will work exactly the same either way.

7. Google Analytics and Traffic Measurement

Google Analytics 4 is a tool that helps website owners understand how people use their site. AfriWidget uses it for a simple reason: without traffic data, we would not know which tools are useful and which ones need improvement.

What We Learn from Analytics

From Google Analytics, we can see the following kinds of information:

  • How many people visited AfriWidget this week, this month, and this year.
  • Which pages are the most popular (for example, the GPA calculator or the BMI calculator).
  • How long visitors stay on each page before moving on.
  • The general country visitors are connecting from (Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, the United Kingdom, etc.).
  • The type of device used (mobile, tablet, or desktop) and the browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.).
  • Which pages referred visitors to AfriWidget (for example, a Google search or a social media post).

What Analytics Does Not Tell Us

Analytics does not tell us your name. It does not tell us your email address. It does not tell us your exact location. It does not tell us what you calculated. It cannot connect a specific visitor to a specific calculation. It is a statistical tool, not a tracking tool.

IP Anonymization

We have enabled IP anonymization on Google Analytics. This means that the last few digits of every visitor's IP address are removed before the data is recorded. As a result, it is impossible to use the analytics data to identify a specific person or device.

8. AI-Powered Insights and the Secure Proxy

Some AfriWidget tools offer optional AI-powered insights. For example, after calculating your GPA, you may see a button offering to explain what your result means and what steps you could take next. These insights are powered by Groq, a company that runs large language models at very high speed.

We want to be completely clear about how this works, because AI features are newer than traditional calculators, and we understand that many people are cautious about them.

What Happens When You Click an AI Button

If, and only if, you click an AI button:

  1. A short summary of your anonymous calculation is sent from your browser to a secure proxy server operated by AfriWidget.
  2. This proxy forwards the summary to Groq's inference engine for processing.
  3. Groq returns an explanation in plain language.
  4. The explanation is sent back to your browser and displayed on your screen.
  5. The summary and the response are immediately discarded. Nothing is stored.

What Is Sent to the AI

The information sent to the AI does not include your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal identifier. It includes only the numerical context required to generate a useful response — for example: "Student with WASSCE aggregate 14 applying to Computer Science at KNUST." There is nothing in that summary that could be used to identify you.

What Is Not Sent to the AI

Your IP address is not sent to Groq. Your browser information is not sent to Groq. Your past calculator use is not sent to Groq. The AI only sees the anonymous summary. If you do not click the AI button, no summary is sent at all — the standard calculator result is displayed and nothing leaves your browser.

11. Your Rights Under International Privacy Laws

AfriWidget is used by people in dozens of countries. Many of these countries have laws that protect your personal information. We respect those laws, and we have designed AfriWidget to comply with the most important ones from the outset. Here is a summary of your rights under the most relevant laws.

Ghana: Data Protection Act (Act 843)

If you are located in Ghana, you have the right to know what personal information is being collected about you and why. Because AfriWidget collects no personal information, this right is honoured automatically — there is nothing to disclose. If you have questions, you can contact the Data Protection Commission of Ghana for more information.

Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)

If you are located in Nigeria, you have the right to be informed about data collection, the right to access any data held about you, the right to correct inaccurate data, and the right to have your data deleted. Because AfriWidget does not hold any personal data, all of these rights are satisfied by default.

South Africa: Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

If you are located in South Africa, you have the right to be notified when your personal information is collected, the right to know what is being done with it, and the right to object to processing. Because AfriWidget collects nothing, there is nothing to object to. The only exception is the anonymous analytics and advertising cookies described in sections 6 and 7, which you can control at any time.

European Union: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EU plus Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein), the GDPR gives you specific rights including the right to access your data, the right to be forgotten, the right to data portability, and the right to object to processing. Because AfriWidget does not collect personal information, all of these rights are honoured by design.

How to Exercise These Rights

If you believe AfriWidget holds any personal information about you and you want to access, correct, or delete it, please contact us at privacy@afriwidget.com. We will respond within 24 to 48 business hours. In practice, we expect that in almost every case, our answer will be: "We do not have any personal information about you."

12. How to Control Cookies and Ads

If you want to reduce or eliminate cookies and personalized ads while browsing AfriWidget and other websites, here are the practical steps you can take.

Adjust Your Browser Settings

Every major browser allows you to view, block, and delete cookies. Look in your browser's Settings or Preferences menu, and search for "Cookies" or "Site Data." You can usually choose to:

  • Block all third-party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.
  • Allow cookies only for websites you trust.

Opt Out of Personalized Ads

Google provides a tool that lets you opt out of personalized advertising across all websites that use Google Ads. Visit Google Ads Settings to make that change. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising from many companies at once at aboutads.info.

Use Privacy-Focused Browsers or Extensions

Browsers such as Brave, Firefox, and Safari include strong built-in privacy features. Extensions such as uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, and Ghostery can also block advertising and tracking scripts. AfriWidget works fine with all of these tools — nothing on our website depends on advertising cookies being enabled.

Clear Your Cookies Regularly

If you want a clean slate, you can clear all cookies from your browser at any time. This will not affect your ability to use AfriWidget.

14. How We Protect Your Information

Even though we collect very little information, we still take security seriously. Here is what we do to protect what little exists.

  • Encrypted connections: Every page on AfriWidget is served over HTTPS, which means all data transmitted between your browser and our server is encrypted.
  • Client-side processing: Because calculations happen in your browser, there is no transmission of your inputs to protect. The strongest security measure is simply not transmitting data at all.
  • Minimal server code: Our proxy server for AI features is deliberately simple. It has one job, and it does not log or store anything.
  • Regular updates: We keep our software up to date to protect against known vulnerabilities.
  • No third-party data brokers: We do not share data with any marketing company, data broker, or advertising network beyond Google AdSense and Google Analytics.

17. Plain-Language Glossary

If any term in this policy was unfamiliar, here is a short glossary in everyday language.

Client-Side

Means "on your own device." Client-side code runs in your browser, not on a distant server. This is the safest way to run a calculator because your inputs never travel across the internet.

Server-Side

Means "on a computer controlled by the website." Server-side code receives data from your browser, processes it, and sends back a response. This is common but means your data leaves your device.

Cookie

A small text file that a website asks your browser to remember. Cookies have legitimate uses but can also be used to track you across the internet.

Analytics

The practice of measuring website traffic. It tells website owners how many people visited, which pages they read, and how long they stayed. When done properly (as we do), it does not identify individual visitors.

AdSense

A Google advertising program that displays ads on websites. AfriWidget uses AdSense to keep our tools free without charging users.

GDPR

The European Union's privacy law. It gives European users strong rights over their personal information.

NDPR

The Nigerian Data Protection Regulation. It protects the privacy of Nigerian users.

POPIA

The South African Protection of Personal Information Act. It protects the privacy of South African users.

HTTPS

A secure, encrypted connection between your browser and a website. Every AfriWidget page uses HTTPS.

IP Address

A number that identifies a device on the internet. We anonymize IP addresses in our analytics, and we do not store them.
