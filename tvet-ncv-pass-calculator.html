How the TVET NC(V) Pass Calculation Works

The National Certificate (Vocational) curriculum provides students with direct practical workplace skills alongside foundational academic theory across NQF Levels 2, 3, and 4. Passing an NC(V) Level requires satisfying strict multi-tier assessment criteria set by the Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET) and certified by Umalusi.

Official Weighting & Assessment Structure

Every candidate registers for seven subjects: three fundamental subjects and four vocational subjects. The weighting rules are:

  • Fundamental Subjects (Language, Life Orientation, Mathematics/Maths Lit): Internal Continuous Assessment (ICASS) carries a 50% weighting, and the national external examination carries a 50% weighting.
  • Vocational Subjects (4 Core and Elective Subjects): ICASS carries 25%, the Integrated Summative Assessment Task (ISAT practical) carries 25%, and the national examination carries 50%.

Minimum Pass Thresholds

To obtain an NC(V) Level certificate, the following marks must be met:

  • Official Language: Minimum 40%
  • Life Orientation: Minimum 40%
  • Mathematics / Mathematical Literacy: Minimum 30%
  • All 4 Vocational Subjects: Minimum 50% in each subject.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between NC(V) and NATED (Report 191)?

NC(V) is a 3-year vocational programme (Levels 2–4) combining general academic education with practical workplace skills, ideal for students who completed Grade 9 or higher. NATED (Report 191 N1–N6) consists of theoretical trimester/semester courses requiring Matric (Grade 12) or equivalent for N4–N6.

Why do vocational subjects require 50% while maths requires only 30%?

The DHET and Umalusi framework mandates higher competence thresholds (50%) in core vocational specialisations to ensure industry readiness and technical safety, while general foundational numeracy retains standard national school benchmarks (30%).

What happens if I fail one vocational subject in NC(V)?

Under Umalusi guidelines, subject credits are retained permanently. You do not need to repeat the entire year; you only register to rewrite the failed vocational subject in the mid-year supplementary or end-of-year examination session.
