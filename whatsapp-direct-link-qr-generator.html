Create a WhatsApp click-to-chat link

A WhatsApp direct link gives customers, clients, students, or community members a quick way to start a conversation without saving a phone number first. This generator creates the standard wa.me format from a country code and phone number. You can also add an optional pre-filled greeting, copy the resulting link, open it in WhatsApp, or download a QR code for a poster, menu, business card, storefront, event sign, or social profile.

Choose the country code first, then enter the local phone number. The number should be active on WhatsApp and should normally be entered without the national trunk prefix. For example, if a local number begins with 0, use the international country code followed by the remaining digits. The generator removes non-numeric characters from the phone field, but you should still confirm the final link before sharing it.

How the wa.me URL works

The core format is https://wa.me/ followed by the full international phone number. The country code is included, while the plus sign and formatting characters are omitted. An optional message is added as URL-encoded text, so spaces and punctuation can travel safely inside the link. When someone opens the link, WhatsApp prepares a chat with the number and places the greeting in the message box. The person still controls whether to send it.

Test your generated link on a phone before printing it or adding it to a campaign. Confirm that the country, number, and greeting are correct. A typo in one digit can send visitors to the wrong account or to an unavailable number. Do not publish a private person’s number without consent, and check your organisation’s privacy and communication policies before placing a number on a public website.

Using a pre-filled greeting

A short greeting can tell the recipient why someone is contacting them. A shop might use “Hello, I would like to ask about your opening hours.” A service provider might use “Hello, I would like a quote for your service.” Keep the message brief and make it easy for the person to edit. Avoid putting passwords, payment details, identity numbers, or sensitive information into a public pre-filled link.

QR codes for offline marketing

The QR code contains the generated link. Visitors can scan it with a phone camera and open the chat link without typing a number. Print it with enough white space around the edges and at a size that remains easy to scan. Test the printed version under the real lighting and distance where it will be used. Keep the caption clear, such as “Scan to chat on WhatsApp,” and include another contact method when the audience may not have WhatsApp or mobile data.

A QR code is not a security guarantee. Anyone who can see or scan it can access the link. Treat a public WhatsApp link like a public phone number. If the destination number changes, create and distribute a new QR code. The QR image generated here is downloadable and created in the browser using the link you entered.

Using links in business and campaigns

Add a direct link to a website contact button, social profile, email signature, digital menu, appointment page, or customer-support message. Use a greeting that identifies the request, such as “Hello, I found you through the event flyer and would like to reserve a place.” Keep separate links for different campaigns when you want to understand which printed or digital channel people used. Always test each link from a device that has WhatsApp installed and confirm that the number opens correctly before publishing.

For a public business number, decide who will reply, when replies are monitored, and what information customers should not send. A direct link makes contact easier, but it does not replace a clear privacy notice or a safe process for payment and identity information. If a campaign is no longer active, remove or replace the link so visitors do not receive an outdated response.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the leading zero?

Usually no. Choose the country code and enter the rest of the local number. Confirm the result against the number’s international format.

Does this send a WhatsApp message automatically?

No. It opens a click-to-chat link and places an optional greeting in the message box. The user must review and send the message.

Can I use the QR code on a flyer?

Yes. Download and test it at the intended print size. Keep clear space around the code so scanning apps can detect its edges.
