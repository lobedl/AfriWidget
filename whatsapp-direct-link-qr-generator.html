Frequently asked questions

What is a WhatsApp click-to-chat link?

A WhatsApp click-to-chat link is a normal web link in the wa.me format that opens a WhatsApp chat with a specific phone number when a visitor taps or clicks it. If the link includes a pre-filled greeting, that greeting appears in the message box, and the visitor can edit or delete it before sending. The link works on desktop and on mobile, and the visitor does not need to save the phone number to their contacts first. It is the standard way to place a WhatsApp contact button on a website, a business card, a menu, a flyer, or an email signature.

Should I include the leading zero of the phone number?

No. In international format the leading zero, which is a national trunk prefix, is dropped and replaced by the country code. For example a Ghanaian local number written as 024 123 4567 becomes 233 24 123 4567 in international format. Enter the country code in the dropdown and the number without the leading zero in the phone field. The generator removes spaces, brackets, and non-numeric characters, but you should still confirm the final link by opening it on a phone before you publish it.

Does the link send a message automatically?

No. The link opens a WhatsApp chat with the number you entered and, if you added one, places the greeting in the message box. The visitor must press send themselves. This is by design, and it is the reason the click-to-chat format is safe to publish publicly: no message is transmitted without a person on the other end deciding to send it. The link cannot read the visitor's contacts, cannot send anything on its own, and cannot access the message history of the visitor or of the destination number.

Can I download the QR code and use it in print?

Yes. Press Download QR Code to save the QR image as a PNG file, then place it in your poster, menu, business card, storefront sign, event flyer, or packaging. Keep a quiet zone of at least four modules of white space around the code, print it at a size that remains easy to scan from the intended distance, and test the printed version with a real phone camera before you send the design to print. A caption such as Scan to chat on WhatsApp helps visitors understand what to expect.

Is the QR code image generated on a server?

No. The QR code is drawn on a canvas element inside your browser using a small QR encoder that runs locally. No image file is uploaded to any server, no QR code is fetched from a third-party API, and nothing about your phone number or greeting is transmitted anywhere. The generated PNG is created by the browser's canvas element and is saved directly to your device. If you press the AI insight button, only anonymous counts and settings are sent, never the phone number itself.

Can I use this for a business WhatsApp account?

Yes, as long as the number is registered on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business and your use complies with WhatsApp's business messaging policies. The wa.me format is the same for personal and business numbers. If you run a WhatsApp Business API account with multiple agents, confirm with your provider that the public-facing number you are publishing is the one that routes correctly to your inbox. This tool builds the link and the QR code; the messaging policy and the destination routing belong to you and your business setup.

Why does my link not open WhatsApp on a desktop computer?

On a desktop, WhatsApp links normally open WhatsApp Web in a browser tab, which requires the visitor to be signed in to WhatsApp on that browser at least once. If the visitor has never signed in, the page will show a QR code that they scan with their phone to connect. On a phone with WhatsApp installed, the link opens the app directly. If the link appears to do nothing at all, check that the phone number is correct, that WhatsApp is installed, and that the browser is not blocking the redirect.

How do I change or update the phone number later?

Because nothing is stored anywhere, you simply return to this page, change the country code or the phone number field, and press Generate Link again to produce a new wa.me URL and a new QR image. Then replace the old link and old QR code wherever they were published. If the number is printed on physical materials, plan for the change by keeping the design editable, so that a single reprinted sign or sticker can carry the new code without a full reprint of the campaign.

Is my phone number sent to a server when I press Generate?

No. The link is built in the page from the fields you filled in, the QR code is drawn locally on a canvas, and nothing is transmitted to any server. The only exception is if you press the AI insight button, in which case only an anonymous summary of the country code length, whether a greeting was added, and the total character count is sent to a small serverless function that runs once and then stops. The phone number itself and the message text are never included in that request.
