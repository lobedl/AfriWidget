Official Guide to UNEB Grading Systems: PLE, UCE & UACE

The Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) is the statutory body established under the UNEB Act to conduct national assessments and award primary and secondary school qualifications across Uganda. Understanding how scores translate into aggregates, grades, and statutory divisions is essential for learners preparing for Primary Leaving Examinations (PLE), Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE), and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE).

PLE (Primary 7)
4 to 36 Aggregates

4 Core subjects. Lower aggregate is better. Div 1 = 4–12.

UCE (Senior 4)
8 to 72 Aggregates

Best 8 subjects. Div 1 = 8–32 with English, Math & Science.

UACE (Senior 6)
0 to 20 Points Scale

3 Principals (A=6) + Sub ICT/Math (1) + General Paper (1).

1. Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) Grading & Senior 1 Selection

At Primary 7 level, candidates sit four compulsory national examination papers:

  • English Language: Graded D1 to F9 (1 to 9 points)
  • Mathematics: Graded D1 to F9 (1 to 9 points)
  • Integrated Science: Graded D1 to F9 (1 to 9 points)
  • Social Studies and Religious Education (SST): Graded D1 to F9 (1 to 9 points)

Your Total Aggregate is the sum of the four subject grades. Division thresholds are defined by UNEB as:

Division 1 (First Grade) • Aggregates 4 to 12
Qualifies for National Elite Boarding Secondary Schools
Division 2 (Second Grade) • Aggregates 13 to 24
Qualifies for Standard Secondary Schools & Day Schools
Division 3 (Third Grade) • Aggregates 25 to 28
Qualifies for Community Secondary Schools & Vocational Training
Division 4 (Fourth Grade) • Aggregates 29 to 32
Pass Grade • Eligible for Vocational Skills Institutes (BTVET)
Division U (Ungraded / Fail) • Aggregates 33 to 36
Must repeat P7 or enter non-formal foundation vocational training

2. Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) Best 8 Calculation

In Senior 4, students sit between 8 and 10 subjects. UNEB computes your candidate performance using your Best 8 Subjects, which must satisfy strict subject grouping criteria:

  • Compulsory Subjects: English Language and Mathematics must be included in the candidate's aggregate computation.
  • Science Requirement: At least two science subjects (e.g. Physics, Chemistry, Biology) must be included.
  • Humanities Requirement: At least one humanities subject (e.g. Geography, History, CRE, IRE) must be included.
  • Best Electives: The remaining spots are filled by the candidate's best scored remaining subjects (Agriculture, Commerce, Entrepreneurship, Literature, French, Luganda, Art, Music).

UCE Division Criteria:

  • Division 1: Aggregates 8 to 32, with a minimum of 7 credits (C6 or better) and passes in English and Math.
  • Division 2: Aggregates 33 to 45, with a minimum of 6 credits.
  • Division 3: Aggregates 46 to 58, with at least 3 credits or 8 passes.
  • Division 4: Aggregates 59 to 72, with at least 1 credit or 2 passes.
  • Division 9 (Fail): Fails to achieve minimum pass standard.

3. Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) 20-Point System

In Senior 6, academic performance is evaluated across a 3-Principal combination plus 2 subsidiary subjects:

Principal Grade A (70% – 100%)
6 Points (Principal Pass)
Principal Grade B (65% – 69%)
5 Points (Principal Pass)
Principal Grade C (60% – 64%)
4 Points (Principal Pass)
Principal Grade D (55% – 59%)
3 Points (Principal Pass)
Principal Grade E (50% – 54%)
2 Points (Principal Pass)
Subsidiary Grade O (40% – 49%)
1 Point (Subsidiary Pass)
Subsidiary Mathematics / ICT & General Paper
1 Point Each (2 Points Total Maximum)

• Social Studies & RE: Distinction 2 (2 pts)

• Principal 1: Physics — Grade A = 6 Points

• Principal 2: Chemistry — Grade B = 5 Points

• Principal 3: Mathematics — Grade A = 6 Points

• Subsidiary: General Paper (GP) — Pass = 1 Point

Total UACE Points = 6 + 5 + 6 + 1 + 1 = 19 Points (out of 20)

Direct Eligibility: Top competitive public university programs (e.g. Electrical / Civil Engineering at Makerere University).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if a candidate fails General Paper or Subsidiary Math in UACE? ▼
If a candidate scores an F9 in General Paper or Subsidiary Math/ICT, they earn 0 points for that paper. However, as long as the student scores at least 2 Principal Passes (Grade E or better) in their main subjects, they still receive an official UACE certificate and remain eligible for direct university admission, albeit with reduced aggregate points.
Can a student with Division 2 in UCE take Science combinations in A-Level? ▼
Yes, provided the student achieved strong Credit grades (Credit 3 or Credit 4 or better) in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Most secondary schools set specific subject-level entry thresholds for science combinations (such as PCM, PCB, BCM) regardless of whether the overall division was Division 1 or Division 2.
What is the minimum requirement for direct university admission in Uganda? ▼
The National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) and UNEB require a candidate to obtain at least Two Principal Passes (Grades A to E) in recognized subjects at the same sitting of the UACE examination, along with at least 5 passes in UCE O-Level.
How are UNEB results verified online? ▼
UNEB results can be checked via the official SMS shortcode service (sending [Exam Type] [Index Number] to 6600) or through the school examination center headteacher portal on the official UNEB e-services portal (uneb.ac.ug).
