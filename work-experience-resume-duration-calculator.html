What this builder does

This tool turns a set of details about your career into a clean, single-column resume or CV, and it calculates the total duration of your work experience automatically as you fill in the form. You add your personal details, your work history, your education, and your skills, and the preview on the page updates. When you press Download PDF, the browser's print engine opens with the resume ready to save, and the print stylesheet hides everything else on the page so the file matches the preview.

Everything runs inside the browser. The resume is assembled in the page from the fields you fill in, and the PDF is produced by the browser's own print engine. Nothing is transmitted to a server, and no library is loaded to make the PDF. If the AI insight button is pressed, only an anonymous summary of the structure is sent, meaning the region preset, the number of roles, the number of education entries, the number of skills, and the total months of experience. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, companies, job titles, and descriptions never leave your device.

The builder is a starting point for a clear, professional-looking document. It is not a substitute for careful editing, honest description, and a final read-through in your own voice. The tool gives you a well-structured template and a reliable duration calculation; the content and the judgement belong to you.

How total work experience is calculated

Each role in the work experience section has a start month and an end month. If the role is ongoing, the checkbox marks it as current and the calculator uses the present month as the end. The tool subtracts the start month from the end month in calendar months, adds the result to a running total, and then divides the total by twelve to give years and months.

The arithmetic is simple on purpose, because the honest answer to a work experience question is usually simple too. Two things are worth being clear about. First, the tool adds each role separately, so if two roles overlapped in time, the same calendar month is counted twice. That is appropriate when you are describing the total experience you bring, and it is not appropriate when an employer is asking for elapsed calendar time. Second, part-time and full-time roles are counted the same way. If an employer asks for full-time equivalent years, adjust the total by hand before you quote it.

A useful convention. Most recruiters will accept a duration rounded to the nearest month. If you worked from 15 January 2020 to 14 January 2023, the honest description is three years, not two years eleven months. Rounding to the nearest month is standard practice and is not misleading.

Building a Ghanaian CV

Ghanaian CVs typically open with a short professional summary, list work experience in reverse chronological order, and include education with WASSCE and any tertiary qualifications named precisely, including the institution and the degree. The Ghana preset in this tool sets a Ghanaian phone format and a Ghana-appropriate location placeholder, and the resume preview uses a clean single-column layout that works equally well with local recruiters and international employers who may be reading the same document.

Two conventions are worth mentioning. First, the CV is often headed with the candidate's full name and the professional title, followed by contact details on a single line, and that is what the preview produces. Second, education entries should name the institution accurately, whether it is the University of Ghana, KNUST, the University of Cape Coast, UPSA, or another recognised institution. If the employer specifically asks for WASSCE grades, add them to the education entry in your own words after downloading the PDF, or include them in the education field on the form.

If you are applying to a multinational with offices in Accra, the Worldwide preset may be a better starting point, because it drops Ghana-specific placeholders and produces a neutral header that reads naturally in any market. Either preset produces the same clean single-column layout; the difference is only in the placeholder text and the phone format hint.

Building a Nigerian CV

Nigerian CVs commonly include a passport photograph in the header, a clear NYSC status line where relevant, and a reverse chronological work history. The Nigeria preset in this tool sets a Nigerian phone format and a Nigerian location placeholder, and the resume preview holds the same set of sections that Nigerian recruiters expect. If you want a photograph, add it after downloading the PDF, because keeping the preview plain makes it easier to reuse the same document across countries where a photograph is not appropriate.

The NYSC status line is a good example of a small detail that matters. If you have completed the one-year National Youth Service Corps programme, mention the state of service and the year. If you have an exemption, mention the exemption. If you have not yet served, mention that as well, because many employers will ask. The right place for this in the resume is either the personal details line or a one-line education note, depending on the format the employer has asked for.

The professional summary at the top of the resume carries more weight in a Nigerian CV than in some other markets. A good summary names the discipline, the years of experience, and one or two areas of strength, in two or three lines. The summary field in this tool is designed for exactly that length. Resist the temptation to write a paragraph; a tight three-line summary almost always outperforms a longer one.

Building a South African CV

South African CVs usually lead with Matric results or the equivalent, then tertiary qualifications, then work experience, and are kept to two pages for experienced hires. The South Africa preset in this tool sets a South African phone format and a South African location placeholder, and the format matches what local recruiters expect. If you are applying abroad with a South African qualification, mention the SAQA evaluation where the employer asks for it, and confirm the exact wording with the employer before you include it.

Matric details can be added in your own words after downloading the PDF, in the education section. A short line such as Matric, 2015, with a description of the subjects that matter for the role is enough for most applications. If the role is a graduate position, the Matric line may need to be more prominent; if the role is a senior one, the Matric line can be a short entry below the tertiary qualifications.

The South African market is one of the few where a two-page resume is often expected rather than merely tolerated. Keep the content tight, put the strongest material first, and use the duration calculations from this tool to describe periods accurately. If you have worked as a contractor or freelancer, group related short engagements under a single contractor heading rather than listing every project separately, unless the projects themselves are the story.

Building an international resume

For applications to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and most of Europe, the convention is a one-page or two-page resume, reverse chronological, without a photograph, without personal details such as date of birth or marital status, and without a listing of every past role. The Worldwide preset in this tool produces a clean resume header that matches that convention.

Two adaptations matter most. First, the phone number should be written with the country code, in a format that is readable to the target market, and the Worldwide preset uses an international placeholder that shows the shape of a country-code number. Second, the summary should be written in the vocabulary of the target market. Terms such as CV, resume, portfolio, and personal statement are not always interchangeable between countries; use the term the employer uses in the job advertisement, and adjust the rest of the document to match.

If you are applying to more than one country, generate one PDF per country and adjust the header, the summary, and the phone number accordingly. The tool makes that easy because everything is local: press Download PDF for the first version, change a few fields, press Download PDF again. There is no account, no history, and no version confusion to manage, because nothing is stored on any server.

How the PDF download works

When you press Download PDF, the browser opens its native print dialog with the resume already isolated for printing. Choose Save as PDF from the print destination menu, set the paper size to A4 if it is not already selected, and confirm. The file that the browser produces is the resume you saw in the preview, without the surrounding interface, without the buttons, and without the form fields.

The print stylesheet hides the navigation, the calculator form, the article, the FAQ, the related tools section, and the footer. It sets the page size to A4 with a sixteen millimetre top and bottom margin and an eighteen millimetre left and right margin, which is a standard professional margin for a printed document. Inside the printed area, the resume uses the same fonts and the same spacing as the on-screen preview, so the two match.

Because the file is generated by the browser and not by a server, the tool does not need a PDF library, and the download works offline once the page has loaded. If the print dialog does not open, check that the browser is not blocking pop-ups for this page, and try again. If the Save as PDF option is not visible, open the print destination menu and select it from the list.

Common mistakes

Filling in the form with more detail than the format can carry. The preview is designed for a two-page resume at most. If you paste three paragraphs of description for every role, the printed document will overflow and look crowded. Aim for three to five bullets per role, each one line or two, and put the rest in a separate cover letter.

Using the same summary for every application. The summary field is the first thing a recruiter reads, and it should be tailored to the role. Adjust the discipline, the seniority, and the focus of the summary to match the job advertisement, even if the rest of the resume stays the same.

Overlooking the region preset. The preset changes the phone placeholder, the location placeholder, and the regional note in the results. It does not change the content of the resume, because the content belongs to you. Pick the preset that matches the country you are applying to, not the country you are currently in.

Forgetting to check the total experience duration against reality. The tool adds each role as entered and does not detect overlapping periods. If you are quoting total experience in an application, compare the total shown here against the actual calendar time you have been working, and adjust the wording if the two are different.

Downloading the PDF before editing the preview. The preview updates as you type, so there is no need to press a separate Update button. Confirm that the preview shows the content you want, check that the total experience is correct, and then download.

Leaving the summary blank. A resume without a professional summary looks incomplete to most recruiters, even when the work history below is strong. A two-line summary is better than no summary. The AI insight button can help if you are stuck, but the summary should ultimately be in your own words.

Assuming the downloaded PDF is editable. The PDF that the browser produces is a fixed document. If you need to update it, return to this page, change the relevant field, and download a new copy. Keep a plain-text version of your resume content in your own notes if you want a durable record.

Where this tool stops

This builder produces a well-structured resume from the fields you fill in, and a PDF that matches the preview. It does not write the content for you, does not verify the accuracy of dates or company names, does not check whether the resume matches the specific job advertisement, and does not know the hiring conventions of every country or industry. Those judgements belong to the person writing the resume and to the recruiting standards of the market they are applying to.

It also does not provide legal or professional advice on employment, and it does not store your data. The tool is a formatting and calculation aid, not an HR system. If you are working with a career adviser, a recruiter, or a professional editor, use the document produced here as a clean starting point for that conversation rather than as a final product.

What the tool does give you is a fast, transparent, private way to build a professional-looking resume, see the total duration of your work experience calculated accurately, preview the document exactly as it will print, and download the PDF in a single click. Used together with careful editing and honest description, it is a practical starting point for any job application.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate total work experience for a CV?

List each role with its start month and end month, then add the months together. If a role is ongoing, use the current month as the end. Divide the total months by twelve to get years, and keep the remainder as months. This tool does that arithmetic for you and shows the total in years and months as you fill in the form. If two roles overlap, the same calendar month is counted twice, which is fine for a combined-experience summary but not for elapsed calendar time. Review any overlap before you submit an official application.

Should I include internships and contract work on my CV?

Include any role where you delivered real work, whether it was paid or unpaid, full-time or part-time, as long as you can describe what you did and what you achieved. Internships, contract engagements, freelance projects, teaching assistantships, and volunteer positions all count when the responsibilities are relevant to the role you are applying for. Label each entry honestly with its nature, such as Intern, Contractor, or Freelance, so the reader understands the context without having to guess.

The print stylesheet hides everything except the resume preview, sets the page size to A4 with fifteen to twenty millimetre margins, and prints the resume exactly as it appears on screen. No server is involved in the file creation, and no library is loaded. The resulting PDF is yours to save, email, or upload to a job portal.

Will the downloaded PDF look exactly like the preview?

Yes, by design. The preview on the page and the printed PDF are the same piece of HTML styled by the same stylesheet, and the print rules only strip away the surrounding interface, the shadows, and the interactive elements. The typography, spacing, headings, dates, and bullet lists are identical between the two. Minor differences in how a browser's print engine renders a page can occur, but they are cosmetic and do not change the layout or content.
