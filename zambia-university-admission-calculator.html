Frequently asked questions

How does the Best 5 admission score work in Zambia?

Zambian universities select candidates using the Best 5 relevant subjects from the ECZ School Certificate. The five usually include English, Mathematics, and the specific prerequisite subjects the target faculty requires. Each grade carries a fixed point value from one for a distinction to nine for a fail, and because lower numbers mean stronger grades, the lowest total wins the available place. The Best 5 aggregate is what actually competes for a quota slot, not the number of credits you hold.

Can I combine two ECZ sittings for the five-credit requirement?

Yes. Most Zambian universities accept subject passes drawn from up to two examination sittings, including combinations of the school certificate and the GCE. The individual faculty decides whether the five credits have to include the key prerequisite subjects from a single sitting, so check the current prospectus for the programme you are targeting before you plan around a combined record.

Can I apply with a Science 5124 combined paper instead of separate Physics and Chemistry?

Yes. UNZA, CBU, and Mulungushi all accept Science 5124, the combined physical science paper, as satisfying the physical science requirement for science and pre-engineering programmes, provided the grade is a credit or better. Some of the more competitive engineering and mining programmes have started asking for the separate Physics and Chemistry papers, so check the current faculty requirement before you assume the combined paper will be accepted.

What is the difference between government quota and self-sponsored admission?

The government quota is funded through the Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Board and has the tightest cut-off lines because demand far outstrips the number of funded places. Self-sponsored admission, sometimes called the parallel or evening stream, uses a wider cut-off band because the student pays the full tuition. Both routes lead to the same degree certificate, and the same programme, at the same university.

Can I enter university with a diploma rather than Grade 12 alone?

Yes. A 2-year or 3-year diploma from an accredited college such as Evelyn Hone, NORTEC, or NRDC, in a related field, can be used to apply for degree entry. Some universities admit diploma holders into the second year of a related bachelor's programme, and others admit them into the first year with an exempted foundation. The diploma route is often faster than repeating Grade 12 for a missing credit.

Do ECZ results expire?

No. The ECZ School Certificate and the GCE do not carry an expiry date. Applicants who completed Grade 12 several years ago apply under the non-school-leaver category and are assessed on the same five-credit baseline alongside any diplomas or professional certificates they have obtained since. There is no penalty for applying late, and the process is the same as for a fresh school leaver.

What should I do if I am one credit short of the baseline?

The fastest route back into eligibility is the mid-year GCE sitting for the single missing subject. A pass upgrade from Grade 7 to Grade 6 converts a pass into a credit, and the combined record usually satisfies the statutory five-credit requirement. The alternative is a TEVETA Level 4 or 5 certificate in a related field, which serves as an accredited bridge to diploma and then degree study.

Does the calculator know the exact cut-off for my programme this year?

No. The cut-offs shown are planning references drawn from published prospectuses and past cycles. The internal cut-off for a specific programme is set after the applicant pool is known, and it moves from year to year. For the exact figure, check the directorate of academic affairs page at the university you are applying to, or the current HEA guidance.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser and nothing you type leaves your device. If you choose to press the optional AI button, only an anonymous summary of your computed Best 5 aggregate, programme, and eligibility status is sent to generate a short written response. Nothing is stored.
