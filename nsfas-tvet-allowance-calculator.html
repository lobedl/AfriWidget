Understanding NSFAS TVET Allowances

The National Student Financial Aid Scheme (NSFAS) covers 100% of tuition and college fees directly with the institution. In addition, qualifying students receive specific living, housing, and transport allowances depending on their enrollment profile and residential distance from campus.

Accommodation vs. Travel Allowances

Under DHET guidelines, students cannot claim both private accommodation and travel allowances at the same time. Students living at home receive a travel allowance (approx. R7,718 to R10,650/year), while students in accredited accommodation have their housing covered up to the annual DHET ceiling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do TVET college students get the same NSFAS allowance as university students?

Tuition is fully funded for both. However, personal care and living allowances differ slightly because TVET colleges frequently provide subsidized learning materials, toolkits, and meals on campus, while university living allowances are calibrated for off-campus grocery costs.

Can I receive both accommodation and travel allowance at the same time?

No. Under DHET rules, students residing in accredited accommodation have their housing covered and are expected to walk or use campus shuttle transport. Students living at home receive the travel allowance instead.
